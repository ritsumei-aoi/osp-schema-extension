TYPE: CORRECTION

Please address the following discrepancies and issues found in the repository:

1. Missing scripts cited in the report:
In `docs/issues/I08.md` (lines 102–103), `src/_triviality_conditions_v3.py` and `src/_individual_gb_test.py` are cited as computational evidence for the triviality conclusions, and `docs/issues/final_reflection.md` (line 57) mentions `_triviality_conditions_v2.py` and `_triviality_conditions_v3.py`. However, these scripts are not present in the repository (`src/`). Either commit all scripts necessary to reproduce and verify your findings, or update the report so that it strictly relies on code committed in the repository.

2. Unsubstantiated claims regarding B(0,n):
In `docs/issues/I08.md` (lines 92–93), the report asserts: "In the B(0,n) case, the deformation involves a supplementary fermion a_0 with a_0^2 = 1/2 (a single parameter)... while in B(0,n) only specific combinations are constrained." However, `docs/math/B0n_definition.md` defines 2n deformation parameters gb_{a_0, b_j^\pm}, and no document in the repository supports the statement that only specific combinations are constrained in B(0,n). Correct or remove these claims so that any comparative statements strictly reflect the documents in `docs/math/` or sources present in the repository.

3. Discrepancy in the obstruction explanation and gamma data:
In `docs/issues/I08.md` (lines 61–68 and 91–92), the report explains that the non-triviality obstruction arises from the constant scalar -1/2 component in H_2 = -b_1^+ b_1^- - 1/2 producing a kappa term during the calculation of [E_{eps-del1_pm}, H_1]. However:
- In `data/C_1_gamma.json`, gamma(E_{eps-del1_pm}, H_1) contains an H_1 component (-1) as well as an H_2 component (-2) under gb_a1p_b1m, along with an E_{2del1_m} component under gb_a1p_b1p.
- H_1 = a_1^+ a_1^- + b_1^+ b_1^- has no constant scalar term. H_2 is not an input generator to the bracket [E_{eps-del1_pm}, H_1], so a scalar term in H_2 cannot enter during the evaluation of this bracket.
- The other witness triples listed in lines 74–77 (e.g. (E_eps_del1_mm, E_2del1_m, E_2del1_m)) target generators that contain no scalar part.
Re-examine the mathematical mechanism of the obstruction and ensure that the analytical explanation in `docs/issues/I08.md` accurately describes why the linear system gamma = delta f is inconsistent, matching the actual data in `data/C_{1,2,3}_gamma.json` and `data/C_{1,2,3}_coboundary.json`.

4. Cross-check of gamma values against mathematical definitions:
The gamma values generated by `src/C_gamma.py` and stored in `data/C_{1,2,3}_gamma.json` have not been systematically verified against independent manual calculations or the formal definitions in `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` (only a single generator pair was checked in `docs/issues/I05.md`). In addition, inspect `_normalize_deformed` in `src/C_gamma.py` to ensure that remaining words after extracting kappa contributions are properly normal-ordered. Provide systematic cross-checks or test scripts verifying the gamma coefficients against analytical derivations for representative pairs across different parities and roots, and record these checks in your report.

5. Cohomology group designation in final reflection:
In `docs/issues/final_reflection.md` (line 67), the report states: "This could be formalized as a cohomology statement: H¹(C(n+1); C(n+1)) has dimension 4n (one class per deformation parameter)." In Lie superalgebra deformation theory and according to `docs/math/C_coboundary_definition.md`, the deformation gamma is a 2-cocycle and trivial deformations are coboundaries delta f of 1-cochains f, meaning equivalence classes of deformations belong to the second cohomology group H^2(g; g), not H^1(g; g). Correct this statement to align with the mathematical framework in `docs/math/`.

Please update the reports (`docs/issues/I08.md`, `docs/issues/final_reflection.md`), code, and data so that they are mutually consistent and adhere strictly to the reference materials, and commit all changes.
```
