# Notation and Terminology

This document defines the mathematical notation and terminology used in this repository.
It is initialized during the $C(n+1)$ schema extension project.

## Conventions

### Oscillators (canonical index order)

| Index | Label (code) | Symbol | Parity | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `a1p` | $a_1^+$ | 1 | $\{a_1^-, a_1^+\} = 1$ |
| 1 | `a1m` | $a_1^-$ | 1 | $(a_1^\pm)^2 = 0$ |
| $2k$ | `bkp` | $b_k^+$ | 0 | $[b_k^-, b_k^+] = 1$ |
| $2k+1$ | `bkm` | $b_k^-$ | 0 | $[b_j^\pm, b_k^\pm] = 0$, $[b_j^-, b_k^+] = \delta_{jk}$ |

The JSON labels are `a_1_p`, `a_1_m` (fermions) and `b_k_p`, `b_k_m` (bosons). Fermions and bosons commute, $[b_k^s, a_1^\sigma] = 0$, in the undeformed algebra.

### Realization of the basis (decision for I01)

The basis is **Weyl-symmetrized quadratic oscillator elements**:

$$W(x,y) = \tfrac12\bigl(xy + (-1)^{p(x)p(y)}\, yx\bigr), \qquad x \le y \text{ in index order}, \quad W(x,y) \ne 0.$$

- Options considered:
  - (A) Frappat-style generators with explicit constants, e.g. $H_1 = a_1^+a_1^- + b_1^+b_1^-$, $H_{n+1} = -b_n^+b_n^- - \tfrac12$. Reason against: the constants are ordering-dependent and break the clean closure check.
  - (B) Weyl-symmetric quadratics (chosen). Reason: the quadratic span is closed under the supercommutator with no constant term, and it matches the dimension count exactly: $\#\{W\} = (2n+2)(2n+3)/2 - 2 = 2n^2+5n+1$.
  - (C) Matrix realization as supermatrices of $\mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)$. Reason against: needs an extra identification map to the oscillator labels.
- Consequence: $H$-type Cartan elements are $W(a_1^+,a_1^-)$ and $W(b_k^+,b_k^-)$, which equal the Frappat forms up to the constant $-\tfrac12$ per fermion/boson pair.

Basis labels: `W(x,y)` with $x \le y$, e.g. `W(a1p,b1m)`, `W(b2p,b2p)`.

### PBW ordering (decision for I01)

Options considered:
1. Odd first, then even, each ordered by $(i,j)$ index pair (chosen).
2. Even first, then odd. Reason against: the odd generators are the only ones that carry $\varepsilon$ roots, so putting them first matches the PBW convention in `B_n_schema_v5.md` ($\kappa < [\text{odd}] < [\text{even}]$).
3. Ordering by root height. Reason against: $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$ roots are not comparable in one height function without fixing a positive system, which is exactly the ambiguity to avoid.

Chosen ordering: $\kappa < [\text{odd}] < [\text{even}]$, with $K$ identified with $1$ and excluded from the basis.

Resulting lists:

- $C(2)$, $n=1$: even (4) `W(a1p,a1m)`, `W(b1p,b1p)`, `W(b1p,b1m)`, `W(b1m,b1m)`; odd (4) `W(a1p,b1p)`, `W(a1p,b1m)`, `W(a1m,b1p)`, `W(a1m,b1m)`.
- $C(3)$, $n=2$: even (11), odd (8). Full lists are generated by `src/C_structure.py` into `data/C_2_structure.json`.
- $C(4)$, $n=3$: even (22), odd (12).

### Root correspondence (for reference)

| Oscillator pair | Root |
|---|---|
| $a_1^\pm b_k^s$ | $\pm\varepsilon \pm \delta_k$ (odd) |
| $a_1^+a_1^-$ | $0$ (Cartan of $\mathfrak{so}(2)$) |
| $b_k^s b_l^t$, $k \ne l$ or $s = t$ | $\pm\delta_k \pm \delta_l$, $\pm 2\delta_k$ |
| $b_k^+b_k^-$ | $0$ (Cartan of $\mathfrak{sp}(2n)$) |

### Parity and the deformation parameters

- $p(\kappa) = 1$, $\kappa^2 = 0$, $\kappa$ central.
- $gb$ parameters: $\mathrm{gb}[\sigma, j, s]$ with $\sigma, s \in \{+,-\}$, $j = 1,\dots,n$; $4n$ values, parity 1.
- Deformed relation: $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}[\sigma,j,s]\,\kappa$.
- Coefficients of the structure constants are dual numbers $c_0 + c_\kappa \kappa$.

### Generator and coefficient labels

- Structure constants: $[X,Y] = \sum_Z c^Z_{XY}\, Z + \kappa\, \sum_Z c^{\kappa,Z}_{XY}\, Z$, with $X,Y,Z$ in the basis above.
- $\gamma(X,Y)$ is the $\kappa$-part, matching `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`.

## Deformation conventions (decisions for I05)

- **Basis under deformation.** The basis symbols $W(x,y)$ are the gb-free Weyl-symmetric words. Only products and brackets are computed with the deformed relations $[b_j^s,a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}[\sigma,j,s]\,\kappa$. Options considered: (a) deform the basis symbols too, so $W(a_1^\pm,b)$ acquires a $\kappa$-constant term that must be projected out. Rejected: it breaks the expansion onto the fixed basis. (b) gb-free symbols with deformed products (chosen). Consequence: $\gamma$ is the $\kappa$-coefficient of $[X,Y]$ in the basis.
- **Sign of $\kappa$.** $\kappa$ is odd and graded-central: $\kappa x = (-1)^{p(x)}x\kappa$. When a contraction is moved to the front past an odd prefix, its $\kappa$-part takes the sign $(-1)^{\#\text{odd prefix}}$. Omitting this sign made the $\kappa$-linear Jacobi fail in the first run (fixed in `src/C_generators.py`, `OscAlgebra.normal`).
- **Linearity.** Since $\kappa^2 = 0$, $\gamma$ is linear in gb. It is computed exactly from the $4n$ unit vectors of gb.
- **Cocycle condition.** $\gamma$ is a 2-cocycle iff the $\kappa$-linear part of super Jacobi vanishes. Checked in `src/C_gamma.py` (`kappa_jacobi_ok`).
