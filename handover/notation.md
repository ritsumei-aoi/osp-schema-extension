# Notation and Terminology

This document defines the mathematical notation and terminology used in this repository.
It is initialized during the $C(n+1)$ schema extension project and finalized in Issue I01.

## Conventions

### Algebra and sizes
- $C(n+1) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)$, $n$ = bosonic rank ($n=1,2,3$ give $C(2), C(3), C(4)$).
- $\dim \mathfrak{g}_{\bar 0} = 2n^2+n+1$, $\dim \mathfrak{g}_{\bar 1} = 4n$.
- Parity $p(\cdot)\in\{0,1\}$; $[X,Y\}$ is the graded bracket $XY-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}YX$.
- $\kappa$: odd central nilpotent element ($\kappa^2=0$, $\kappa$ supercommutes with everything, i.e. anticommutes with $a_1^\pm$ and commutes with $b_k^\pm$). $K$: even central element, identified with the scalar $1$ and **not** a basis element.

### Oscillator generator labels
| Math | Label | Parity | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| $a_1^+, a_1^-$ | `a_1_p`, `a_1_m` | 1 | $\{a_1^-,a_1^+\}=1$, $(a_1^\pm)^2=0$ |
| $b_k^+, b_k^-$ ($1\le k\le n$) | `b_{k}_p`, `b_{k}_m` | 0 | $[b_k^-,b_l^+]=\delta_{kl}$ |
| $\kappa$ | `kappa` | 1 | central, $\kappa^2=0$ |

Oscillator word ordering (normal ordering): `kappa < a_1_p < a_1_m < b_1_p < b_1_m < ... < b_n_p < b_n_m`.

### Basis labels
Signs: `p` = $+$, `m` = $-$.

**Even basis** ($2n^2+n+1$ elements)

| Root | Label | Realization |
|---|---|---|
| Cartan, $k=1..n+1$ | `H_{k}` | $H_1=a_1^+a_1^-+b_1^+b_1^-$, $H_k=b_{k-1}^+b_{k-1}^--b_k^+b_k^-$, $H_{n+1}=-b_n^+b_n^--\tfrac12$ (Weyl-symmetrised, see below) |
| $2\delta_k$ | `E_2del{k}_p` | $(b_k^+)^2$ |
| $-2\delta_k$ | `E_2del{k}_m` | $(b_k^-)^2$ |
| $\delta_i+\delta_j$, $i<j$ | `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp` | $b_i^+b_j^+$ |
| $\delta_i-\delta_j$, $i<j$ | `E_del{i}_del{j}_pm` | $b_i^+b_j^-$ |
| $-\delta_i+\delta_j$, $i<j$ | `E_del{i}_del{j}_mp` | $b_i^-b_j^+$ |
| $-\delta_i-\delta_j$, $i<j$ | `E_del{i}_del{j}_mm` | $b_i^-b_j^-$ |

**Odd basis** ($4n$ elements): first sign is that of $\varepsilon$, second that of $\delta_k$.

| Root | Label | Realization |
|---|---|---|
| $\varepsilon+\delta_k$ | `E_eps1_del{k}_pp` | $a_1^+b_k^+$ |
| $\varepsilon-\delta_k$ | `E_eps1_del{k}_pm` | $a_1^+b_k^-$ |
| $-\varepsilon+\delta_k$ | `E_eps1_del{k}_mp` | $a_1^-b_k^+$ |
| $-\varepsilon-\delta_k$ | `E_eps1_del{k}_mm` | $a_1^-b_k^-$ |

Explicit lists (generated by `src/C_generators.py`, identical to the below):

- $C(2)$, $n=1$ (4 | 4): even `H_1 H_2 E_2del1_p E_2del1_m`; odd `E_eps1_del1_pp E_eps1_del1_pm E_eps1_del1_mp E_eps1_del1_mm`.
- $C(3)$, $n=2$ (11 | 8): even `H_1 H_2 H_3 E_2del1_p E_2del2_p E_del1_del2_pp E_2del1_m E_2del2_m E_del1_del2_mm E_del1_del2_pm E_del1_del2_mp`; odd `E_eps1_del{1,2}_{pp,pm,mp,mm}` (k fastest).
- $C(4)$, $n=3$ (22 | 12): even `H_1..H_4`, `E_2del{1,2,3}_p`, `E_del{12,13,23}_pp`, `E_2del{1,2,3}_m`, `E_del{12,13,23}_mm`, `E_del{12,13,23}_pm`, `E_del{12,13,23}_mp`; odd `E_eps1_del{1,2,3}_{pp,pm,mp,mm}`.

### Weyl-symmetrised Cartan
The Cartan generators of the doc are the Weyl-symmetric combinations
$h_a=a_1^+a_1^--\tfrac12$, $h_k=b_k^+b_k^-+\tfrac12$ via
$H_1=h_a+h_1$, $H_k=h_{k-1}-h_k$, $H_{n+1}=-h_n$. In normal-ordered words the
constants cancel except in $H_{n+1}$ ($-\tfrac12$) and $H_1$ ($0$); with this choice $\mathfrak g$ closes
under the bracket with **no** central constant. (Frappat's $H_{n+1}=-b_n^+b_n^--\tfrac12$ is the $k=n+1$ instance.)

### PBW ordering (decision of I01)
Options considered:
1. **(A) Odd-block first (B(0,n) v5.0 compatible):** $\kappa<$ [odd: ordered by $(\mathrm{sgn}\,\varepsilon,\mathrm{sgn}\,\delta,k)$] $<$ [even: `H`, `E_2del_p`, `E_del_pp`, `E_2del_m`, `E_del_mm`, `E_del_pm`, `E_del_mp`].
2. (B) Triangular/height order: $\kappa<$ negative roots $<$ Cartan $<$ positive roots, odd and even interleaved by height.
3. (C) $\varepsilon$-graded blocks: $\kappa<$ $\varepsilon$-charge $-1$ odd $<$ even (charge 0) $<$ $\varepsilon$-charge $+1$ odd.

**Chosen: (A).** Reasons: (i) it is the B(0,n) v5.0 convention ("κ < odd < even"), so Schema 1 stays backward compatible and the B(0,n) tooling carries over; (ii) all $\varepsilon$-roots are odd, so the odd block stays contiguous and the ε-sign sub-order $pp,pm,mp,mm$ is a plain refinement of B's "sign outer, $k$ inner" order; (iii) the order is independent of the choice of positive system, so it does not need re-deriving for each $n$.
Full order of $\mathfrak g$: odd `E_eps1_del{k}_{pp,pm,mp,mm}` (type outer, $k$ inner), then even
`H_1..H_{n+1}`, `E_2del{k}_p`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp`, `E_2del{k}_m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_mm`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pm`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_mp` ($i<j$ lexicographic).

### Deformation (gb) and layers
- `gb_{σ}_{j}_{s}` is written `gb_a_1_{σ}_b_{j}_{s}` with $\sigma,s\in\{p,m\}$; $4n$ parameters, matrix `gb_matrix` of shape $2\times 2n$ (rows `a_1_p`, `a_1_m`; columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, ..., b_n_p, b_n_m`). Relation $[b_j^s,a_1^\sigma]=-\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\,\kappa$. gb are numeric scalars.
- $[X,Y]_\gamma=[X,Y]_0+\kappa\,\gamma(X,Y)$ with $\kappa$ written to the **left**. $\gamma(X,Y)\in\mathfrak g\oplus\mathbb C K$; the `K` component of $\gamma$ is the coefficient of $\kappa\cdot 1$ (labelled `Z = "K"`).
- Layers: Schema 1 `C_{n}_structure.json`, Schema 2 `C_{n}_gamma.json`, Schema 3 `C_{n}_evaluated.json`, Schema 4 `C_{n}_coboundary.json`.
- Odd map $f:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak g\oplus\mathbb CK$, $f(Z_j)=\sum_i\phi_{ij}Z_i$, with $\phi_{ij}\neq0$ only if $p(Z_i)\neq p(Z_j)$ (for $K$: $p(K)=0$).
