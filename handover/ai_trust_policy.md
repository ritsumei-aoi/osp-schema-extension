# AI Trust Policy

## Forbidden Actions
- Do NOT use `git reset --hard` or `git push --force`.
- Do NOT modify or delete `.env`, `.git`, or system configuration files.
- Do NOT attempt to access external network resources unless explicitly instructed.

## Boundary
- If a task requires a decision not covered by the issues, or if you encounter a mathematical ambiguity, record the ambiguity, the assumption you adopt, and your reason in the Response section of the current issue, and continue.
