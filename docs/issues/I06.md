### Response

**Implementation: `src/C_evaluate.py`**

**gb Assignment chosen**: all gb = +1 (representative sign profile).
- This is the simplest non-trivial case; it avoids sign cancellations that could mask structure.
- An alternative (all gb = 0) gives the undeformed algebra; all gb = +1 gives maximal deformation.
- All 4n deformation parameters are activated simultaneously.
