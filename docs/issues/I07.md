#### Implementation

**Script**: `src/C_coboundary.py`

**Coboundary formula** (from `docs/math/C_coboundary_definition.md`):
  (δf)(X,Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X,Y])

**Parametrization of f**: f(Z_j) = sum_i phi_{i,j} Z_i where p(Z_i) ≠ p(Z_j).

The coboundary coefficients (δf)(X,Y)[Z] are expressed as linear combinations of phi_{i,j} parameters.
