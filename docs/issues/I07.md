### Response

**Decisions**

1. **Choice of f** (options: (a) generic f with unknown φ_ij, solved later – no concrete Layer 4 data; (b) a hand-guessed sparse f; (c) the f induced by the natural change of variables that removes the deformation at the oscillator level). Chose (c): shift the fermions `a_1^σ → a_1^σ + κ Σ_{j,s} c^σ_{j,s} b_j^s`. Since κ is central and κ²=0 this keeps CAR and `[b,b]`, and changes `[b_j^s, a^σ]` by `κ[b_j^s, w^σ]` (nonzero only for the conjugate boson), so a deformation with `gb_{σ,j,m} = −c_{σ,j,p}`, `gb_{σ,j,p} = +c_{σ,j,m}` is generated. The generators transform as `X' = X + κ F_c(X)`; **f := F_c**, computed exactly with the oscillator engine and decomposed in the Layer 1 basis. f is odd by construction (checked: no φ_ij with equal parity).
2. **Central direction.** f(X) for odd X can contain a constant, i.e. a K (=1) component (this is the origin of the Z = "K" part of γ found in I05). So f maps g → g ⊕ CK; K is central so it drops out of every [·,f(·)] and the K-part of δf is only −f_K([X,Y]). Recorded in the schema.
3. **Sign convention.** Tested both signs against γ: with the stated `c ↔ gb` correspondence and the doc's δ formula, γ = +δf exactly (the other sign gives γ = −δf), so no extra scalar is needed.
4. **Files.** Required `C_{n}_coboundary.json` = f for the all-plus gb profile; additionally `C_{n}_coboundary_<profile>.json` for the five other profiles of I06 (needed for the I08 comparison matrix). Spec appended to `docs/json_schema_specification.md`.
