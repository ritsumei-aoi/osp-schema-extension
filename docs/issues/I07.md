### Response

#### 1. Odd Linear Map Specification
Following `docs/math/C_coboundary_definition.md`, the odd linear map $f: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ satisfies $p(f(X)) = 1 - p(X)$:
- Maps $\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}} \to \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$ (parameterized by $\phi_{Y_i, X_j}$ for $X_j \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}, Y_i \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$).
- Maps $\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}} \to \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$ (parameterized by $\phi_{X_k, Y_l}$ for $Y_l \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}, X_k \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$).
- Total parameters: $2 \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}) = 2 \times (2n^2 + n + 1) \times 4n$.
  - $n=1$: $2 \times 4 \times 4 = 32$ parameters.
  - $n=2$: $2 \times 11 \times 8 = 176$ parameters.
  - $n=3$: $2 \times 22 \times 12 = 528$ parameters.

#### 2. Coboundary Operator Formula
The coboundary $\delta f: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ is computed by:
$$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
where each elementary component $E_{\text{tgt}, \text{src}}$ produces:
- $(-1)^{p(X)} [X, \text{tgt}]$ when $Y = \text{src}$.
- $-(-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, \text{tgt}]$ when $X = \text{src}$.
- $- c_{XY}^{\text{src}} \cdot \text{tgt}$ from the bracket decomposition.
