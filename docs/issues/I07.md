### Response

**Decisions**
- **Choice of f** (the issue leaves it open). Options considered: (a) an arbitrary fixed sample map (e.g. f(E)=H on a few generators) — would not be tied to the gb of Layer 3 and gives no information; (b) solve for f from γ by least squares and store the solution — correct but hides the structure; (c) derive f from the oscillator algebra. Chose (c), then use (b) in I08 as an independent search.
  Derivation: let D be the odd derivation of the *undeformed* oscillator algebra with D(a_1^±)=0, D(b_j^s)=Σ_σ' c_{j,s,σ'} a_1^σ'. The shift b → b' = b + κ D(b) gives [b_j^s', a^σ] = -gb_{σ,j,s}κ + κ c_{j,s,-σ}, so with c_{j,s,σ'} := gb_{-σ',j,s} the primed oscillators satisfy the *undeformed* relations. Writing generators as X(a,b) = X(a,b') - κDX one finds
  γ(X,Y) = D[X,Y] - [DX,Y] - (-1)^{p(X)}[X,DY] = (δf)(X,Y) with f := -D,
  using [X,κZ]=(-1)^{p(X)}κ[X,Z] and the identity (δf)(X,Y) = (-1)^{p(X)}[X,fY] + [fX,Y] - f[X,Y] (equivalent to the doc formula by supersymmetry of the bracket; checked numerically in the tests).
  f is odd: D(b b') ∝ a b' + a' b (even→odd) and D(a b) = -a D(b) ∝ a a' (odd→even).
- f is stored g-valued. D(a_p b_s) ∝ a_p a_m and a_p a_m = h_a + ½ = (Cartan combination) + ½·K, so f has a K-component for odd X (n=1 plus: 4 entries, n=2: 8, n=3: 12). Since K ∉ g it is recorded separately (`odd_map.central_component`) and does not enter δf. This is the same K-residue as in I05 (ambiguity 2), and it is the only place where Layer 3 and Layer 4 can differ (see I08).
- Parameter profiles are those of I06 (plus/minus/alt/zero/single); `C_{n}_coboundary.json` is `plus`.
- Spec section 4 appended to `docs/json_schema_specification.md`.
