### Response

#### Decisions and Results

**Implementation**: Created `src/C_coboundary.py` implementing the coboundary formula:
$$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)}[X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)}[Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$

The odd linear map $f$ is parameterized by phi coefficients:
- $f(\text{even gen}) = \sum_i \phi_{i,j} \cdot (\text{odd gen}_i)$ (even → odd)
- $f(\text{odd gen}) = \sum_i \phi_{i,j} \cdot (\text{even gen}_i)$ (odd → even)

**Phi parameter counts**:
- n=1: 32 parameters (4 even × 4 odd + 4 odd × 4 even)
- n=2: 176 parameters (11 × 8 + 8 × 11)
- n=3: 528 parameters (22 × 12 + 12 × 22)

**Generated files**:
- `data/C_1_coboundary.json`: 28 coboundary entries
- `data/C_2_coboundary.json`: 171 coboundary entries
- `data/C_3_coboundary.json`: 561 coboundary entries

**Consistency**: All generators in coboundary results verified to be in the Layer 1 basis for n=1,2,3.
