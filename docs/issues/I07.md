### Response

**Decisions and Results**:

1. **Odd linear map $f$**: Parameterized as $f(Z_j) = \sum_i \phi_{ij} Z_i$ where $p(Z_i) \neq p(Z_j)$ (parity-reversing). This gives:
   - C(2): 32 phi parameters (4 even × 4 odd × 2 directions)
   - C(3): 176 phi parameters (11 × 8 × 2)
   - C(4): 528 phi parameters (22 × 12 × 2)

2. **Coboundary formula**: $(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)}[X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)}[Y, f(X)] - f([X,Y])$
   Implemented as three terms, each iterated over all phi parameters and basis elements.
