#### 1. Mathematical Formulation & Implementation
We implemented Layer 4 coboundary generation in [src/C_coboundary.py](file:///Users/aoi/notebook/git/thm01-R-batch/thm01-R-gemini38f-b06-G/src/C_coboundary.py).
An odd linear map $f \in \operatorname{Hom}_{\bar{1}}(\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g})$ reverses parity:
- For $X \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$, $f(X) \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$.
- For $X \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$, $f(X) \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$.

The space $\operatorname{Hom}_{\bar{1}}(\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g})$ is parameterized by elementary odd maps:
$$f(Z_j) = Z_i \quad (p(Z_i) \neq p(Z_j))$$
parameterized by symbols `phi_Zi_Zj`.
The total dimension is $2 \cdot \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \cdot \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}})$:
- $n=1$: $2 \times 4 \times 4 = 32$
- $n=2$: $2 \times 11 \times 8 = 176$
- $n=3$: $2 \times 22 \times 12 = 528$

The differential coboundary operator on an odd 1-cochain $f$ is given by:
$$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
where the Lie bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]$ is the undeformed Lie superalgebra bracket of $C(n+1)$ from Layer 1.
