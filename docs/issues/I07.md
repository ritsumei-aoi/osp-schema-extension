### Response

### 1. Implementation Details
- Implemented `src/C_coboundary_generators.py` to evaluate the coboundary operator:
  $$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
  for any odd linear map $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\bar{1}}(\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g})$.
- The parameter space $\mathrm{Hom}_{\bar{1}}(\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g})$ is spanned by basis maps $f_{\phi_{ij}}(Z_j) = Z_i$ where $p(Z_i) \neq p(Z_j)$, having dimension:
  - $n=1$: $2 \times 4 \times 4 = 32$ parameters.
  - $n=2$: $2 \times 11 \times 8 = 176$ parameters.
  - $n=3$: $2 \times 22 \times 12 = 528$ parameters.
- Output Schema 4 JSON files in `data/`:
  - `data/C_1_coboundary.json` ($n=1, C(2)$): 394 non-zero coboundary entries.
  - `data/C_2_coboundary.json` ($n=2, C(3)$): 5,354 non-zero coboundary entries.
  - `data/C_3_coboundary.json` ($n=3, C(4)$): 24,198 non-zero coboundary entries.
