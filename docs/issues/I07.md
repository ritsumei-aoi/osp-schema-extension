### Response

#### 1. Mathematical Grounding & Coboundary Implementation
- Defined the odd linear map $f: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ with parameterization $f(Z_b) = \sum_{a} \phi_{ab} Z_a$ where $p(Z_a) \neq p(Z_b)$.
- Implemented `src/C_coboundary_generators.py` evaluating the standard Lie superalgebra coboundary operator:
  $$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
- Total number of odd linear map parameters $\phi_{ab}$ equals $2 \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}})$:
  - $n=1$ ($C(2)$): $2 \times 4 \times 4 = 32$ parameters.
  - $n=2$ ($C(3)$): $2 \times 11 \times 8 = 176$ parameters.
  - $n=3$ ($C(4)$): $2 \times 22 \times 12 = 528$ parameters.
