### 1. Coboundary Map Formulation
- The odd linear map $f: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ maps $f(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \subseteq \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$ and $f(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}) \subseteq \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$:
  $$f(Z_j) = \sum_{i: p(Z_i) \neq p(Z_j)} \phi_{Z_i, Z_j} Z_i$$
  Total parameters of $f$: $2 \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \times \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}})$ ($n=1: 32$, $n=2: 176$, $n=3: 528$).
- The coboundary operator $\delta f: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ is computed by:
  $$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
