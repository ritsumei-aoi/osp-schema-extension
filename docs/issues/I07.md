### Response

#### 1. Implementation Summary
- Developed `src/C_coboundary.py` to evaluate the coboundary operator $(\delta f)(X, Y)$ generated by odd linear maps $f: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ (where $p(f(X)) = 1 - p(X)$):
  $$(\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y])$$
- Parametrized $f$ using elementary map parameters $\phi_{i, j}$ corresponding to basis mappings $f(Z_j) = Z_i$ with $p(Z_i) = 1 - p(Z_j)$.
  - $n=1$: 32 parameters ($4 \text{ even} \times 4 \text{ odd} + 4 \text{ odd} \times 4 \text{ even}$)
  - $n=2$: 176 parameters ($11 \text{ even} \times 8 \text{ odd} + 8 \text{ odd} \times 11 \text{ even}$)
  - $n=3$: 528 parameters ($22 \text{ even} \times 12 \text{ odd} + 12 \text{ odd} \times 22 \text{ even}$)
- Generated Schema 4 JSON files: `data/C_1_coboundary.json`, `data/C_2_coboundary.json`, and `data/C_3_coboundary.json`.

#### 2. Verification & Testing Output
- Implemented unit tests in `tests/test_C_coboundary.py` verifying:
  - Exact match of parameter count $2 \cdot \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}) \cdot \dim(\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}})$.
  - Parity grading of $(\delta f)(X, Y)$: $p((\delta f)(X, Y)) = (p(X) + p(Y) + 1) \pmod 2$.
  - Graded anti-symmetry of $(\delta f)(X, Y)$ across all generator pairs and linear map parameters.
- Test execution: `pytest` passed 14/14 tests cleanly.
