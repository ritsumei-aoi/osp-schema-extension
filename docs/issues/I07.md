### Response

#### 1. Choice of the odd linear map $f$ (decision)
Options considered:
* (a) **Generic** $f$ with $2\cdot(2n^2+n+1)\cdot 4n$ free symbols $\phi_{ij}$ and store $\delta f$ as a
  matrix — rejected as the stored object: for $n=3$ this is 528 symbols × ~1 000 cochain entries; the
  generic operator is instead built on the fly in I08 (`coboundary(bt, f)` is written for *any* $f$).
* (b) **Canonical** $f$ derived from the oscillator algebra — chosen. Reasoning: the deformed relation
  $[b_k^s,a_1^\sigma]=-\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,k,s}\kappa$ is removed by the change of variables
  $a_1^\sigma\mapsto a_1'^\sigma=a_1^\sigma+\kappa S^\sigma$ with
  $S^\sigma=\sum_l(\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,l,-}b_l^+-\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,l,+}b_l^-)$:
  indeed $[b_k^s,\kappa S^\sigma]=\kappa[b_k^s,S^\sigma]=+\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,k,s}\kappa$, and
  $\{a_1'^-,a_1'^+\}=1+O(\kappa^2)=1$, $[b,b]$ unchanged. For every basis element
  $X(a_1',b)=X(a_1,b)+\kappa f(X)$ ($\kappa^2=0$), which defines an odd map $f$ with
  $f(X)\in\mathfrak g\oplus\mathbb C K$. Expanding $[X(a'),Y(a')]$ two ways gives
  $\gamma(X,Y)=f([X,Y\})-(-1)^{p(X)}[X,f(Y)\}-[f(X),Y\} = -(\delta f)(X,Y)$ with exactly the
  document's $\delta$ (this is where the supercentral-κ sign rule $[X,\kappa Y\}=(-1)^{p(X)}\kappa[X,Y\}$ is used).
  So $\gamma=\delta(-f)$ is predicted, and Layer 4 records $f$ and $\delta f$ for the comparison in I08.
* $f$ is computed **mechanically** (not from the hand formula): the script substitutes $a_1'^\sigma$ into the
  realization words in the engine and decomposes the κ-coefficient. Constant terms of $f(X)$
  (e.g. $-\tfrac12\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}$ in $f(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1})$) are dropped — legitimate since
  $\delta(cK)(X,Y)=-c([X,Y\})K$ is itself a constant — and recorded in `odd_map_f.dropped_constant_terms`.

Explicit $f$ for $n=1$ (symbolic):
$f(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1})=\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}H_2+\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,-}E_{2\delta_1}$,
$f(E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1})=-\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,-}H_2-\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}E_{-2\delta_1}$,
$f(E_{-\varepsilon\pm\delta_1})$ likewise with $\mathrm{gb}_{-,1,\cdot}$,
$f(H_1)=-\mathrm{gb}_{-,1,-}E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}+\mathrm{gb}_{-,1,+}E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1}+\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,-}E_{-\varepsilon+\delta_1}-\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}E_{-\varepsilon-\delta_1}$,
$f=0$ on $H_2, E_{\pm2\delta_1}$ (and on every purely bosonic generator for all $n$).

#### 2. Implementation
`src/C_coboundary.py`: `coboundary(bt, f)` implements
$(\delta f)(X,Y)=(-1)^{p(X)}[X,f(Y)\}-(-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)}[Y,f(X)\}-f([X,Y\})$ for an arbitrary
odd map given as $\{Z_j:\{Z_i:\phi_{ij}\}\}$ using the **Layer 1 JSON bracket table** (not the engine);
`canonical_f(n)` builds $f$; `check_layer4` verifies that $f$ is parity-reversing, that every label of
$f$ and $\delta f$ lies in the Layer 1 basis, that $p((\delta f)(X,Y))=p(X)+p(Y)+1$, and compares with γ.
Files: `C_{n}_coboundary.json` (symbolic linear forms) and `C_{n}_coboundary_{profile}.json` for the
seven I06 profiles. A bug surfaced in `oscillator.LinPoly` (constructor rejected a `LinPoly` argument) and was fixed.
