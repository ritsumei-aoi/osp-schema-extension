### Response

#### Mathematical ambiguities and the decisions taken
1. **Parity of the gb parameters.** `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` says "gb must have parity 1".
   But $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma]$ has parity 1 and $\kappa$ has parity 1, so if gb were an odd (Grassmann)
   quantity the right-hand side $-\mathrm{gb}\,\kappa$ would be *even* and the relation would violate
   parity. In the pre-oscillator-form picture of the same document, gb is just the mixed block
   $(b_j^s|a_1^\sigma)$ of a bilinear form, i.e. a number. **Decision**: gb are ordinary scalars; the
   odd parity of the deformation is carried by $\kappa$ (so $\gamma$ is an *odd* 2-cochain:
   $p(\gamma(X,Y)) = p(X)+p(Y)+1$). This is the only reading compatible with both the parity rule and the
   coboundary document (where $f$ is odd). Recorded in `handover/notation.md` §5 and in the JSON
   (`parameter_parity_note`).
2. **Where $\kappa$ sits and how it (anti)commutes.** Chosen: $\kappa$ supercentral, written on the left,
   $[X,Y\}_\gamma=[X,Y\}_0+\kappa\gamma(X,Y)$. Consequence $[X,\kappa Y\}=(-1)^{p(X)}\kappa[X,Y\}$,
   which reproduces the sign pattern of `C_coboundary_definition.md` (see I07).
3. **Constant terms.** The $\kappa$-part of a bracket may contain a constant ($K$) term, e.g.
   $[a_1^+b_1^-,(b_1^+)^2]$ has $\kappa$-part $-2\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}\,b_1^+b_1^- = -2\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}(H_2) - \mathrm{gb}_{+,1,+}$ for $n=1$.
   Following "realized ... by adjoint representation" (the document) and "$K=1$ is excluded from the basis"
   (B(0,n) v5.0), these terms are **dropped from γ** but stored verbatim under
   `inhomogeneous_deformation.dropped_constant_terms` so nothing is lost (they are re-examined in I08).

#### Implementation
* `src/C_gamma.py`: `compute_gamma(n)` runs the deformed engine (`OscillatorAlgebra(n, deformed=True)`),
  splits each bracket into $\kappa$-free and $\kappa$ parts and decomposes both.
  `gb_matrix` is the $2\times 2n$ array with rows `a_1_p, a_1_m`, columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, ..., b_n_p, b_n_m`,
  entries `gb_a_1_{σ}_b_{k}_{s}`; the $4n$ deformed oscillator relations are listed explicitly.
* `check_consistency()` verifies (a) the $\kappa$-free part equals the Schema 1 file, (b) the parity rule,
  (c) every coefficient is a homogeneous linear form in the gb symbols, and (d) the **order-κ Jacobi
  identity** of $L=\mathfrak g\oplus\kappa\mathfrak g$ (derived by hand from the supercentral rule):
  $\gamma(X,[Y,Z])+(-1)^{p(X)}[X,\gamma(Y,Z)]-\gamma([X,Y],Z)-[\gamma(X,Y),Z]-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}\gamma(Y,[X,Z])-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)+p(Y)}[Y,\gamma(X,Z)]=0$
  on all ordered triples. (d) is the real test that the κ conventions and the decomposition are coherent.
* `tests/test_C_gamma.py` (14 parametrised tests, incl. hand-computed n=1 values and a negative control for (d)).
