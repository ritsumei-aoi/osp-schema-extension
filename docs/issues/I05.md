### Response

#### Design
- **gb matrix** (2 × 2n): rows `a_1_p, a_1_m`, columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, …, b_n_p, b_n_m`; entry label `gb_a1{σ}_b{j}{s}` = $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_j^s}$; 4n parameters.
- **Deformed relation**: $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\kappa$ (exactly as in `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`), κ odd central in $A$ ($x\kappa = (-1)^{p(x)}\kappa x$, $\kappa^2=0$); all other oscillator relations unchanged.
- **γ computation** (`src/C_gamma.py`): for each ordered pair of basis elements, the bracket of their (fixed) oscillator realizations is normal-ordered in the deformed $\mathcal{U}(A)$ with *symbolic* gb, split as $[X,Y]_0 + \kappa\,\gamma(X,Y)$ (κ on the left), and both parts decomposed exactly in $\mathfrak{g} \oplus \mathbb{C}K$. γ comes out linear in gb (asserted).
- **`inhomogeneous_deformation` field**: list of `{X, Y, Z, coeff: {gb_label: rational}}` with `Z ∈ basis ∪ {"K"}`; spec updated in `docs/json_schema_specification.md` §3.

#### Ambiguities and decisions (No-Gate)
1. **Parity of gb.** The doc says "gb must have parity 1" because κ is odd. But $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma]$ is odd (even·odd) and κ is odd, so in $[b,a] = -\mathrm{gb}\,\kappa$ the coefficient must be *even* for parity to be preserved; this also matches the pre-oscillator-form construction, where gb are values of the $\mathbb{C}$-valued form $(b|a)$. (In the B(0,n) case, $a_0^2 = \tfrac12 + \kappa\sum \mathrm{gb}\, b$ genuinely needs odd gb — the statement seems inherited from there.) Options: (a) Grassmann-odd gb — inconsistent with parity preservation for C(n+1); (b) gb ordinary scalars, keep the label "parity 1" as metadata. **Chosen (b)**; recorded in `gb_matrix.parity_note`.
2. **Value space of γ.** The doc writes $\gamma:\mathfrak{g}\otimes\mathfrak{g}\to\mathfrak{g}$, but the computation produces central $K$ (=1) components, e.g. $\gamma$(`E_2del1_p`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`): $[(b_1^+)^2, a_1^+b_1^-]$ has κ-part $-2g\,b_1^+b_1^- = 2g H_2 + g K$, $g = \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_1^+}$ (hand-verified, also a unit test). Options: (a) drop $K$ parts, (b) store them as `Z = "K"`. **Chosen (b)**: no information is lost; dropping is a projection that I08 can apply explicitly. This turns out to be the crux of I08.
3. **Even–even pairs** also carry γ (through the $a_1^+a_1^-$ term in $H_1$), e.g. $\gamma(H_1,H_2)$ is odd; this is expected and included.
