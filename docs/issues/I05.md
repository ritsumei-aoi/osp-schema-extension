### Response

**Implementation: `src/C_gamma.py`**

**Method:** The deformation is $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma]_\text{def} = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s} \cdot \kappa$.
For each pair $(X, Y)$ with odd total parity, we compute $\gamma(X,Y)$ by extracting the linear-in-gb coefficient of the deformed bracket $[X,Y]_\gamma - [X,Y]_0$.

The algorithm for each gb parameter:
1. Replace one passage of $b_j^s$ (from $X$) past $a_1^\sigma$ (from $Y$) by $-1$ (setting that gb=1).
2. Multiply the remaining reduced monomials.
3. Decompose into basis generators.

This yields $\gamma(X,Y) = \sum_Z \text{coeff\_expr}(Z) \cdot Z$ where coeff_expr is a linear combination of gb parameters.

**Results:**

| n | gb_matrix size | gamma_terms | parity_violations | bad_Z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 54 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 8 | 344 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | 12 | 1148 | 0 | 0 |

All gamma terms satisfy parity constraint (only even-odd or odd-even pairs) and all $Z$ labels are in the basis.

**Sample (n=1):**
- $\gamma(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}, H_1)$: contributes to $H_1$ with $-\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_1^+}$ and to $H_2$ with $-2\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_1^+}$, and to $E_{2\delta_1}$ with $+\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_1^-}$.
