### Response

#### 1. Implementation
- Implemented `src/C_gamma.py` to evaluate the 2-cocycle $\gamma: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ mediating the inhomogeneous deformation $[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$.
- **`gb_matrix` specification**:
  - Dimensions: $2 \times 2n$ with $4n$ total parameters.
  - Rows: `["a_1_p", "a_1_m"]` (fermionic pair).
  - Columns: `["b_1_p", "b_1_m", ..., "b_n_p", "b_n_m"]` (CCR bosonic oscillators).
  - Parameter labels: `gb_a_1_{sigma}_b_{j}_{s}`.
  - Deformed exchange relation: $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_j^s} \cdot \kappa$.
- **Graded Leibniz 2-cocycle evaluator**:
  - Expands the supercommutator of quadratic oscillator words under the deformed exchange relations.
  - Normal-orders the residual operator and projects onto the basis of $\mathfrak{g}$.

#### 2. Generated Schema 2 Files in `data/`
- `data/C_1_gamma.json`: $C(2)$ (4 deformation parameters, 48 non-zero $\gamma_{XY}^Z$ terms).
- `data/C_2_gamma.json`: $C(3)$ (8 deformation parameters, 408 non-zero $\gamma_{XY}^Z$ terms).
- `data/C_3_gamma.json`: $C(4)$ (12 deformation parameters, 1,608 non-zero $\gamma_{XY}^Z$ terms).

#### 3. Verification & Testing
Added test suite `tests/test_c_gamma.py`:
- Verified exact $2 \times 2n$ dimension and parameter counts ($4n$) for $n=1, 2, 3$.
- Verified Schema 1 vs Schema 2 consistency across `algebra`, `basis`, `parity`, and `schema_version`.
- Verified graded anti-symmetry $\gamma(Y, X) = -(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} \gamma(X, Y)$ for all generator pairs.
- Verified parity grading rule: $p(Z) \equiv (p(X) + p(Y) + 1) \pmod 2$ for all non-zero terms (governed by odd parameter $\kappa$).
