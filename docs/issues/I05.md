### Response

**Decisions**
- gb matrix: `2 x 2n`, rows `a_1_p, a_1_m`, columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, ..., b_n_p, b_n_m`, entry `gb_a_1_{σ}_b_{j}_{s}` defined by `[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = -gb_{σ,j,s} κ` (4n parameters).
- Computation of γ_{abc}: not by hand formulas but by evaluating `[X,Y}` in the *deformed* oscillator algebra (rewriting rule `b a = a b - gb κ`, κ central odd, κ²=0, κ moved to the front with the graded sign) and reading off the κ-coefficient. Basis lifts are the PBW (fermion-first) words of Schema 1. Because κ²=0, γ is exactly linear in gb.
- **Ambiguity 1 (parity of gb)**: the docs say gb has parity 1, but `[b,a]` is odd and κ is odd, so a *Grassmann-odd* gb would make `gb κ` even — a parity mismatch. Assumption adopted: gb are ordinary commuting scalars; "parity 1" labels the odd oscillator pair (b,a). This is the only reading under which the bracket is parity-homogeneous.
- **Ambiguity 2 (K residue)**: the κ-part of `[X,Y]` is not always in g: for some pairs it contains `κ·1 = κ·K` (e.g. coefficient `-gb_{p,1,p}` for `(H_1, E_{ε+δ1})` at n=1). The docs declare γ: g⊗g→g and K=1 is not a basis element, so I store this scalar piece separately (`inhomogeneous_deformation.central_component`) and keep `gamma` g-valued. Options considered: (a) drop it (loses information and would break the cocycle identity), (b) enlarge the basis by K (contradicts the B(0,n) convention), (c) store separately (chosen). Its role in triviality is analysed in I08.
- Cartan lifts: the κ-part can also depend on how H_k are lifted (constants); I kept the I01 Cartans, which are the unique ones for which Schema 1 closes without a central term.
- Spec: Schema 2 section appended to `docs/json_schema_specification.md`.
