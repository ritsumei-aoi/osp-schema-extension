### Response

#### 1. Inhomogeneous Deformation Formulation
Implemented symbolic gamma structure computation in `src/C_gamma.py` based on `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`:
- **Deformation Parameters ($gb$ matrix)**:
  For each bosonic pair $k \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, there are 4 deformation parameters:
  $$\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+, b_k^+}, \quad \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+, b_k^-}, \quad \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-, b_k^+}, \quad \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-, b_k^-}$$
  forming a matrix of shape $2 \times 2n$ with $4n$ total parameters.
- **Deformed Bracket**:
  $$[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y) = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \sum_{p=1}^{4n} \mathrm{gb}_p \cdot \gamma_{XY}^{(p)}$$
- **Parity Preservation**:
  Since $p(\kappa) = 1$, the bracket $[X, Y]_\gamma$ preserves $\mathbb{Z}_2$-grading with $p(\gamma(X, Y)) \equiv p(X) + p(Y) + 1 \pmod 2$.
