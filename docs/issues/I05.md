**Design decisions**:
- Deformation relation: [b_j^s, a_1^σ] = -gb_{σ,j,s}*κ (from C_inhomogeneous_definition.md)
- 4n parameters: gb_{σ,j,s} for σ∈{+,-}, j=1,...,n, s∈{+,-}, all parity 1
- γ computed by tracking every (boson, fermion) swap in normal_order_product
  When b_j^s (left) moves past a_1^σ (right) in PBW normal ordering, the contraction
  term contributes -gb_{σ,j,s} * (remaining monomial). This gives γ(X,Y) as a sum over
  gb parameters of Lie algebra-valued contributions.

**Implementation**: `src/C_gamma.py`
- `normal_order_with_gamma(oscs)`: tracks gamma contributions in parallel with normal ordering
- `supercommutator_with_gamma(X, pX, Y, pY)`: applies to all (mX, mY) monomial pairs
- `compute_gamma_coefficients(n)`: computes all forward and reverse gamma entries
- `build_schema2(n)`: assembles Schema 2 JSON
