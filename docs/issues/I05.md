### Response

Implemented `src/C_gamma_generator.py` to derive the first-order gamma terms
from the deformed mixed oscillator relations using exact CAR/CCR
normal-ordering. It produces `data/C_1_gamma.json`, `data/C_2_gamma.json`,
and `data/C_3_gamma.json`, with respectively 4, 8, and 12 distinct gb
parameters and 58, 330, and 926 nonzero gamma components. Added the Schema 2
field definitions to `docs/json_schema_specification.md` and tests for matrix
shape, parameter coverage, canonical Schema 1 basis references, and parity.

Decision on an inconsistency: `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` described
gb parameters as odd, but its defining relation has an odd left side
($b$ is even and $a$ is odd) and odd $\kappa$. Homogeneity therefore requires
gb parity 0. I chose parity 0 rather than preserve the conflicting parity-1
note, corrected the reference note, and documented the reason in the schema
specification. The symbolic reducer places $\kappa$ to the left of the
oscillator word and includes the sign from moving odd $\kappa$ across an odd
prefix. Scalar terms are emitted as the existing central identity `K`, not
added to the Schema 1 basis or dimensions.
