### Response

#### 1. Mathematical Grounding & Gamma Computation
- Grounded in `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`, the inhomogeneous deformation is defined via the fundamental deformed commutation relations:
  $$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa, \quad [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = +\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
- The deformed Lie superalgebra bracket is:
  $$[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$$
  where $\kappa$ has parity 1 ($\kappa^2 = 0$) and deformation parameters $\mathrm{gb}$ have parity 1.
- Implemented `src/C_gamma_generators.py` using queue-based normal ordering across the deformed Weyl-Clifford algebra $W(1|n)$, extracting first-order variations $\kappa \cdot \Delta(X, Y)$ and projecting onto the $C(n+1)$ Lie algebra basis.
