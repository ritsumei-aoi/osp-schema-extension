### Response

**Method** (`src/C_gamma.py`)
- gb_matrix: 2 × 2n, rows a_1^±, columns b_j^±; 4n parameters `gb_a_1_{σ}_b_{j}_{s}`, parity 1.
- The oscillator engine of I03 is extended with the rewriting rule `b_j^s a^σ = a^σ b_j^s − gb_{σ,j,s} κ`, κ odd central (κ moves left past fermions with a sign), κ² = 0. The graded bracket `XY − (−1)^{xy}YX` of all ordered basis pairs is normal-ordered; words beginning with κ give κγ(X,Y). One unit parameter is switched on at a time; since κ² = 0, γ is exactly linear in gb (tested against random integer gb).
- Option considered: define the deformation through a modified "a²" as in B(0,n). Not applicable (no supplementary fermion); the exchange-relation form is the one given in `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`.

**Finding that required a decision (target space of γ)**: some κ-terms are κ·(2N_k + 2)-type, e.g. κ-part of `[E_eps1_del1_pp, E_2del1_m}` for n=1 = 2κ(b⁺b⁻+1) = −2κH₂ + κK. The scalar K (=1) is **not** in the span of the basis (the constant of H_{n+1} is tied to −N_n), so γ does not land in g but in g ⊕ ℂK. The definition document says γ: g⊗g → g, which cannot hold literally. Options: (a) drop K-components (silently wrong: loses terms, breaks cocycle); (b) enlarge the basis by K (changes dimension formulas fixed by the mandatory rules); (c) keep Schema 1 unchanged and let γ take values in g ⊕ ℂK, recorded under label `"K"`. **Chosen (c).** I07/I08 will treat both f: g→g (strict) and f: g→g⊕ℂK.

2. parity rule p(Z)=p(X)+p(Y)+1 for every γ entry;
