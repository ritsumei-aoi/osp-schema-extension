### 1. Inhomogeneous Deformation Implementation
- Implemented `src/C_gamma.py` to construct the $4n$ deformation parameters `gb_a1{sigma}_b{j}{s}` ($\sigma \in \{+, -\}$, $s \in \{+, -\}$, $j \in \{1, \ldots, n\}$) corresponding to the $2 \times 2n$ $\mathrm{gb}$ matrix.
- Computed the deformed Lie superalgebra 2-cocycle $\gamma: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ using exact recursive normal-ordering of oscillator products at order $\kappa$:
  $$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa, \quad [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
