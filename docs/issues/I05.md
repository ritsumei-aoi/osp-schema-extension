### Response

#### Method
`src/C_gamma.py` reuses the I03 engine with the deformed mixed relation
[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = -gb_{σ,j,s} κ (so [a_1^σ, b_j^s] = +gb κ), κ odd central, κ² = 0. For every ordered pair of
basis elements the bracket is computed in U(A), normal ordered (κ moved to the left with Koszul sign) and decomposed:
[X,Y]_γ = [X,Y]_0 + κ(γ(X,Y) + γ_K(X,Y) K). The κ⁰ part is checked to equal Schema 1; the κ¹ part is linear in gb.

#### gb_matrix (2 × 2n)
Rows `a_1_p, a_1_m`; columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, …, b_n_p, b_n_m`; entries `gb_a1{σ}_b{j}{s}`; 4n parameters.

#### Ambiguities and decisions
1. **Parity of gb.** The math doc says the gb "must have parity 1". [b, a] is odd and κ is odd, so gb·κ is odd only
   if gb is an even (ordinary) scalar; an odd gb would make the relation parity-violating. Options: (a) treat gb as
   Grassmann-odd parameters, (b) treat gb as ordinary scalars and read "parity 1" as the parity of the deformation
   term κ·gb (equivalently, γ is an *odd* cochain). Adopted (b); recorded in `gb_matrix.parameter_type`.
2. **Central (κK) terms.** The deformed brackets are not always inside g ⊕ κg: for X, Y of opposite parity a
   κ·constant term appears (e.g. [H_1, a⁺b⁺]_γ = 2a⁺b⁺ − κ(gb_{a1p,b1p} H_1 + gb_{a1p,b1m} E_2del1_p + gb_{a1p,b1p} K),
   hand-derived and matched by a unit test). Options: (a) drop it silently, (b) keep it in a separate list.
   Adopted (b): `gamma` holds the g-valued part (the cochain on L = g ⊕ κg of the math doc), `gamma_central` the
   κK part. Both are analysed in I08.
3. Pair coverage: all ordered pairs stored (antisymmetry is checked rather than assumed).

#### Consistency checks (all recorded in each JSON's `consistency_checks`)
- κ⁰ part of the deformed bracket == Schema 1 structure constants (exact equality of dicts).
- p(γ(X,Y)) = p(X)+p(Y)+1 for every entry; κK terms only for mixed parity.
- graded antisymmetry of γ and γ_K.
- **Cocycle condition**: super Jacobi of [ , ]_γ on L = g ⊕ κ(g ⊕ CK) to first order in κ, for all dim³ basis
  triples, both for the g-valued γ alone and with γ_K. Zero failures. A unit test corrupts one γ coefficient and
  confirms the check fails (non-vacuous).
