### Response

**Implementation: `src/C_gamma.py`**

The gamma cocycle arises from the deformed relation [b_j^s, a_1^sigma] = -gb_{sigma,j,s} * kappa.
For each pair (X_even, X_odd), the gamma coefficient is computed by identifying which
b oscillators in X_even "cross" the a_1^sigma in X_odd.

For X = b_i^{ti} b_j^{tj} (even) and Y = a_1^sigma b_k^s (odd):
  gamma(X, Y) = -gb_{sigma,j,tj} * b_i^{ti} b_k^s - gb_{sigma,i,ti} * b_j^{tj} b_k^s

The result b^{...} b_k^s products are expressed as basis generators (or Cartan combinations
for N_i = b_i^+b_i^- contributions, dropping the constant -1/2).

Also computed: gamma(H_k, Y) for all Cartan generators H_k, since H_k also contains b oscillators.

**Results**:
- n=1: 4 gb parameters, 24 gamma coefficients
- n=2: 8 gb parameters, 180 gamma coefficients
- n=3: 12 gb parameters, 588 gamma coefficients

**Consistency check**: All gamma entries involve basis generators only, with p(X)+p(Y)=1 (odd)
and p(Z)=0 (even). ✓ All 3 cases passed.
