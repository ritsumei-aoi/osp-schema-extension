### Response

**Decisions and Results**:

1. **gb matrix**: Defined with size $2 \times 2n = 4n$ parameters for C(n+1):
   - $\text{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_j^s}$ where $\sigma \in \{+,-\}$, $j = 1,\ldots,n$, $s \in \{+,-\}$
   - Deformation relation: $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\text{gb}_{\sigma,j,s} \cdot \kappa$

2. **Gamma computation**: Implemented in `src/C_gamma.py`:
   - `deformed_normal_order_gamma`: extends normal ordering to track first-order gb contributions from boson-fermion swaps
   - `express_gamma_in_basis`: maps oscillator gamma terms to basis generators using the Cartan inverse
   - `compute_gamma_bracket`: computes $\gamma(X, Y)$ such that $[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$
