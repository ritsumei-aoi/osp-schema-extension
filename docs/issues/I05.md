### Response

**Script**: `src/C_gamma.py`

**Method**: Extended normal-ordering to track kappa contributions. When swapping a boson b_j^s over a fermion a_1^sigma in the deformed algebra, the deformed exchange relation `b_j^s * a_1^sigma = a_1^sigma * b_j^s - gb_{sigma,j,s} * kappa` produces a kappa term. These are accumulated and decomposed in the basis to give gamma_{abc} coefficients symbolically linear in gb parameters.

**gb matrix**: 4n parameters, indexed by (sigma ∈ {p,m}, j=1..n, s ∈ {p,m}).

**Results**:
- C_1_gamma.json (n=1): 4 gb params, 50 gamma entries
- C_2_gamma.json (n=2): 8 gb params, 292 gamma entries
- C_3_gamma.json (n=3): 12 gb params, 842 gamma entries

**Consistency**: All gamma entries reference valid basis generators and gb labels. ✓
