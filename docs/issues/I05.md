### Response

#### gb Matrix

For C(n+1), the 4n deformation parameters are indexed by (sigma, j, s) where:
- sigma ∈ {p,m} (a_1^sigma label)
- j ∈ {1,...,n} (bosonic index)
- s ∈ {p,m} (b_j^s label)

Label convention: `gb_{sigma}{j}{s}` (e.g., `gb_p1p` = gb_{a_1^+, b_1^+}).

The exchange relation: [b_j^s, a_1^sigma] = -gb_{sigma,j,s} * kappa.

#### Gamma Computation

The symbolic gamma coefficients are computed by tracking where b-a crossings
introduce kappa factors. Three cases contribute:

1. **[E_even, E_odd]**: XY - YX introduces b_{ix}^{sx}*a_1^sigma crossing, giving:
   γ(X,Y) = -cx * gb_{sigma,ix,sx} * E(b_{jx}^{sx2}, b_k^s) - (i↔j term)
   
2. **[H_l, E_odd]**: H_l has bosonic N_b terms that similarly cross with a_1^sigma.

3. **{E_odd, E_odd}**: b_k^{s1}*a_1^sigma2 crossing gives:
   γ(X,Y) = -gb_{sigma2,kx,s1} * E_eps1_del{ky}_{sigma1,s2} - (symmetric term)
