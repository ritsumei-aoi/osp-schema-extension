### Response

**Decisions and assumptions:** The source defines `gb` and `κ` as odd, but
their product is even while the mixed bracket of an even boson and odd
fermion must be odd. I considered declining to assign gamma coefficients
until that parity conflict is resolved, or following the literal exchange
relation while treating parameter names as formal coefficient labels. I
chose the latter to complete the no-gate task; the generated data does not
infer additional signs from parameter parity, and the source inconsistency is
documented in the schema spec and JSON. I also found that some oscillator
terms require a scalar to be expressed in the documented Cartan basis. Rather
than discard those terms, I record them as target `K`, the central identity
already defined by Schema 1 and equal to 1.

**Results:** Added `src/C_gamma.py` for the 2×2n parameter matrix and exact
first-order coefficient extraction from the literal mixed relations. It
checks rank, basis, and parity against Schema 1 before generation. Created
`data/C_1_gamma.json`, `data/C_2_gamma.json`, and `data/C_3_gamma.json`, and
documented the Schema 2 field shape, central target, and parity caveat in
`docs/json_schema_specification.md`.

**Verification:** `python src/C_gamma.py` generated all three files.
`pytest -q tests/test_C_gamma.py` passed (4 tests), validating 2×2, 2×4, and
2×6 matrices, all Schema 1 basis/parameter references, and the rank-1
coefficient `γ(E_2del1_p,E_eps1_del1_mp) = -2 gb_a1_m_b1_p E_2del1_p`.
Generated gamma entry counts are 60, 334, and 932 for ranks 1, 2, and 3;
all reported targets are Schema 1 generators or `K`.
