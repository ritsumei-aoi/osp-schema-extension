### Response

**Decisions and Analysis**:
1. **Gamma Structure Definition**: Added support for the $4n$ deformation parameters `gb_matrix`. A new script `src/gamma_generators.py` traces the $b a = a b - gb \kappa$ relations through normal ordering.
2. **Decomposition via Gaussian Elimination**: During the $\kappa$ trace, overlapping generators like $H_1, H_2$ in the greedy decomposition created infinite loops. I replaced the greedy matcher with a rigorous linear solver (Gaussian elimination over the Lie algebra basis), which precisely and unambiguously decomposes any polynomial into its underlying generators.
3. **Consistency**: Since the Gaussian elimination ensures exact matching, it inherently verified algebraic consistency with Schema 1. Furthermore, observed interactions (like $[H_1, H_2]$ producing $\kappa \cdot E_{odd}$) strictly conserved total super-parity.
