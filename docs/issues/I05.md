#### Method
- Deformed oscillator algebra (`src/C_osc.py`, `deformed=True`): $b_j^s a_1^\sigma = a_1^\sigma b_j^s - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\kappa$,
  $\kappa$ odd supercentral ($\kappa x = (-1)^{p(x)}x\kappa$), $\kappa^2=0$; all other relations as in Schema 1.
- `src/C_gamma.py`: for every ordered pair of basis elements, compute $[X,Y\}$ in the deformed algebra, normal-order with
  $\kappa$ on the far left, split $[X,Y\} = P_0 + \kappa P_1$. Check $P_0 = [X,Y]_0$ (Schema 1), and decompose
  $\gamma(X,Y) := P_1$ in basis + `K`.
- `gb_matrix` (2 × 2n): rows `a_1_p, a_1_m`, columns `b_1_p, b_1_m, …, b_n_m`, symbol `gb_{σ}_{j}_{s}` = $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma,b_j^s}$; 4n parameters.
- Schema 2 field `inhomogeneous_deformation` = {`relation`, `bracket`, `gb_matrix`, `gamma_target`, `gamma`}, `gamma`
  is a list of `{X, Y, Z, coeff}` with `coeff` a SymPy-parsable linear form in the gb (spec §2).

#### Consistency checks with Schema 1 (all pass for n=1,2,3)
1. $\kappa$-free part of every deformed bracket equals the Schema 1 structure constants.
2. Basis order / labels identical to `C_{n}_structure.json`.
3. Parity: $p(Z) = p(X)+p(Y)+1$ for every γ entry (`K` counted as even), as required since $p(\kappa)=1$.
4. Every coefficient is a homogeneous linear form in the gb (no constant term).
5. Super-antisymmetry $\gamma(X,Y) = -(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}\gamma(Y,X)$.
6. **2-cocycle condition**: the algebra $L = g\oplus\kappa g\oplus\mathbb C\kappa K$ with $[X,Y]_L=[X,Y]_0+\kappa\gamma(X,Y)$,
   $[X,\kappa A]=(-1)^{p(X)}\kappa[X,A]_0$, $[\kappa A,Y]=\kappa[A,Y]_0$ is fed to the I04 Jacobi checker, for each unit
   gb vector (enough by linearity) — passes. A negative control (one γ coefficient doubled) is detected.

#### Decisions / ambiguities
- **Parity of gb**: the documents say the gb "have parity 1". Taken literally (Grassmann-odd gb) the product
  $\mathrm{gb}\cdot\kappa$ would be even, contradicting $[b,a]$ being odd. Adopted: gb are ordinary commuting scalars;
  "parity 1" is recorded in `gb_matrix.parity` with a note that it refers to the odd sector/odd $\kappa$ they multiply.
- **Central part**: $\gamma(X,Y)$ may contain a constant ($\kappa\cdot1$) whenever $p(X)\ne p(Y)$ (20/84/180 entries). This
  is not in $g$; it is stored as `Z = "K"` rather than dropped, so that I08 can test triviality both modulo and including it
  ("up to scalar" in `C_coboundary_definition.md`).
- **Sign convention of $\kappa$ placement**: $\kappa$ is put on the left ($[X,Y]_\gamma=[X,Y]_0+\kappa\gamma$), matching the
  derivation of the $\delta f$ formula in I07 (an isomorphism $X\mapsto X+\kappa f(X)$).
