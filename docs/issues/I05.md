### Response

#### Mathematical setup adopted (and ambiguities resolved)
* Deformed relations: $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\kappa$, κ odd, $\kappa^2=0$,
  super-central ($\kappa b = b\kappa$, $\kappa a = -a\kappa$). The gb matrix is $2\times 2n$
  (rows $a_1^+, a_1^-$; columns $b_1^+, b_1^-,\dots,b_n^+,b_n^-$), $4n$ parameters.
* **Parity of gb (ambiguity)**: the math note says gb has parity 1, but $[b,a]$ is odd and κ is odd,
  so a literal odd gb would make the relation parity-inconsistent. Adopted: gb are even scalars; the
  "parity 1" is the parity of the mixed (boson–fermion) block of the pre-oscillator form in which they
  live. Recorded in the `parity_note` of `gb_matrix` and in `notation.md` §5.
* **Codomain of γ (new finding)**: $\gamma(X,Y)$ is not always in $\mathfrak g$: contraction of one
  $(b,a)$ pair into κ can leave $a_1^+a_1^-$ or $b_k^+b_k^-$, whose symmetrized part is in $\mathfrak g$
  but whose constant part is $\pm\tfrac12 K$. Hence $\gamma:\mathfrak g\otimes\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak g\oplus\mathbb{C}K$
  and the deformed algebra lives on $L=\mathfrak g\oplus\kappa(\mathfrak g\oplus\mathbb{C}K)$. Schema 2
  therefore allows `Z = "K"`. Example (n=1): $\gamma(N_a, E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1}) = -\mathrm{gb}_{+,1,-}(N_a+\tfrac12 K)$
  with $N_a = H_1+H_2$.
* Pure deformation parity rule $p(Z)=p(X)+p(Y)+1$; purely bosonic pairs are undeformed; the Cartan
  subalgebra becomes non-abelian at order κ (e.g. $\gamma(H_1,H_2)\neq0$).

#### Implementation
`src/C_gamma.py`: `compute_gamma(n)` evaluates every ordered super-commutator in the deformed
oscillator algebra (`OscillatorAlgebra(n, deformed=True)`) and reads off the κ-component via
`CBasis.decompose`; `build_schema2` writes `data/C_{n}_gamma.json` (spec §2); `check_consistency`
performs four checks: (1) gb-independent part == Schema 1 on disk, (2) γ linear homogeneous in gb,
(3) parity rule, (4) 2-cocycle condition = super Jacobi of the deformed bracket at order κ over all
basis triples, symbolically in gb (`check_cocycle`).

Options considered for the γ representation: (a) numeric per sign profile only (rejected: loses the
linear dependence on gb needed for I08's general conjecture); (b) **symbolic linear polynomials with a
parallel machine-readable `coeff_terms` map (chosen)**; (c) sympy expressions (rejected: slower,
and exact `Fraction` polynomials suffice because κ²=0 keeps everything linear).
