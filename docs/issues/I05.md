#### Decisions and Results

**Implementation**: Created `src/C_gamma.py` implementing:
- `normal_order_with_gb(word, coeff, result)`: Modified normal ordering that tracks gb contractions arising from deformed boson-fermion commutators [b_j^s, a_1^sigma] = -gb * kappa.
- `compute_gamma_bracket(x, y, n)`: Computes gamma(X, Y) as the kappa-linear correction to the bracket.
- `compute_all_gamma(n)`: Iterates over all generator pairs and collects nonzero gamma entries.

**Design**: The gamma computation hooks into the normal ordering process. Each boson-fermion swap generates a gb correction; the remaining oscillators are normal-ordered using undeformed relations and decomposed into basis generators.

**gb matrix**: For C(n+1), the gb matrix has 4n parameters:
- gb_{a1p_bj{s}} and gb_{a1m_bj{s}} for j=1,...,n, s∈{p,m}
- All parameters have parity 1 (since kappa is odd)
