#### Method and conventions (ambiguity record, per ai_trust_policy)

γ(X,Y) is computed from first principles: the deformed oscillator relation
`[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = −gb_{σ,j,s}·κ` is wired into the normal-ordering engine as
an extra first-order-in-κ contraction channel (`oscillator_algebra.py`,
`deform=True`); `κ² = 0` terms are dropped. Bookkeeping conventions adopted
(recorded because the math docs leave them implicit):
- κ is odd and supercentral; every κ-term is normalized with κ at the far
  left, picking up a sign `(−1)^{p(prefix)}` when moved past odd letters.
- The gb symbols are treated as *formal commuting scalar coefficients*
  attached to κ (their declared parity 1 lives in the pairing with κ, which
  is what the parity consistency check below validates). This matches the
  B(0,n) treatment where gb parameters multiply κ in `a_0²`.
- γ(X,Y) is then literally the coefficient of κ in `[X,Y}_deformed`, matching
  `[X,Y]_γ = [X,Y]_0 + κ·γ(X,Y)` in `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`.

γ(X,Y) may contain a scalar component (from a deformed contraction followed
by a classical one); it is recorded as `Z = "K"`, consistent with the
pre-oscillator-form picture where mixed-parity pairings land on κ itself.
