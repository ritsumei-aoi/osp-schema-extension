### Response

#### 1. Formulation and Implementation
- Implemented `src/C_gamma.py` to evaluate the inhomogeneous deformation of $C(n+1) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)$ using the deformed oscillator exchange relations:
  $$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \quad (\sigma \in \{+, -\},\ s \in \{+, -\},\ j=1,\ldots,n)$$
- Constructed the $2 \times 2n$ `gb_matrix` representing the $4n$ deformation parameters indexed by the fermionic pair $(a_1^+, a_1^-)$ and bosonic oscillators $(b_j^+, b_j^-)$:
  - $n=1$: 4 parameters (`gb_a1_p_b1_p`, `gb_a1_p_b1_m`, `gb_a1_m_b1_p`, `gb_a1_m_b1_m`)
  - $n=2$: 8 parameters
  - $n=3$: 12 parameters
- Computed the deformed Lie bracket $[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$ and expanded $\gamma(X, Y)$ into the basis of $C(n+1)$. Central scalar components $K$ act trivially in the adjoint representation, yielding clean, exact rational decomposition into the Lie superalgebra basis.
