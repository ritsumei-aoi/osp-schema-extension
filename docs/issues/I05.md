### Response

**Decision and implementation:** Added `src/C_gamma.py` to build the $2\times2n$ parameter matrix and compute first-order gamma terms from the stated mixed-oscillator exchange relation, retaining rational coefficients and dropping terms of order $\kappa^2$. Generated Schema 2 files for ranks 1–3. Gamma outputs may contain `K` when the scalar identity component is needed. The generator verifies that rank, family, basis, and parity agree with the matching Schema 1 data.

The source definitions contain a parity conflict: they assign parity 1 to both `gb` and $\kappa$, so `gb*kappa` is even, but $[b,a]$ for an even $b$ and odd $a$ must be odd. Considered changing `gb` to even (which would restore parity compatibility) or retaining the stated parities and proceeding formally. Chose the latter to honor the fixed definitions and avoid silently changing their assumptions; the generated files and schema documentation explicitly mark the result as formal-only until the parity convention is resolved. Under the stated odd-odd coefficient convention, $-\mathrm{gb}\,\kappa=+\kappa\,\mathrm{gb}$ when $\kappa$ is factored on the left.

**Verification:** Schema 2 consistency checks passed against Schema 1 bases and parity maps. Generated parameter/gamma term counts were: C(2), 4 parameters/60 terms; C(3), 8/334; C(4), 12/932. `pytest -q` passed all 27 tests, covering matrix shape, all parameter references and gamma labels, the basic deformed exchange relation, first-order truncation, and generated-file reproducibility.
