### Response

#### 1. Deformation Matrix `gb_matrix`
The inhomogeneous deformation parameters are indexed by the fermionic CAR pair (`a_1_p`, `a_1_m`) and bosonic CCR oscillators (`b_k_p`, `b_k_m`, $k=1,\ldots,n$):
- Size: $2 \times 2n = 4n$ parameters.
- Parameters: $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_k^s}$ where $\sigma \in \{+, -\}$, $s \in \{+, -\}$.
- Deformed exchange relation:
  $$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_j^s} \cdot \kappa.$$

#### 2. Gamma Cocycle Computation
The deformation is realized in the oscillator enveloping algebra $\mathcal{U}(A)$ with odd central parameter $\kappa$ ($\kappa^2 = 0$):
- Commutator: $[X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$.
- Reduction algorithm: Whenever a bosonic oscillator $b_j^s$ is commuted past a fermionic oscillator $a_1^\sigma$, the first-order $\kappa$-contribution $- \mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma, b_j^s}$ is extracted.
- Modulo Central Element $K$: In the adjoint representation, central scalars act trivially. Elements are projected onto the Lie superalgebra basis $\mathfrak{g} \subset \mathcal{U}(A) / \mathbf{C}K$.
- Graded Parity: Since $\kappa$ is odd ($p(\kappa) = 1$) and $[X, Y]_\gamma$ preserves parity, the deformed bracket component $\gamma(X, Y)$ satisfies:
  $$p(Z) \equiv (p(X) + p(Y) + 1) \pmod 2.$$
