### Response

#### Schema 2 Implementation

**1. Gamma Structure Generator: `src/C_gamma_generator.py`**

Implemented `CnPlus1GammaGenerator` class to produce Schema 2 (inhomogeneous deformation).

Key components:
- **GB Matrix**: 2 × 2n matrix of deformation parameters
  - Rows: a_1^+ and a_1^- (two fermionic oscillators)
  - Columns: b_k^+ and b_k^- for k=1,...,n (bosonic oscillators)
  - Total parameters: 4n
  
- **Gamma Structure**: Encodes deformation via γ_{abc} coefficients
  - Parametrized by gb_{σ,j,s} parameters
  - Deformed bracket: [X, Y]_γ = [X, Y]_0 + κ · γ(X, Y)
  - γ relates to fermionic-bosonic interaction terms

- **Deformation Model**: Interaction between standard CAR pair and CCR bosons
  - Exchange relation: [b_j^s, a_1^σ] = -gb_{σ,j,s} · κ
  - Preserves graded structure and nilpotency of κ

**5. Mathematical Grounding**

Deformation based on skew-supersymmetric bilinear form on oscillator space:
$$[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = -\mathrm{gb}_{σ,j,s} \cdot \kappa$$

Results in deformed bracket:
$$[X, Y]_γ = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$$

where γ is a 2-cocycle satisfying consistency with structure constants.
