### Response

Implemented `src/C_gamma.py` and generated `data/C_1_gamma.json`,
`data/C_2_gamma.json`, and `data/C_3_gamma.json`. The generator applies the
mixed oscillator exchange relations to first order, extracts exact rational
coefficients, and records the `2 x 2n` parameter matrix and gamma brackets.
Scalar terms are represented by `K`, the identity symbol already declared in
Schema 1.

Decision on a source ambiguity: `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` originally
said gb has parity 1, but its relation has an even oscillator and an odd
oscillator on the left, and odd $\kappa$ on the right. Since
$p([b,a])=1=p(\kappa)$, parity preservation requires $p(\mathrm{gb})=0$.
Considered retaining odd gb (which makes the right-hand side even) or making
$\kappa$ even (which contradicts its stated central-extension parity).
Instead kept the exchange relation and odd central $\kappa$, corrected the
inconsistent gb parity note, and set `parameter_parity` to 0 in Schema 2.
This makes each computed $\kappa\,\mathrm{gb}\,Z$ correction have the same
parity as its bracket inputs.

Verification: `python3 -m pytest tests/test_C_gamma.py -q` passed (6 tests),
checking rank/field/basis/parameter consistency, parity, and first-order
super-Jacobi for every generator triple at ranks 1, 2, and 3. Generated
gamma-entry totals are 58, 330, and 926 (with respectively 4, 8, and 12
deformation relations). Completion criteria are checked above.
