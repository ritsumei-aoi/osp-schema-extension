the inhomogeneous deformation (gamma structure, Schema 2).

the standard fermionic pair (a1+, a1-) and the bosonic oscillators. Refer to

### Response

#### Decisions and Results

**Implementation**: Created `src/C_gamma.py` that computes gamma coefficients by:
1. Modifying the normal-ordering procedure to track deformation terms: when a boson $b_j^s$ is swapped past a fermion $a_1^\sigma$, the deformed relation $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\text{gb}_{\sigma,j,s} \cdot \kappa$ generates a κ-proportional term.
2. Computing the deformed bracket $[X, Y]_\gamma$ for all generator pairs.
3. Expressing the κ-proportional part in terms of basis generators and gb parameters.

**gb matrix design**: Size 2 × 2n, with parameters indexed by (fermion sign, boson index, boson sign):
- n=1: 4 parameters (gb_a1p_b1_p, gb_a1p_b1_m, gb_a1m_b1_p, gb_a1m_b1_m)
- n=2: 8 parameters
- n=3: 12 parameters

**Generated files**:
- `data/C_1_gamma.json`: 4 gb params, 23 gamma entries
- `data/C_2_gamma.json`: 8 gb params, 106 gamma entries
- `data/C_3_gamma.json`: 12 gb params, 281 gamma entries

**Consistency verification**: All generators in gamma results confirmed to exist in the Schema 1 basis for n=1,2,3.
