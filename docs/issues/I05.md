### Response

**Approach.** I extended the oscillator engine with a deformation hook `gb` implementing
$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\,\kappa$ (nilpotent odd $\kappa$, $\kappa^2=0$).
`src/C_gamma.py` computes, for each basis pair, the **deformed** graded commutator and splits it into
its $\kappa^0$ part (must equal Schema 1) and its $\kappa^1$ part, which is exactly $\gamma(X,Y)$.
The $\kappa^1$ part is decomposed onto the basis via the same `decompose` routine (which handles
symbolic gb-linear coefficients), giving $\gamma$ coefficients **linear in the 4n gb parameters**.

**(1) gb_matrix.** Size $2\times 2n$: rows = fermion sign $\sigma\in\{+,-\}$, columns =
$(j,s)$ with $j=1..n$, boson sign $s\in\{+,-\}$. 4n parameters named `gb_{F}_{j}_{B}`
(e.g. `gb_p_1_p` $=\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_1^+}$), parity 1.

**(2) gamma coefficients.** Computed for all PBW pairs $X\le Y$; the opposite order follows from the
graded cochain symmetry $\gamma(X,Y)=-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}\gamma(Y,X)$ (verified in tests). $\gamma$ is a
genuine 2-cocycle because the deformed bracket is itself a graded commutator in the (still
associative) deformed algebra $L=\mathfrak g\oplus\kappa\mathfrak g$.

**(3) `inhomogeneous_deformation` field** records carrier $\kappa$, the deformation relation, and the
bracket law $[X,Y]_\gamma=[X,Y]_0+\kappa\,\gamma(X,Y)$.

**(4) Outputs.** `data/C_1_gamma.json` (62 γ-records), `data/C_2_gamma.json` (337),
`data/C_3_gamma.json` (936).

**(5) Consistency with Schema 1.** `consistency_with_schema1` checks that the $\kappa^0$ part of every
deformed bracket exactly reproduces the I03 structure constants: **PASS for n=1,2,3** (no mismatches).

**Hand-check (n=1).** $\gamma(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1},E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1})=-2\,\mathrm{gb}_{p,1,p}\,
E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}$: the undeformed self-anticommutator vanishes ($(a_1^+)^2=0$); the single
deformed swap $b^+a^+=a^+b^+-\mathrm{gb}_{p,1,p}\kappa$ yields $(a_1^+b_1^+)^2=-\mathrm{gb}_{p,1,p}\kappa\,
a_1^+b_1^+$, doubled. Matches the engine.

**Tests.** `tests/test_C_gamma.py` (gb count, consistency, on-basis targets, hand value, cochain
antisymmetry). Full suite: 33 passed. All completion criteria met.
