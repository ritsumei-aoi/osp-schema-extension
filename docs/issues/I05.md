#### Decision and results

Implemented `src/C_gamma.py` to construct the \(2\times2n\) gb matrix and
derive first-order coefficients from the exchange relations using exact
CAR/CCR normal ordering. Considered preserving the source's statement that
`gb` is odd versus assigning it even parity. Preserving odd parity makes
`gb*kappa` even, although `[b,a]` is odd, and fails the CAR compatibility
condition for generic independent parameters. Chose parity 0, which makes
the relation homogeneous and preserves the CAR with odd central `kappa`;
corrected the parity statement in `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`
