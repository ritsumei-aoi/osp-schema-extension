## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

**Decision and rationale:** Finalized `handover/notation.md` with explicit even and odd basis lists for C(2), C(3), and C(4), oscillator label mappings, parity, and the CAR/CCR conventions. Considered (1) grouping all odd generators before the even generators, following the B(0,n) schema, and (2) triangular order with negative roots before Cartan and positive roots after. Chose (2), since it exposes the positive-root convention directly and is more useful for consistent root-space and bracket organization. Specified the positive odd roots as $\varepsilon\pm\delta_k$ and positive even roots as $\delta_i\pm\delta_j$ ($i<j$) and $2\delta_k$, plus deterministic orders within each group.

**Verification:** The displayed dimensions match the C(n+1) formulas: C(2) has $4+4=8$, C(3) has $11+8=19$, and C(4) has $22+12=34$. The generic lists give $n+1+2n+4\binom n2=2n^2+n+1$ even generators and $4n$ odd generators. Generator labels distinguish the signs of $\varepsilon$ and $\delta_k$, and the realization table uses `a_1_p/m` and `b_k_p/m` consistently.
