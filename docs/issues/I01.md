## Context & Background

B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### 2. PBW Ordering: Options and Decision

Two concrete ordering schemes were evaluated:
- **Option A (Parity-Grouped: $\kappa < [\text{Odd}] < [\text{Even}]$)**:
  - $\kappa$ precedes all generators.
  - Odd root generators $E_{\pm \varepsilon \pm \delta_k}$ ordered lexicographically by bosonic index $k$, then sign combination `(pp, pm, mp, mm)`.
  - Even generators ordered as: Cartans $H_1, \ldots, H_{n+1}$, positive long roots $E_{2\delta_k}^+$, positive cross roots $E_{\delta_i+\delta_j}^{++}$, negative long roots $E_{2\delta_k}^-$, negative cross roots $E_{\delta_i+\delta_j}^{--}$, and mixed cross roots $E_{\delta_i\pm\delta_j}^{+-}, E^{-+}$.
- **Option B (Triangular Root-Height Graded Ordering)**:
  - $\kappa < \text{Cartan} < \text{Positive Roots (Even + Odd)} < \text{Negative Roots (Even + Odd)}$.

**Decision**: **Option A** is adopted because it matches the established schema convention of $B(0,n)$ v5.0 (`PBW: κ < [odd] < [even]`), allows direct reuse of the 4-layer schema architecture, and provides clear separation between even Lie algebra brackets and odd deformation sectors.
