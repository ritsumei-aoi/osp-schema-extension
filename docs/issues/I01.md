## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

**Decisions** (full options analysis in `handover/notation.md`):
- Basis: Weyl-symmetrized quadratic elements $W(x,y)$ over the oscillators $a_1^\pm, b_k^\pm$. They close under the supercommutator with no constant terms, and their count is $2n^2+5n+1$.
- PBW ordering: $\kappa < [\text{odd}] < [\text{even}]$, with $K=1$ excluded from the basis. Options considered were even-first and height-based ordering; rejected for the reasons in the notation document.
- Generator labels: oscillators `a1p, a1m, bkp, bkm` in the code; `a_1_p, a_1_m, b_k_p, b_k_m` in JSON.

**Even and odd basis lists** (generated by `src/C_generators.py`, `OscAlgebra.basis_pairs()`):
- $C(2)$ ($n=1$): even 4, odd 4, total 8.
  - even: `W(a1p,a1m)`, `W(b1p,b1p)`, `W(b1p,b1m)`, `W(b1m,b1m)`
  - odd: `W(a1p,b1p)`, `W(a1p,b1m)`, `W(a1m,b1p)`, `W(a1m,b1m)`
- $C(3)$ ($n=2$): even 11, odd 8, total 19 (matches the reference table in `Cn1_definition.md`).
- $C(4)$ ($n=3$): even 22, odd 12, total 34 (matches the reference table).

**Ambiguity recorded**: the reference uses Frappat-style generators with constants ($H_{n+1}=-b_n^+b_n^- - \tfrac12$). The Weyl-symmetric basis differs from those by constant shifts on Cartan elements. The assumption is that the shift does not affect triviality, since the shifts are central and absorbed into $K$. This is revisited in I04 and I08.
