## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Decisions and Results

**1. Even and odd basis lists** — defined for all three cases:

| Algebra | n | Even dim | Odd dim | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C(2) = osp(2\|2) | 1 | 4 | 4 | 8 |
| C(3) = osp(2\|4) | 2 | 11 | 8 | 19 |
| C(4) = osp(2\|6) | 3 | 22 | 12 | 34 |

Even generators consist of Cartan elements H_k (k=1,...,n+1) plus root generators for ±2δ_k and ±(δ_i ± δ_j) with i<j. Odd generators are E_eps1_del{k}_pp/pm/mp/mm corresponding to roots ±ε ± δ_k. Full lists recorded in `handover/notation.md` Section 3.

**2. PBW ordering** — Two options considered:

- **Option A (chosen)**: κ < odd < even. Consistent with B(0,n) v5.0 schema convention and standard PBW ordering for oscillator superalgebras where fermionic generators precede bosonic.
- **Option B (rejected)**: κ < even < odd. Sometimes used in physics literature but breaks backward compatibility with B(0,n) schema.

Within odd: group by ε-sign (pp, pm, mp, mm), then by δ-index. Within even: Cartan first, then positive/negative/mixed roots. Detailed ordering in `handover/notation.md` Section 4.

**3. Generator label conventions** — Following B(0,n) patterns:

- Odd: `E_eps1_del{k}_{σ1σ2}` where σ1 = p/m for ε-sign, σ2 = p/m for δ-sign.
- Even: unchanged from B(0,n) (`H_k`, `E_2del{k}_p/m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_{σ1σ2}`).
- Oscillator labels: `a_1_p`, `a_1_m` for standard fermion pair; `b_{k}_p`, `b_{k}_m` for bosonic oscillators.

All conventions documented in `handover/notation.md`.
