## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

2. Determine PBW ordering with ε-roots: list at least two concrete options, choose one, and justify the choice.
3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.
4. Update `handover/notation.md` with the finalized conventions.

### Response

#### Decisions and Results

**1. Even and odd basis lists** — defined for C(2), C(3), C(4) in `handover/notation.md`.

Dimension verification:
- C(2): (4|4)=8, formula: (2·1²+1+1)|(4·1)=(4|4) ✓
- C(3): (11|8)=19, formula: (2·4+2+1)|(4·2)=(11|8) ✓
- C(4): (22|12)=34, formula: (2·9+3+1)|(4·3)=(22|12) ✓

**2. PBW ordering** — Two options considered:
- Option A: Split odd sector by ε-sign (ε+ generators before −ε generators).
- Option B (chosen): Group odd sector by bosonic index k, with sub-order pp/pm/mp/mm within each k.
- Justification: Option B preserves structural parallelism with B(0,n) and simplifies iteration over bosonic indices.

**3. Generator labels** — Odd generators use `E_eps1_del{k}_{ss}` where the two sign characters encode the signs of ε and δ_k respectively (`p`=+, `m`=−). Even generators follow the same convention as B(0,n).

**4. Notation file updated** — `handover/notation.md` now contains all conventions, concrete basis lists, Cartan generator realizations, central elements, and dimension formulas.
