## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Summary
All conventions are written to `handover/notation.md` (sections 1–5). Key decisions:

1. **Basis** (Frappat roots $\pm 2\delta_k$, $\pm\delta_i\pm\delta_j$, $\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_k$, plus $n+1$ Cartans):
   - C(2): 4 | 4, C(3): 11 | 8, C(4): 22 | 12 — the full lists are in notation.md §4 and match
     even $=2n^2+n+1$, odd $=4n$ (verified again programmatically in I03).
2. **PBW ordering** — options considered:
   - (A) `κ < [odd] < [even]`, odd block ordered `pp(k=1..n), pm, mp, mm` (positive odd roots $\varepsilon\pm\delta_k$ first);
   - (B) same outer order, but odd generators interleaved per $k$;
   - (C) triangular `n^- < h < n^+`.
   **Chosen: (A).** Reason: minimal change from the B(0,n) v5.0 convention (`κ < odd < even`, positive-before-negative
   inside the odd block, the even block unchanged except for the extra Cartan `H_{n+1}`). The ordering is a
   presentation choice only; structure constants do not depend on it.
3. **Labels**: `H_1..H_{n+1}`, `E_2del{k}_p/m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp/mm/pm/mp` (B(0,n) labels kept),
   new odd labels `E_eps1_del{k}_{pp,pm,mp,mm}` = $a_1^{\pm}b_k^{\pm}$; oscillators `a_1_p, a_1_m, b_k_p, b_k_m`;
   word order `κ < a_1_p < a_1_m < b_1_p < b_1_m < … < b_n_m`.

#### Ambiguities found in the documents and the assumptions adopted
- **Normalization of $E_{\pm2\delta}$**: `Cn1_definition.md` gives $\tfrac12(b_n^\pm)^2$ for the simple generator but
  $(b_k^\pm)^2$ in the "all root generators" list. Adopted $(b_k^\pm)^2$ uniformly (matches B(0,n) table); this only rescales
  structure constants.
- **Order of oscillators in odd generators** ($b_1^+a_1^-$ vs $a_1^-b_1^+$): adopted fermion-first, since after the
  deformation of I05 the two orders differ by a $\kappa$-term.
- **Cartan constants**: kept $H_{n+1} = -b_n^+b_n^- - \tfrac12$ exactly as given; this is the super-symmetrized form needed
  for closure without $K$ (checked in I03).
