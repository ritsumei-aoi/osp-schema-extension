## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Basis Lists

C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) root system: even roots ±δ_k±δ_l, ±2δ_k; odd roots ±ε±δ_k.

**n=1 (C(2))**: even 4|odd 4
- Even: H_1, H_2, E_2del1_p, E_2del1_m
- Odd: E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm

**n=2 (C(3))**: even 11|odd 8
- Even: H_1,H_2,H_3, E_2del1_p,E_2del2_p,E_del1_del2_pp, E_2del1_m,E_2del2_m,E_del1_del2_mm, E_del1_del2_pm,E_del1_del2_mp
- Odd: E_eps1_del{k}_{pp,pm,mp,mm} for k=1,2

**n=3 (C(4))**: even 22|odd 12
- Even: H_1,...,H_4 + 9 positive even root generators + 9 negative even root generators
- Odd: E_eps1_del{k}_{pp,pm,mp,mm} for k=1,2,3

Dimension formula verification: even=2n²+n+1, odd=4n, total=2n²+5n+1.

#### PBW Ordering Decision

Two options considered:
1. κ < odd < even (matches B(0,n) convention, keeps κ as lowest PBW symbol)
2. odd < even < κ (κ at top — less standard)

**Chosen**: Option 1 (κ < odd < even). Justification: (a) consistent with B(0,n) base case, simplifying extension of existing scripts; (b) κ is nilpotent odd central element — natural to treat as "ground" symbol; (c) within odd, order pp→pm→mp→mm with k=1,...,n; within even, Cartans first then roots by length.

#### Generator Labels

Odd root E_{±ε±δ_k}: label `E_eps1_del{k}_{ss}` where first s ∈ {p,m} = ε-sign, second s = δ_k-sign.
Even root labels: same as B(0,n) (`E_2del{k}_{p/m}`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_{pp/mm/pm/mp}`).
Cartan: H_1,...,H_{n+1} (n+1 generators for so(2)×sp(2n)).

All conventions documented in `handover/notation.md`.
