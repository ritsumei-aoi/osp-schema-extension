## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Decision and results

Defined the C(2), C(3), and C(4) basis lists from the even roots
\(\pm2\delta_k,\pm\delta_i\pm\delta_j\), the even Cartan rank \(n+1\),
and the four odd roots \(\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_k\) for each \(k\).
The labels encode the signs of \(\varepsilon\) and \(\delta_k\) in that
order. For PBW ordering, considered odd-before-even and even-before-odd
blocks (both preceded by \(\kappa\)); chose odd-before-even to preserve the
B(0,n) v5.0 ordering and keep the odd deformation sector contiguous.
The identity \(K=1\) is excluded from the basis; \(\kappa\) denotes the
extension and is not a generator of the undeformed algebra.

#### Verification

Checked basis counts against the root dimensions: C(2) \(3\mid4\),
C(3) \(11\mid8\), and C(4) \(22\mid12\). `handover/notation.md` now
defines the label-to-root and label-to-oscillator mappings, ordering, and
all three explicit bases.
