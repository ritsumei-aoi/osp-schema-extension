## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

#### 1. Basis Definitions for $C(2)$, $C(3)$, and $C(4)$
- **$C(2) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2)$ ($n=1$)**:
  - Even basis (4): `["H_0", "H_1", "E_2del1_p", "E_2del1_m"]`
  - Odd basis (4): `["E_eps1_del1_pp", "E_eps1_del1_pm", "E_eps1_del1_mp", "E_eps1_del1_mm"]`
- **$C(3) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|4)$ ($n=2$)**:
  - Even basis (11): `["H_0", "H_1", "H_2", "E_2del1_p", "E_2del2_p", "E_del1_del2_pp", "E_2del1_m", "E_2del2_m", "E_del1_del2_mm", "E_del1_del2_pm", "E_del1_del2_mp"]`
  - Odd basis (8): `["E_eps1_del1_pp", "E_eps1_del1_pm", "E_eps1_del1_mp", "E_eps1_del1_mm", "E_eps1_del2_pp", "E_eps1_del2_pm", "E_eps1_del2_mp", "E_eps1_del2_mm"]`
- **$C(4) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|6)$ ($n=3$)**:
  - Even basis (22): Cartans (`["H_0", "H_1", "H_2", "H_3"]`), positive roots (`["E_2del1_p", "E_2del2_p", "E_2del3_p", "E_del1_del2_pp", "E_del1_del3_pp", "E_del2_del3_pp"]`), negative roots (`["E_2del1_m", "E_2del2_m", "E_2del3_m", "E_del1_del2_mm", "E_del1_del3_mm", "E_del2_del3_mm"]`), mixed roots (`["E_del1_del2_pm", "E_del1_del3_pm", "E_del2_del3_pm", "E_del1_del2_mp", "E_del1_del3_mp", "E_del2_del3_mp"]`).
  - Odd basis (12): 4 generators for each index $k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.

#### 2. PBW Ordering Decision
- **Option 1 (Parity Block Ordering)**: $\kappa < [\text{odd basis}] < [\text{even basis}]$.
- **Option 2 (Root Weight Lexicographical Ordering)**: Sorted by root weight vectors.
- **Choice**: Selected **Option 1**.
- **Reason**: Option 1 maintains backward compatibility with the $B(0,n)$ v5.0 schema convention (`κ < [odd] < [even]`), ensuring that existing coboundary and Jacobi verification algorithms operate without structure changes.

#### 3. Generator Label Conventions
- Standard fermion pair: `a_1_p` ($a_1^+$), `a_1_m` ($a_1^-$).
- Bosonic oscillators: `b_k_p` ($b_k^+$), `b_k_m` ($b_k^-$).
- Odd generators: `E_eps1_del{k}_{pp|pm|mp|mm}`.
- Even generators: Cartans `H_0` ($\mathfrak{so}(2)$) and `H_k` ($\mathfrak{sp}(2n)$), root generators `E_2del{k}_{p|m}`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_{pp|mm|pm|mp}`.
