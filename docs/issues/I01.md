## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

2. Determine PBW ordering with ε-roots: list at least two concrete options, choose one, and justify the choice.
3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.
4. Update `handover/notation.md` with the finalized conventions.

### Response

#### Proposals and Decisions
1. **Basis Definitions**:
   - For $C(2) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2)$ ($n=1$): Even basis consists of 4 elements (`H_1`, `H_2`, `E_2del1_p`, `E_2del1_m`), Odd basis consists of 4 elements (`E_eps1_del1_pp`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`, `E_eps1_del1_mp`, `E_eps1_del1_mm`). Total dim = 8.
   - For $C(3) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|4)$ ($n=2$): Even basis consists of 11 elements (`H_1`, `H_2`, `H_3`, `E_2del1_p`, `E_2del2_p`, `E_del1_del2_pp`, `E_2del1_m`, `E_2del2_m`, `E_del1_del2_mm`, `E_del1_del2_pm`, `E_del1_del2_mp`), Odd basis consists of 8 elements (`E_eps1_del1_pp`, ..., `E_eps1_del2_mm`). Total dim = 19.
   - For $C(4) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|6)$ ($n=3$): Even basis consists of 22 elements (4 Cartans, 6 $2\delta_k$ roots, 12 $\delta_i \pm \delta_j$ roots), Odd basis consists of 12 elements (`E_eps1_del1_...` through `E_eps1_del3_...`). Total dim = 34.

2. **PBW Ordering Options & Selection**:
   - *Option A*: Parity-grouped PBW ordering ($\kappa < [\text{odd}] < [\text{even}]$). Preserves schema layer separation and compatibility with B(0,n) v5.0 structure.
   - *Option B*: Root-height ordered PBW scheme (interleaving odd and even generators by root height).
   - *Selection*: Option A was chosen to maintain backward compatibility with the existing v5.0 schema structure and simplify matrix calculations for coboundaries.

3. **Generator Label Conventions**:
   - Fermionic oscillators: `a_1_p`, `a_1_m` with CAR $\{a_1^-, a_1^+\} = 1$.
   - Bosonic oscillators: `b_k_p`, `b_k_m` ($k=1, \dots, n$) with CCR $[b_k^-, b_l^+] = \delta_{kl}$.
   - Odd generators: `E_eps1_del{k}_{sign1}{sign2}` for $\pm\varepsilon \pm \delta_k$.
   - Even generators: `H_1` to `H_{n+1}`, `E_2del{k}_p/m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp/mm/pm/mp`.

4. **Deliverables**:
   - Updated `handover/notation.md` with full specifications.
