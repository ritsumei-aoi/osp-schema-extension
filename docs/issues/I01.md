## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

2. Determine PBW ordering with ε-roots: list at least two concrete options, choose one, and justify the choice.
3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.
4. Update `handover/notation.md` with the finalized conventions.

### Response

**Decision summary (2026-10-08, Claude Fable 5.1, no-gate: decided without human approval)**

1. **Basis lists.** Defined in `handover/notation.md` §3 for n=1,2,3 and as a general rule.
   Counts check against `Cn1_definition.md`: (even, odd) = (4,4), (11,8), (22,12).
   Cartan: `H_1..H_{n+1}` exactly as in `Cn1_definition.md` (H_1 = a_1^+a_1^- + b_1^+b_1^-, H_k = N_{k-1} - N_k, H_{n+1} = -N_n - 1/2).
   The constant -1/2 in H_{n+1} is kept because [(b_n^+)^2, (b_n^-)^2] = -4N_n - 2 = 4 H_{n+1}; without it the bracket would not close.
   Consequence recorded for later issues: the scalar 1 (= K) is *not* in the span of the basis
   (Cartan span = span{N_a - 1/2, N_k + 1/2}).

2. **PBW ordering.** Three options were listed (notation.md §4). Adopted: `kappa < [odd] < [even]`
   with odd roots ordered `pp, pm, mp, mm` (by sign pattern, then k). Reason: exact mirror of the
   B(0,n) v5.0 convention, keeps kappa adjacent to the odd sector, and keeps the odd/even split that
   the coboundary parameterization (odd map f) needs. Oscillator-level order:
   `kappa < a_1_p < a_1_m < b_1_p < b_1_m < ... < b_n_p < b_n_m`.

3. **Generator labels.** `E_eps1_del{k}_{st}` with s = sign of eps (which fermion a_1^s), t = sign of
   delta_k (which boson b_k^t); even labels unchanged from B(0,n) (`E_2del{k}_p/m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp/pm/mp/mm`).
   gb labels: `gb_a1{σ}_b{j}{s}`.

4. **Ambiguity resolved (recorded per ai_trust_policy).** `Cn1_definition.md` gives E_{2δ_n} = ½(b_n^+)^2
   among simple generators but (b_k^±)^2 among all root generators. Adopted coefficient 1 for every root
   vector (same as the B(0,n) JSON). Only structure-constant normalizations change (e.g. [E_2del{n}_p, E_2del{n}_m] = 4 H_{n+1}).

**Verification.** The basis/label rules were implemented in `src/C_generators.py` (committed with I03) and
the dimension assertions `len(even) == 2n^2+n+1`, `len(odd) == 4n` hold for n = 1, 2, 3. A quick
oscillator computation confirmed closure of the chosen Cartan span: {E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_mm} =
N_1 + 1 - N_a = -H_1 - 2H_2 (n=1), i.e. no K component appears in the undeformed brackets.
