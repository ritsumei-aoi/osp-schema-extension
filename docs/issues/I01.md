## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

### Response

Updated `handover/notation.md` with the even/odd root sets, explicit positive-root
lists for C(2), C(3), and C(4), inclusion of negative roots and the Cartan
basis, PBW order, and oscillator generator labels.

For the odd-root order, I considered grouping by the sign of $\varepsilon$
(all $\varepsilon-\delta_i$ before all $\varepsilon+\delta_i$) and grouping by
index (the two roots for each $i$ adjacent). I chose sign-grouping because the
two root families remain contiguous as $n$ varies and map directly to generator
families. The documented PBW order places negative roots, Cartan generators,
and positive roots in successive blocks, with explicit within-block orders.

Verification: the listed positive even-root counts for $n=1,2,3$ are 1, 4,
and 9; positive odd-root counts are 2, 4, and 6. Including their negatives and
the $n+1$ Cartan generators gives even/odd dimensions $4|4$, $11|8$, and
$22|12$, matching `docs/math/Cn1_definition.md`. The issue requirements and
all three completion criteria are satisfied.
