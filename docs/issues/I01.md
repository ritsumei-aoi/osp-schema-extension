## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.

### Response

**Decision and rationale:** I considered (1) preserving the B(0,n) schema's odd-first/even-second blocks and (2) interleaving generators in a root-triangular order (negative roots, Cartan, positive roots). I chose (1) for schema compatibility and documented deterministic ordering within each block. A homogeneous basis can use either total order for PBW monomials. Root signs are encoded with the suffix order `pp`, `pm`, `mp`, `mm`; odd-root labels place the $\varepsilon$ sign first.

**Results:** Updated `handover/notation.md` with the even and odd roots, explicit basis lists for C(2), C(3), and C(4), their dimensions, oscillator labels and relations, Cartan/generator realizations, and PBW conventions. The C(n+1) algebra basis excludes extension symbols `K` and `κ`.

**Verification:** The listed basis counts are C(2) $4|4$, C(3) $11|8$, and C(4) $22|12$, agreeing with $2n^2+n+1|4n$. The labels cover every even root pair and all four odd-root sign combinations for each $i$.
