## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### 1. Root Systems for C(n+1) = osp(2|2n)

From Frappat et al., the root system is Δ = {±δ_k ± δ_l, ±2δ_k, ±ε ± δ_k} (1 ≤ k < l ≤ n).

**Even roots**: {±2δ_k} ∪ {±(δ_i ± δ_j) | i < j}
**Odd roots**: {±ε ± δ_k | k = 1, ..., n}

The even subalgebra is SO(2) × Sp(2n) with dimension 2n²+n+1.

##### C(2) = osp(2|2) (n=1)
Even dimension: 2(1)²+1+1 = 4
Odd dimension: 4(1) = 4
Total: 8

**Even basis**: H_1, H_2, E_{2δ_1}, E_{-2δ_1}
**Odd basis**: E_{ε+δ_1}, E_{ε-δ_1}, E_{-ε+δ_1}, E_{-ε-δ_1}

##### C(3) = osp(2|4) (n=2)
Even dimension: 2(2)²+2+1 = 11
Odd dimension: 4(2) = 8
Total: 19

**Even basis**: 
- Cartan: H_1, H_2, H_3
- Positive: E_{2δ_1}, E_{δ_1-δ_2}, E_{δ_1+δ_2}, E_{2δ_2}
- Negative: E_{-2δ_1}, E_{-δ_1+δ_2}, E_{-(δ_1+δ_2)}, E_{-2δ_2}

**Odd basis**: E_{ε±δ_1}, E_{ε±δ_2}, E_{-ε±δ_1}, E_{-ε±δ_2} (8 generators)

##### C(4) = osp(2|6) (n=3)
Even dimension: 2(3)²+3+1 = 22
Odd dimension: 4(3) = 12
Total: 34

**Even basis**:
- Cartan: H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4
- Positive even roots (Sp(6)): {2δ_1, 2δ_2, 2δ_3, δ_1-δ_2, δ_1+δ_2, δ_2-δ_3, δ_2+δ_3, δ_1-δ_3, δ_1+δ_3} (9 generators)
- Negative: (9 generators)

**Odd basis**: E_{ε±δ_i}, E_{-ε±δ_i} for i=1,2,3 (12 generators)

#### 2. PBW Ordering with ε-roots: Two Options

**Option A (Fermionic-First):** Prioritize fermionic roots before bosonic roots within each sign sector.
Order: κ < Cartan < [ε+δ_1, ..., ε+δ_n, ε-δ_1, ..., ε-δ_n] < [2δ_1, δ_1-δ_2, δ_1+δ_2, 2δ_2, ...] < [negative roots reversed]

**Option B (Bosonic-First):** Prioritize bosonic roots before fermionic roots, following standard Sp(2n) ordering.
Order: κ < Cartan < [2δ_1, δ_1-δ_2, δ_1+δ_2, 2δ_2, ...] < [ε+δ_1, ..., ε-δ_n] < [negative roots reversed]

**Choice: Option A (Fermionic-First)**

**Justification**: 
- C(n+1) is fundamentally distinguished from B(0,n) by its standard fermionic pair (a_1^+, a_1^-) replacing the supplementary fermion a_0.
- Placing ε-roots before δ-roots highlights this distinguishing feature.
- The fermionic structure is "outer" (acts on a distinct Fock space) and should have precedence in the ordering.
- This is consistent with oscillator realization where fermionic operators are fundamental.

#### 3. Oscillator Generator Labels

Extending the B(0,n) convention, using a_1_p and a_1_m for a_1^± and following the subscript pattern:

**Even roots** (same as B(0,n)):
- 2δ_k: E_2del{k}_p (positive), E_2del{k}_m (negative)
- δ_i ± δ_j: E_del{i}_del{j}_pp, E_del{i}_del{j}_pm, E_del{i}_del{j}_mp, E_del{i}_del{j}_mm

**Odd roots** (new for C(n+1)):
Using four subscripts to denote (ε sign, δ sign):
- ε+δ_k: E_eps_del{k}_pp
- ε-δ_k: E_eps_del{k}_pm
- -ε+δ_k: E_eps_del{k}_mp
- -ε-δ_k: E_eps_del{k}_mm
