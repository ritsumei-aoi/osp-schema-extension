## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

2. Determine PBW ordering with ε-roots: list at least two concrete options, choose one, and justify the choice.
3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.

#### Decisions and Results

1. **Even and odd basis lists**: Defined for C(2) (4|4=8), C(3) (11|8=19), and C(4) (22|12=34). Dimensions verified against the formula: even = $2n^2+n+1$, odd = $4n$, total = $2n^2+5n+1$.

2. **PBW ordering**: Two options considered:
   - **Option A (chosen)**: κ < [odd] < [even]. Consistent with B(0,n) convention and places the nilpotent central element first, simplifying graded product computations.
   - **Option B (rejected)**: κ < [even] < [odd]. Breaks compatibility with the established B(0,n) conventions.

3. **Generator labels**: Following the B(0,n) pattern, odd generators use `E_eps1_del{k}_{ss}` where `ss ∈ {pp, pm, mp, mm}` encodes the signs of the ε and δ components. Even generators retain the same labels as B(0,n) (`H_k`, `E_2del{k}_p/m`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_{ss}`). An additional Cartan generator `H_1 = a_1^+ a_1^- + b_1^+ b_1^-` arises from the so(2) factor.

4. **Deliverable**: `handover/notation.md` updated with all conventions, basis lists, PBW ordering, generator labels, and dimension formulas.
