## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

**Decision log (No-Gate condition):**

#### 1. Even and Odd Basis Lists

The root system of C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) has:
- Even roots: $\pm 2\delta_k$ and $\pm(\delta_i \pm \delta_j)$ for $i < j$, plus $n+1$ Cartan elements.
- Odd roots: $\pm\varepsilon \pm \delta_k$ for $k = 1,\ldots,n$ (total $4n$ generators).

Dimension formula: even = $2n^2+n+1$, odd = $4n$, total = $2n^2+5n+1$.

| Case | n | even dim | odd dim | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C(2) | 1 | 4 | 4 | 8 |
| C(3) | 2 | 11 | 8 | 19 |
| C(4) | 3 | 22 | 12 | 34 |

Full basis lists are recorded in `handover/notation.md` §5.

#### 2. PBW Ordering Decision

Two options were considered:
- **Option A** (chosen): Graded by parity — `κ < [odd] < [even]` — consistent with the B(0,n) PBW convention.
- **Option B**: Triangular decomposition — positive roots < Cartan < negative roots.

**Rationale**: Option A directly extends B(0,n) and maximizes schema backward compatibility.

#### 3. Generator Label Convention

Odd generators use the prefix `E_eps1_del{k}_{sign1}{sign2}` where:
- `eps1` = the ε direction (the fermionic oscillator pair index)
- `sign1` = sign of ε: p (+) or m (-)
- `sign2` = sign of δ_k: p (+) or m (-)

Example: `E_eps1_del2_pm` = $E_{\varepsilon - \delta_2} = a_1^+ b_2^-$.

All conventions are recorded in `handover/notation.md`.
