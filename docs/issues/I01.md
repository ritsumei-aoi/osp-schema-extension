## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

#### Summary
`handover/notation.md` now documents oscillators, generator labels, the even/odd basis lists for
C(2), C(3), C(4), the PBW ordering, and the bracket/deformation/coboundary conventions used by later issues.

#### Basis (counts checked against `Cn1_definition.md`)
| n | algebra | even (2n²+n+1) | odd (4n) | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C(2)=osp(2\|2) | 4 | 4 | 8 |
| 2 | C(3)=osp(2\|4) | 11 | 8 | 19 |
| 3 | C(4)=osp(2\|6) | 22 | 12 | 34 |

Even = (n+1) Cartans H_1..H_{n+1} + 2n long roots ±2δ_k + 4·C(n,2) roots ±δ_i±δ_j.
Odd = 4n roots ±ε±δ_k, realized as a_1^σ b_k^s.

#### PBW ordering — options considered
1. **(A, adopted)** κ < [odd] < [even], same as B(0,n) v5.0. Odd block ordered by sign pattern
   (pp, pm, mp, mm) then by k — the direct analogue of B(0,n)'s `E_del{k}_p` then `E_del{k}_m`.
2. (B) Root-height ordering: positive roots < Cartan < negative roots (triangular-decomposition PBW).
3. (C) Odd block ordered by k first (`E_eps1_del1_pp, _pm, _mp, _mm, E_eps1_del2_pp, ...`).

Reason for (A): it keeps backward compatibility with the B(0,n) v5.0 schema (κ < odd < even, K excluded),
so any tooling indexing "odd first" works unchanged; grouping by sign pattern keeps all a_1^+ generators before
a_1^- generators, which matches the oscillator normal order `a_1_p < a_1_m < b_...`; every odd generator word is
therefore already normal ordered. (B) is natural for Verma-module work but not needed here; (C) has no advantage.

#### Labels / ambiguities recorded
- Labels: `E_eps1_del{k}_{σs}` with σ = sign of a_1, s = sign of b_k; `eps1` leaves room for a future m>1 (`eps2`, ...).
- Ambiguity 1: `Cn1_definition.md` gives E_{2δ_n} = ½(b_n^+)² among the simple generators but (b_k^±)² in the
  "all root generators" list. **Adopted** (b_k^±)², consistent with the B(0,n) label table; this only rescales two
  basis vectors.
- Ambiguity 2: H_{n+1} = -b_n^+b_n^- - ½ and H_1 = a_1^+a_1^- + b_1^+b_1^- carry constants. Adopted as given; their
  span equals span{a_1^+a_1^- - ½, b_k^+b_k^- + ½}, which is exactly what makes the span of generators close
  without K (verified in I03: no K component appears in any undeformed bracket).
