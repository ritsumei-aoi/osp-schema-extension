## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

**1. Basis Lists for C(2), C(3), C(4)**
- **C(2) (n=1)**
  - **Even (4):** Cartans: $H_1, H_2$; Roots: $E_{2\delta_1}, E_{-2\delta_1}$
  - **Odd (4):** $E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}, E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1}, E_{-\varepsilon+\delta_1}, E_{-\varepsilon-\delta_1}$
- **C(3) (n=2)**
  - **Even (11):** Cartans: $H_1, H_2, H_3$; Roots: $E_{\pm 2\delta_1}, E_{\pm 2\delta_2}, E_{\pm\delta_1\pm\delta_2}$
  - **Odd (8):** $E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_1}, E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_2}$
- **C(4) (n=3)**
  - **Even (22):** Cartans: $H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4$; Roots: $E_{\pm 2\delta_1}, E_{\pm 2\delta_2}, E_{\pm 2\delta_3}, E_{\pm\delta_1\pm\delta_2}, E_{\pm\delta_1\pm\delta_3}, E_{\pm\delta_2\pm\delta_3}$
  - **Odd (12):** $E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_1}, E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_2}, E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_3}$

**2. PBW Ordering Options and Decision**
- **Option 1 (Root Lexicographical with $\varepsilon > \delta_1$):**
  Orders roots such that $\varepsilon > \delta_1 > \dots > \delta_n > 0$. The simple root system naturally incorporates $\varepsilon - \delta_1$, meaning positive roots are those with positive coefficients in this ordering. PBW is Negative Roots < Cartans < Positive Roots.
- **Option 2 (Root Lexicographical with $\varepsilon$ smallest):**
  Orders roots such that $\delta_1 > \dots > \delta_n > \varepsilon > 0$.
- **Decision:** I have chosen **Option 1**, because it naturally aligns with the standard simple root system for $C(n+1)$ as defined in Frappat (where $\varepsilon - \delta_1$ is a simple root).

**3. Generator Labels**
Generator labels have been mapped to strings like `E_eps_del{k}_pm` for $E_{\varepsilon - \delta_k}$, in consistency with the $B(0,n)$ schema. These have been recorded in `handover/notation.md`.
