## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

#### 1. Oscillator Realization and Generator Labels
- **Standard Fermionic Pair**: `a_1_p` ($a_1^+$) and `a_1_m` ($a_1^-$) with CAR $\{a_1^-, a_1^+\} = 1$, parity 1.
- **Bosonic Oscillators**: `b_k_p` ($b_k^+$) and `b_k_m` ($b_k^-$) ($k = 1, \ldots, n$) with CCR $[b_j^-, b_k^+] = \delta_{jk}$, parity 0.
- **Central Elements**: `kappa` (odd, $p(\kappa)=1$, $\kappa^2=0$) and `K` (even, $p(K)=0$, central unit scalar).

#### 2. PBW Ordering Options & Decision
- **Option A (Lexicographical order)**: Sort generator names alphabetically. Evaluated and rejected because it destroys root-system structure (e.g. separating Cartan generators and grouping $2\delta_k$ arbitrarily).
- **Option B (Root-system structured extension of $B(0,n)$ v5.0)**: Order $\kappa < [\text{odd}] < [\text{even}]$.
  - `odd`: Grouped by $k=1, \ldots, n$ and sign combinations `pp` ($+\varepsilon+\delta_k$), `pm` ($+\varepsilon-\delta_k$), `mp` ($-\varepsilon+\delta_k$), `mm` ($-\varepsilon-\delta_k$).
  - `even`: Cartan generators `H_1` ... `H_{n+1}`, followed by positive $2\delta$ roots, positive mixed $\delta$ roots, negative $2\delta$ roots, negative mixed $\delta$ roots, and mixed-sign $\delta$ roots.
- **Decision**: Option B is selected to preserve consistency with the $B(0,n)$ v5.0 schema convention.
