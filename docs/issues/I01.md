## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

Defined the even roots with the inherited B(0,n) labels and the odd roots with
`E_eps1_del{k}_{pp,pm,mp,mm}`, where the suffix records the signs of
$\varepsilon$ and $\delta_k$ in that order. Listed the full C(2), C(3), and
C(4) bases in `handover/notation.md`; their dimensions agree with the formulas
$(2n^2+n+1)|4n$ (4|4, 11|8, and 22|12).

Considered (1) the existing parity-block order, `κ < odd < even`, and (2) a
root-height order with positive roots, Cartan, and negative roots. Selected
the parity-block order because it preserves the established B(0,n) convention
and makes the schema extension directly comparable. The specified within-block
orders make this a complete total order; `K=1` is excluded from the basis.

Verification: counted the even basis entries as $n+1+2n+4\binom n2 =
2n^2+n+1$ and the odd entries as $4n$ for n=1,2,3. Confirmed the labels and
oscillator names against `docs/math/Cn1_definition.md`. All three completion
criteria are checked above.
