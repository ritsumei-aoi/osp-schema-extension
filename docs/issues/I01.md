## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

## Requirements

2. Determine PBW ordering with ε-roots: list at least two concrete options, choose one, and justify the choice.
3. Design oscillator generator labels consistent with `handover/notation.md`.

### Response

**Decisions**
- Basis: n+1 Cartan elements H_1..H_{n+1} (symmetric ordering, so no constant/central term appears in brackets), 2n² even roots, and 4n odd `E_eps1_del{k}_{στ}`. Counts (4|4), (11|8), (22|12) match `Cn1_definition.md`; re-verified by tests in I03.
- PBW options considered: A (κ<odd<even, as in B(0,n) v5.0), B (κ<even<odd), C (root-height). Chose A for compatibility with the v5.0 schema and because it needs no choice of positive ε-system. Full conventions in `handover/notation.md`.
- Ambiguity recorded: `Cn1_definition.md` gives E_{±2δ_n}=½(b_n^±)² among the simple generators but (b_k^±)² in the general list. Adopted (b_k^±)² uniformly (one label rule; only rescales structure constants).
