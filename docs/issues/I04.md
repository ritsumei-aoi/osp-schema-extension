### Response

**Script**: `src/verify_C_structure.py`

**Root cause of initial failures (now fixed)**:
1. A bug in `_normal_order`: `_is_zero_monomial` was applied to un-normalized monomials. E.g., `(a1p, a1m, a1p, b1p)` has two a1p's but is NOT zero — it reduces via CAR to `(a1p, b1p)`. The fix: only apply the zero condition when identical fermions are ADJACENT and already in normal order (ia==ib case in the swap loop).
2. A bug in `build_bracket_table` in the verifier: anti-symmetric fill double-counted by processing both (X,Y) and (Y,X). Fixed by snapshotting original entries before filling.
