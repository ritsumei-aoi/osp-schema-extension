#### 1. Verification Methodology
The verification script was implemented in `src/verify_C_structure.py` and wrapped as an automated pytest in `tests/test_verify_C_structure.py`.
The verification loads `data/C_n_structure.json` ($n=1, 2, 3$) and evaluates:

1. **Graded Anti-Symmetry**:
   For every pair of basis generators $(X, Y)$:
   $$[X, Y] + (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} [Y, X] = 0.$$
2. **Super Jacobi Identity**:
   For every triple of basis generators $(X, Y, Z)$:
   $$(-1)^{p(X)p(Z)} [X, [Y, Z]] + (-1)^{p(Y)p(X)} [Y, [Z, X]] + (-1)^{p(Z)p(Y)} [Z, [X, Y]] = 0.$$
