Root cause of earlier failures (during I03 development): the `zero_nilpotent` check
in `normal_order_product` was incorrectly placed before bubble-sort swaps, causing
monomials like ["a_1_p","a_1_m","a_1_p","b_1_p"] to be zeroed before generating the
fermionic contraction term. This underestimated Cartan eigenvalues by a factor (e.g.,
[H_1, E_pp] gave 1 instead of 2), producing Jacobi residuals of the form 2*E_pp.
Fixed in I03 final commit.
