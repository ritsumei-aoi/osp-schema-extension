#### Root Cause Analysis of Bugs Found During Verification

During verification, two bugs in `C_generators.py` were found and fixed:

**Bug 1: N_bk formula** — The formula `N_bk = -(H_{k+1}+...+H_{n+1}) - 1/2` was incorrect. The correct formula derived from `H_l = N_b{l-1} - N_bl` and `H_{n+1} = -N_bn - 1/2` is:
`N_bk = H_{k+1} + ... + H_n - H_{n+1} - 1/2`.
The old formula had all H coefficients as -1; the correct formula has H_{k+1},...,H_n with +1 and only H_{n+1} with -1. This bug was invisible for n=1 (where H_n = H_1 and H_{n+1} = H_2 coincide), explaining why n=1 passed initially.

**Bug 2: Double-storing of brackets** — The even-even and odd-odd loops iterated over all ordered pairs (X,Y) and (Y,X), causing the checker (which infers [Y,X} from [X,Y} via anti-symmetry) to double-count. Fixed by only iterating over pairs with basis index(Y) >= basis index(X).

After both fixes: all n=1,2,3 pass anti-symmetry and Super Jacobi checks.
