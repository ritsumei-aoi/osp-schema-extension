- **Graded Anti-Symmetry**:

  $[X, [Y, Z\}\} = [[X, Y\}, Z\} + (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} [Y, [X, Z\}\}$ for all triples $(X, Y, Z) \in \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g}$.
