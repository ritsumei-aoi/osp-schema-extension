#### Decisions and Results

1. **Verification script**: Implemented `src/verify_C_structure.py` with two checks:
   - **Anti-symmetry**: Verifies $[X, Y\} = -(-1)^{p_X p_Y} [Y, X\}$ for all generator pairs.
   - **Super Jacobi identity**: Verifies $(-1)^{p_X p_Z}[X,[Y,Z\}\} + \text{cyclic} = 0$ for all generator triples.

2. **Bug found and fixed**: Initial verification revealed Super Jacobi failures due to a bug in the `decompose_in_basis` function in `C_generators.py`. The Cartan generators H_k share oscillator words (b_k^+ b_k^-) across multiple generators, and the original greedy matching algorithm incorrectly attributed bracket results. Fixed by implementing a forward-sweep Gaussian elimination exploiting the tridiagonal structure of the Cartan subsystem. JSON files were regenerated after the fix.

   - C(2) n=1: Anti-symmetry PASS, Super Jacobi PASS (120 triples)
