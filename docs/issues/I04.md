1. **Graded anti-symmetry**:

   $$(-1)^{p(X)p(Z)} [X, [Y, Z\}] + (-1)^{p(Y)p(X)} [Y, [Z, X\}] + (-1)^{p(Z)p(Y)} [Z, [X, Y\}] = 0$$
   tested for all triples $(X, Y, Z) \in \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g}$.
