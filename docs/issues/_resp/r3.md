**Decisions**

Brackets are computed as supercommutators of the oscillator realizations and decomposed into the basis (roots by their unique monomial, Cartans by an exact (n+2)x(n+2) inverse including a K column).
- Options considered: (a) hard-code the Frappat commutation table, (b) compute brackets from oscillator realizations.
