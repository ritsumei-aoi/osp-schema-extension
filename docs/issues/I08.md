### Response

## Triviality comparison

Implemented `src/C_triviality.py` to compare the evaluated Layer 3 coefficients
against the generic odd-map coboundary space in Layer 4. It checks that the
bases agree, that Layer 4 has no `K` output (as required because `K=1` is not
an independent member of $\mathfrak g$), and that each of the `4n` parameters
has an isolated central-component witness.

| Algebra | Parameters | Evaluated gamma terms | Nonzero all-plus `K` components | Independent `K` witnesses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C(2) | 4 | 60 | 10 | 4 |
| C(3) | 8 | 334 | 42 | 8 |
| C(4) | 12 | 932 | 90 | 12 |

For every tested rank and each $1\leq j\leq n$, the gamma data contains these
isolated components:

| Generator pair $(X,Y)$ | Central component of $\gamma(X,Y)$ |
|---|---|
| (`E_2del{j}_p`, `E_eps1_del{j}_pm`) | $+\tfrac12\,\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^+}K$ |
| (`E_2del{j}_m`, `E_eps1_del{j}_pp`) | $-\tfrac12\,\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^-}K$ |
| (`E_2del{j}_p`, `E_eps1_del{j}_mm`) | $+\tfrac12\,\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^+}K$ |
| (`E_2del{j}_m`, `E_eps1_del{j}_mp`) | $-\tfrac12\,\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^-}K$ |

At the representative all-`+1` assignment, C(2) therefore has, for example,
$[E_{2\delta_1},E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1}]$, a $+\tfrac12K$ gamma component;
the corresponding Layer 4 coboundary has no `K` component for any choice of
$f$. The other three C(2) pairs give $-\tfrac12K,+\tfrac12K,-\tfrac12K$.
The same four witnesses occur independently for every bosonic index at ranks
$n=2,3$. Thus all three all-plus evaluated deformations are non-trivial.

The root cause is the scalar shift in the terminal Cartan realization:
$H_{n+1}=-b_n^+b_n^- -\tfrac12$. More generally, for each $j$,
$$
b_j^+b_j^-=\sum_{k=j+1}^{n}H_k-H_{n+1}-\tfrac12K.
$$
The mixed-oscillator correction produces number-operator terms; expressing
them in the Cartan basis leaves the displayed central identity component.
Because the odd-map coboundary takes values in the Layer 1 basis, which
excludes `K`, it cannot remove any of these isolated components.

## Triviality condition conjecture

Under the literal formal exchange-rule convention used to generate Schema 2,
the evidence supports: **the deformation is trivial if and only if every
$\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^\sigma,b_j^s}$ is zero.** Necessity follows from the four
isolated `K` witnesses for each $j$: each is a nonzero constant multiple of
one distinct parameter and no Layer 4 coboundary can contain `K`. Sufficiency
is immediate at the zero assignment, where $\gamma=0$ and $f=0$ is a
coboundary. This pattern is checked for n=1,2,3; its extension to all n is a
conjecture based on the rank-independent root-pair pattern and Cartan identity.

The source parity caveat from I05 remains material: it assigns odd parity to
both `gb` and `kappa`, although their product appears in a mixed commutator
between an even and an odd oscillator. The result above is conditional on the
literal formal coefficient computation and does not resolve that underlying
parity inconsistency.

witnesses and non-trivial all-plus profiles for C(2)/C(3)/C(4);
`python -m pytest -q` -> `12 passed in 2.51s`. Completion criteria are checked
above.
