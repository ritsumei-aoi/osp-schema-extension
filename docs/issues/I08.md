### 0. Setting and the three readings of "trivial"

`[X,Y]_γ = [X,Y]_0 + κ(γ_g(X,Y) + γ_K(X,Y)·K)` with a non-zero constant part `γ_K`
(K = even central element = 1). Because the handover definition `γ: g⊗g → g` and

| reading | f | γ compared | meaning |

| **R2 projected** | f: g → g | γ_g only (K-components discarded) | "γ: g⊗g → g" by projection |
| **R3 extended** | f: g → g ⊕ CK | full γ | deformation of the centrally extended algebra g̃ = g ⊕ CK |

### 2. Rigorous analysis of the discrepancies

**Why the g-components always match.** The deformed oscillator relation
`[b_j^t, a_1^s] = −gb_{s,j,t} κ` is removed by the change of variables
`a_1^s ↦ a_1^s + Σ d_{s,j,t} b_j^t κ` (d_{s,j,+} = gb_{s,j,−}, d_{s,j,−} = −gb_{s,j,+});
CAR/CCR are unchanged because κ is central and κ² = 0. A basis element becomes
X + κ·l(X) with l(X) quadratic in the oscillators, i.e. l(X) ∈ g ⊕ CK, and comparing
brackets gives γ = −δl (sign convention of `C_coboundary_definition.md`; verified exactly in I07
for all n and 5 profiles). Hence γ is a coboundary of f_rep = −l: g → g ⊕ CK, and its
g-part γ_g = δ(f_rep)_g is a coboundary of a map into g.

**Why the K-components can never match under R1.** For any f: g → g,
`(δf)(X,Y) = ±[X,f(Y)] ∓ [Y,f(X)] − f([X,Y]) ∈ g`, so `(δf)_K ≡ 0`. Therefore
`δf = γ` is solvable with f: g → g **iff γ_K ≡ 0**. The script verified on all d² ordered
pairs (n = 1, 2, 3) the closed formula
$$\gamma_K(X,Y) = -f_K([X,Y]),\qquad f_K(E_{\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_k}) = \tfrac12\,\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^{\pm},b_k^{\pm}},\quad f_K(\text{even}) = 0,$$
i.e. γ_K is the pull-back of the bracket by the linear functional λ_gb: g_1 → C,
λ_gb(E_eps1_del{k}_{st}) = ½ gb_a1{s}_b{k}{t}. Since g = C(n+1) is simple, hence perfect
(checked: dim [g,g] = dim g = 8, 19, 34), every odd generator is a bracket of two basis elements,
so γ_K ≡ 0 ⇔ λ_gb = 0 ⇔ gb = 0. This also explains the counts: the failing pairs for a
profile are exactly the (X,Y) with [X,Y] having a component along an odd generator whose gb
parameter is non-zero.

**Origin of the constants.** γ_K arises from normal ordering `b_k^+ b_k^- = N_k^s − ½K`
and `a_1^+a_1^- = N_0^s + ½K` inside the κ-terms; the Frappat Cartan elements absorb such
constants in the undeformed brackets but not in the deformation terms, where single oscillator
pairs such as `b_k^+ b_k^-` appear unsymmetrized (I05 example `[a_1^+b_1^-, b_1^+b_1^-]`).
The embedding g ⊂ U(A) is unique (g perfect ⇒ no K-shift of the embedding is a subalgebra), so
γ_K is intrinsic, not an artefact of the basis.

### 3. General conjecture (with proof sketch)

Let g = C(n+1), gb ∈ C^{2×2n}, and γ_gb the cocycle of the inhomogeneous deformation.

**Conjecture / Theorem (verified exactly for n = 1, 2, 3 and proved for all n by the
argument above).**
1. *(Extended, R3)* For **every** gb the deformation is trivial as a deformation of
   g̃ = g ⊕ CK: γ_gb = δf_rep with the explicit odd map
   f_rep = −l (supported on the odd generators and H_1; f_rep(odd) ∈ sp(2n) ⊕ CK,
   f_rep(H_1) ∈ g_1, f_rep = 0 on the other even generators).
2. *(Projected, R2)* For every gb, γ_g = δ(f_rep)_g with f: g → g; the g-part is always a
   coboundary.
3. *(Strict, R1)* The full cocycle γ_gb is a coboundary of a map f: g → g
   **if and only if gb = 0**: the obstruction is the linear functional
   λ_gb = ½·gb on g_1, through γ_K(X,Y) = −λ_gb([X,Y]); for gb ≠ 0 the deformation is
   non-trivial. In particular the set of trivial gb is a linear subspace (dimension 0 for R1,
   4n for R2/R3); no sign pattern, row/column structure or relation among the 4n parameters is
   special — the joint symbolic solve in (φ, gb) returned exactly the 4n constraints
   `gb_a1{s}_b{j}{t} = 0` for R1 and no constraints for R2/R3, for n = 1, 2, 3.
4. Uniqueness: the odd cocycles h with δh = 0, h: g → g ⊕ CK, have h_K = 0 (h_K vanishes on
   [g,g] = g), so f_K = ½·gb is the same for every solution f of δf = γ; the K-part of f is
   therefore an invariant of the deformation, equal to ½ gb.

Interpretation: the inhomogeneous deformation of C(n+1) defined by `[b, a_1] = −gb κ` is
"trivial modulo the centre": it can be absorbed into a redefinition of the generators
provided the redefinition is allowed to shift odd generators by multiples of κK = κ.
Within g alone it is non-trivial for every non-zero gb, and the invariant that detects this is
the vector gb itself (as a functional on the odd root vectors).
