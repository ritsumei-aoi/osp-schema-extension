### 2. Rigorous Analysis of Discrepancies

#### Obstruction Examples (C(2), n=1)
- `[E_eps1_del1_mp, H_1] → E_2del1_p`: residual = 6
- `[E_eps1_del1_mp, H_1] → H_1`: residual = -2
- `[E_eps1_del1_mp, H_2] → E_2del1_p`: residual = 2

The obstructions concentrate on brackets involving odd generators (ε-roots) with Cartan elements. This is structurally significant: in C(n+1), the Cartan subalgebra includes H_1 = a_1^+a_1^- + b_1^+b_1^- which involves the fermionic number operator a_1^+a_1^-. The deformation modifies the mixed commutators [b_j^s, a_1^σ], which directly affects how H_1 acts on odd generators. The coboundary operator δf cannot reproduce this specific pattern because f is constrained by the undeformed bracket structure.

#### Mathematical Root Cause
The coboundary image has dimension equal to the rank of the coboundary matrix:
- C(2): rank 28 out of 32 φ parameters (4 null directions)
- C(3): rank 168 out of 176 (8 null)
- C(4): rank 516 out of 528 (12 null)

The null space dimension of δ equals 4n in each case, matching exactly the number of gb parameters. However, the gamma image (also 4n-dimensional) does NOT intersect the coboundary image except at the origin.

### 3. Symbolic Analysis: gb Conditions for Triviality

#### Method
Analyzed the combined system [A_γ | -A_δ] · [gb; φ] = 0 with symbolic gb and φ parameters.

#### Results

| Algebra | gb params | φ params | Combined rank | Null dim | gb free | gb pivot |
|---------|----------|---------|---------------|----------|---------|----------|
| C(2) | 4 | 32 | 32 | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| C(3) | 8 | 176 | 176 | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| C(4) | 12 | 528 | 528 | 12 | 0 | 12 |

**Key finding**: All gb parameters appear as pivot variables (none are free). The null space dimension equals the number of gb parameters, but all free directions lie entirely in the φ subspace. This means:

**The ONLY gb assignment making γ trivial is gb = 0 (the undeformed case).**

### 4. General Conjecture

**Conjecture**: For all n ≥ 1, the inhomogeneous deformation of C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) parameterized by the gb matrix is **non-trivial for every non-zero gb assignment**. That is, the deformation 2-cocycle γ is never a coboundary.

**Supporting evidence**:
1. Verified computationally for n = 1, 2, 3
2. The structural pattern is uniform: combined rank = number of φ parameters, gb free count = 0
3. The null space dimension equals 4n (= number of gb parameters) in each case, suggesting the pattern is dimension-independent

**Contrast with B(0,n)**: For B(0,n) = osp(1|2n), the deformation IS trivial (γ = δf is solvable). The key structural difference is:
- B(0,n) uses a supplementary fermion a_0 with a_0² = 1/2 (not a standard CAR pair)
- C(n+1) uses a standard fermionic pair a_1^± with {a_1^-, a_1^+} = 1

The standard CAR pair in C(n+1) creates an obstruction that the supplementary fermion in B(0,n) does not: the deformation interacts with both a_1^+ and a_1^- independently, doubling the deformation space (4n vs 2n parameters) while the coboundary space cannot accommodate this larger structure. The extra so(2) factor in the even subalgebra of C(n+1) (absent in B(0,n)) further constrains the coboundary image, making triviality impossible.

**Physical interpretation**: The non-triviality of the deformation for C(n+1) means that the inhomogeneous deformation represents a genuine cohomological obstruction — it cannot be removed by a change of basis. This contrasts with B(0,n) where the deformation is merely a coordinate artifact.
