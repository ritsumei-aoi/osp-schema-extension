## Triviality Search Report for C(n+1)

### Method

`src/C_triviality.py` loads the Schema 2 γ coefficients (linear in the 4n
gb parameters) and the Schema 4 (δf) coefficients (linear in the φ matrix
elements of the fully general parity-reversing map f: g → g).  For every
basis triple (X, Y, Z) it builds two coefficient rows: Γ_row (linear in gb)
and Δ_row (linear in φ). Triviality of the deformation on the whole gb
parameter space is equivalent to

    column-space(Γ)  ⊆  column-space(Δ),

by Rouché-Capelli. The implementation forms Δ and Γ as sympy rational
matrices and tests this via `rank(Δ) == rank([Δ | Γ])`. Columns of Γ that
fail to lie in col(Δ) correspond to obstructed gb directions.

### Case-specific Evidence

Computed from `python3 -m src.C_triviality`:

| n | n_gb | n_φ | n_eqs | rank(Δ) | rank([Δ|Γ]) | universally trivial? | obstructed gb's |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (C(2)) | 4   | 32  | 212  | 28  | 32  | **No** | all 4 |
| 2 (C(3)) | 8   | 176 | 2748 | 168 | 176 | **No** | all 8 |

Observation 1: `rank(Δ) = n_φ − 4n` and `rank([Δ|Γ]) = n_φ` exactly.
Thus the obstruction cokernel has dimension 4n — exactly the number of gb
parameters — and the induced map `gb → cokernel(Δ)` is injective. In
particular, no non-zero gb vector lies in col(Δ): the deformation is
non-trivial for every non-zero gb.

Observation 2: `rank(Δ) + 4n = n_φ` means Δ has nullity 4n — i.e., the
space of f-ansatz choices with `δf = 0` has dimension 4n. This matches the
dimension of gb and is consistent with the standard super-cocycle
interpretation (gauge freedom in f dual to the deformation parameters).

#### Concrete illustration (n = 1, (X, Y) = (H₁, H₂))

γ(H₁, H₂) = −gb_{a₁⁺,b₁⁺} · E_{ε+δ,−} − gb_{a₁⁺,b₁⁻} · E_{ε−δ,−}
            − gb_{a₁⁻,b₁⁺} · E_{−ε+δ,−} − gb_{a₁⁻,b₁⁻} · E_{−ε−δ,−}

(δf)(H₁, H₂) coefficient of E_{ε+δ,−} = φ_{E_{ε+δ,−}, H₁} + 2·φ_{E_{ε+δ,−}, H₂}
             coefficient of E_{−ε−δ,−} = −φ_{E_{−ε−δ,−}, H₁} − 2·φ_{E_{−ε−δ,−}, H₂}
             coefficient of E_{ε−δ,−} = −φ_{E_{ε−δ,−}, H₁}
             coefficient of E_{−ε+δ,−} = φ_{E_{−ε+δ,−}, H₁}

Setting γ = δf gives 4 scalar equations for 6 φ unknowns per this one
(X, Y). These alone are solvable, but when combined with the 124 "δf-only"
equations (triples where γ = 0 but δf has terms) and all other matching
rows, the φ unknowns are forced into contradictions with any non-zero gb.

### Analysis of Discrepancies

Mechanism: Δ encodes the structure of brackets `[X, Z]` and `f([X,Y])`.
Its image lies in the subspace of the triple space generated by applying
the undeformed Lie bracket to odd/even partners. The γ map, in contrast,
produces coefficients purely in the four "sign slots" of the odd basis
E_{ε·σ, δ_k·s} indexed by gb_{σ,k,s} — these four parameters feed into
distinct, independent axes of the triple space.

Concretely, for every (ordered) adjoint-action equation of the form
"H · E_{ε·σ, δ_k·s} = (±1 ± 1) · E_{same}", the γ-side depends on
gb_{σ,k,s} with coefficient ±1 or ±2, while the δf-side depends on at most
two φ symbols. The overdetermined system that remains after eliminating φ
retains exactly one obstruction per gb parameter.

### General Conjecture

**Conjecture.** For C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) with n ≥ 1, the inhomogeneous
deformation defined by `[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = −gb_{σ,j,s} · κ` is **trivial if
and only if the entire 4n-vector of gb parameters vanishes**. Equivalently,
every non-zero element of the gb parameter space defines a non-trivial
cohomology class in H²(g; g) along the κ-direction.

The precise linear statement: the four maps

    gb_{σ,j,s} ↦ γ_{σ,j,s}  : Q → H²(g; g)

are linearly independent and inject into H², so the obstruction space has
dimension 4n (full rank). Verified by direct rank computation for n = 1
and n = 2; the pattern (nullity(Δ) = 4n, rank(Δ) + 4n = n_φ) extends to
n = 3 by the uniform combinatorics of the triple space.

### Active Investigation

- Built a general sympy framework (`build_matrices` in
  `src/C_triviality.py`) that computes Δ, Γ, and their rank data for any n.
- Confirmed every γ-nonzero triple has a matching δf-nonzero entry
  (`γ-only = 0` for both n), so there is no "structural" obstruction from
  the pattern of nonzero entries — the obstruction is purely linear.
- The rank deficit `rank([Δ|Γ]) − rank(Δ) = 4n` matches the gb dimension
  for both tested n, so no gb direction escapes the obstruction.
