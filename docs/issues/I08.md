### Response

**Comparison and case-specific results:** Added `src/C_triviality.py` for an exact support and central-projection comparison of Layers 3 and 4. In all three stored all-plus profiles, the deformation is non-trivial. Each Layer 4 coboundary is valued in the Schema 1 basis and has no `K` output; the evaluated deformation has nonzero `K` terms:

| Algebra | Evaluated gamma terms | Nonzero `K` terms | Terms outside coboundary support | Central constraint rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C(2) | 60 | 10 | 11 | 4/4 |
| C(3) | 334 | 42 | 44 | 8/8 |
| C(4) | 932 | 90 | 93 | 12/12 |

For example, in all three ranks the all-plus Layer 3 data contains
`(E_2del1_p, E_eps1_del1_pm) -> -1 K` and
`(E_2del1_m, E_eps1_del1_pp) -> +1 K`; no Layer 4 coboundary term can produce `K`. In contrast, set every `gb` parameter to zero: all symbolic Layer 2 terms vanish, and choosing `f=0` gives $\delta f=0$, so the two structures coincide (trivial).

**Necessary-and-sufficient condition (formal conjecture):** For each $j$, the symbolic $K$-projection contains four isolated equations:

| Generator pair `(X,Y)` | $K$ coefficient |
|---|---|
| (`E_2del{j}_p`, `E_eps1_del{j}_pm`) | $-\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^+}$ |
| (`E_2del{j}_m`, `E_eps1_del{j}_pp`) | $+\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^-}$ |
| (`E_eps1_del{j}_mm`, `E_2del{j}_p`) | $+\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^+}$ |
| (`E_eps1_del{j}_mp`, `E_2del{j}_m`) | $-\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^-}$ |

The comparison verifies these independent rows for $n=1,2,3$, giving central constraint rank $4n$. Since $\delta f$ has no `K` component, triviality forces every `gb` to vanish; conversely, the zero assignment is trivial with $f=0$. This is the conjectured iff condition for general rank under the implemented oscillator formulas.

**Caveat:** This conclusion is formal, not a parity-consistent superalgebra result: the source assigns odd parity to both `gb` and $\kappa$, making their product even although the mixed oscillator commutator is odd. The parity conflict recorded in I05 must be resolved before interpreting the formal nontriviality result as a theorem about a valid deformation.

**Verification:** `python3 src/C_triviality.py` reported non-triviality for all-plus at C(2), C(3), and C(4), with full-rank central constraints and a trivial all-zero specialization. `pytest -q` passed all 42 tests.
