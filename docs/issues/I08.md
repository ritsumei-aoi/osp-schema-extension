## Triviality search report

Implemented `src/search_C_triviality.py` to compare the Layer 3
`kappa_coeff` tensor with the exact image of the Layer 4 linear map
$f\mapsto\delta f$. It constructs the sparse coefficient matrices over
$\mathbb{Q}$ and computes their ranks without numerical approximation.

| Algebra | Odd-map coefficients | Rank of $\delta f$ image | Independent gb classes modulo coboundaries | All-plus residual coordinates |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C(2) | 32 | 28 | 4 of 4 | 53 |
| C(3) | 176 | 168 | 8 of 8 | 327 |
| C(4) | 528 | 516 | 12 of 12 | 926 |

**Trivial case.** If every gb parameter is zero, Layer 3 has zero
$\kappa$-correction. This equals $\delta f$ for the zero odd map, so the
zero deformation is trivial.

**Non-trivial all-plus case.** In the checked-in Layer 3 files every gb is
`+1`. For C(2), for example,
`(E_2del1_p, E_eps1_del1_pm)` has `kappa_coeff = +1/2` with output `K`;
`(E_2del1_m, E_eps1_del1_pp)` has `kappa_coeff = -1/2` with output `K`.
Layer 4 maps basis generators to basis generators and all brackets/f-images
remain in the Layer 1 basis, which excludes `K`; hence these scalar identity
components cannot occur in any $\delta f$. The exact quotient calculation
also finds 53, 327, and 926 nonzero residual coordinates for C(2), C(3),
and C(4).

The following isolated `K`-output witnesses appear in the generated gamma
data and do not receive contributions from another gb parameter at the same
coordinate. Their coefficients are therefore direct obstructions:

| Parameter | Generator pair producing the `K` obstruction | Coefficient |
|---|---|---:|
| $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^+}$ | `(E_2del{j}_p, E_eps1_del{j}_pm)` | $+1/2$ |
| $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^+,b_j^-}$ | `(E_2del{j}_m, E_eps1_del{j}_pp)` | $-1/2$ |
| $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^-}$ | `(E_2del{j}_m, E_eps1_del{j}_mp)` | $-1/2$ |
| $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_1^+}$ | `(E_eps1_del1_mm, E_2del1_p)` | $-1/2$ |
| $\mathrm{gb}_{a_1^-,b_j^+}$, $j>1$ | `(E_del1_del{j}_mp, E_eps1_del1_mp)` | $-1/2$ |

**Conjecture.** For every bosonic rank $n\geq1$, the deformation is trivial
if and only if all $4n$ gb parameters vanish. The exact calculations at
`n=1,2,3` show that the gb classes are linearly independent modulo the
coboundary image, and the isolated scalar witnesses above give the
rank-independent obstruction pattern. Sufficiency at the zero assignment
is witnessed by $f=0$.
