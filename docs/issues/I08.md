2. **Mismatching terms**: γ ≠ δf (obstruction to triviality)
3. **Gamma-only terms**: γ ≠ 0 but δf absent (indicates over-determined system)
4. **Coboundary-only terms**: δf ≠ 0 but γ absent (indicates under-determined system)

#### Root Cause Analysis

**Why is the all_positive profile non-trivial?**

**Mathematical Obstruction**:

The coboundary formula $(δf)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)}[X, f(Y)] - ... - f([X, Y])$ produces coefficient factors that depend on:
1. The structure of the odd map f
2. The commutation relations in the algebra
3. Parity-weighted signs

For the symmetric all_positive gb assignment:
- The deformation γ uniformly couples all ε-roots to Cartan
- The coboundary (δf) produces reduced coefficients due to the f-structure
- Systematic mismatch: γ_abc > (δf)_abc for most terms

**Specific Example (C(2))**:
```
γ(H_1, E_eps_del{k}) = gb_a1+_b_k = 1 (uniform)
(δf)(H_1, E_eps_del{k}) ∝ [H_1, f(E_eps_del{k})] / (parity factors)
                         ≈ 1/2 (reduced by f-structure)
```

This 2:1 ratio is consistent across all n, indicating a **structural obstruction** rather than accidental mismatch.
