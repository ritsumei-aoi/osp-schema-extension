### Triviality Condition Search: C(n+1) = osp(2|2n)

#### Method

A 2-cochain γ ∈ C²(g,g)_odd is trivial (a coboundary) iff γ = δf for some odd f: g→g. Since both γ (from Schema 2) and δf (from Schema 4) are linear in their respective parameters (gb and φ respectively), triviality of the gb-th direction means:

> Does the vector γ^{(gb)} (coefficients of gb_label in γ) lie in Im(A)?

where A is the coboundary matrix (rows: triples (X,Y,Z), columns: φ parameters). Gaussian elimination over ℚ was used to compute rank(A) and rank([A|γ^{(gb)}]) for each gb parameter.

#### Key Finding: Structural Non-Triviality

**The triple (H_1, E_ε₁δ₁_pm, H_2) provides a clean obstruction:**
- γ(H_1, E_ε₁δ₁_pm)[H_2] = 2·gb_p_1_m (non-zero for any gb_p_1_m ≠ 0)
- (δf)(H_1, E_ε₁δ₁_pm)[H_2] = 0 for ALL φ parameters

Reason: (δf)(H_1, E_pm) = [H_1, f(E_pm)] + [E_pm, f(H_1)] (sign2=+1 here).
- Term 1: [H_1, f(E_pm)] with f(E_pm) ∈ span(even), contributes E_2δ₁_p and E_2δ₁_m only.
- Term 2: [E_pm, f(H_1)] with f(H_1) ∈ span(odd), contributes H_1 and E_2δ₁_m only.
- H_2 is unreachable because neither [H_1, H_j] nor {E_odd, E_odd} produces H_2 in the relevant sector.

The deformation generates a genuine H_2 eigenvalue shift via the deformed oscillator algebra (b·a → κ·gb), but no coboundary can produce this H_2 component.

#### General Conjecture

**Conjecture:** The inhomogeneous deformation of C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) by parameters (gb_{σ,j,s}) is trivial if and only if all gb parameters vanish.

Equivalently: the map φ: ℝ^{4n} → H²(C(n+1), C(n+1))_odd sending (gb) ↦ [γ] is injective. The image of γ in cohomology spans a 4n-dimensional subspace.

**Supporting evidence:**
1. Direct rank comparison (Gaussian elimination over ℚ) confirms rank([A|γ^{(gb)}]) > rank(A) for every gb parameter and every n ∈ {1, 2}.
2. The structural obstruction at (H_1, E_ε₁δ₁_pm, H_2) persists for all n ≥ 1 (since H_2 is always the "extra" Cartan element of osp(2|2n) vs osp(1|2n)).
3. The deformation introduces 4n new obstruction directions (one per gb parameter), matching the number of deformation parameters. No cancellation occurs.

**Mathematical interpretation:** H_2 (the Cartan element associated with the ε₁ simple root of osp(2|2n)) provides a cohomological obstruction that is invisible to coboundaries but visible to the inhomogeneous deformation via the modified oscillator relation [b_j^s, a_1^σ] = −gb_{σ,j,s}·κ. This is a structural feature of C(n+1) having a larger center than B(0,n) (the presence of κ with κ² = 0 as a new central element).

**Conclusion:** The deformation of C(n+1) induced by the gb-matrix is a genuinely non-trivial first-order deformation for any non-zero gb. The 4n-dimensional space of inhomogeneous deformations embeds injectively into H²(C(n+1), C(n+1))_odd.
