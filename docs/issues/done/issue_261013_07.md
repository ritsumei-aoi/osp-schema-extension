### Response

#### 1. Mathematical Grounding & Coboundary Operator Definition

From `docs/math/C_coboundary_definition.md`:
1. An **odd linear map** $f: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ reverses $\mathbb{Z}_2$-parity:
   - For even generators $X \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$ ($p(X)=0$): $p(f(X)) = 1 \implies f(X) \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$.
   - For odd generators $X \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}}$ ($p(X)=1$): $p(f(X)) = 0 \implies f(X) \in \mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}$.
   - Parameterized by $\phi_{ij}$ such that $f(Z_j) = \sum_{i} \phi_{ij} Z_i$, with $p(Z_i) = 1 - p(Z_j)$.
2. The **coboundary operator** $\delta f: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ is defined by:
   $$ (\delta f)(X, Y) = (-1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] - (-1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] - f([X, Y]) $$
   where $[ \cdot, \cdot ]$ is the standard Lie superalgebra bracket on $\mathfrak{g}$.
3. Graded skew-symmetry is verified identically:
   $$ (\delta f)(Y, X) = - (-1)^{p(X) p(Y)} (\delta f)(X, Y) $$
