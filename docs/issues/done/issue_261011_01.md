## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Basis, label, and ordering proposals (approved)

The basis lists below follow the supplied \(C(n+1)=\mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)\)
root system. The even Cartan basis is \(H_1,\ldots,H_{n+1}\), and the even
nonzero roots are \(\pm2\delta_k\) and \(\pm(\delta_i\pm\delta_j)\) for
\(i<j\). The odd roots are \(\pm\varepsilon\pm\delta_k\). Thus the proposed
dimensions are \(2n^2+n+1\) even and \(4n\) odd.

**C(2), \(n=1\)**

- Even: `H_1`, `H_2`, `E_2del1_p`, `E_2del1_m` (4 generators).
- Odd: `E_eps1_del1_pp`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`, `E_eps1_del1_mp`,
  `E_eps1_del1_mm` (4 generators).

**C(3), \(n=2\)**

- Even: `H_1`, `H_2`, `H_3`; `E_2del1_p`, `E_2del2_p`;
  `E_del1_del2_pp`, `E_del1_del2_pm`;
  `E_2del1_m`, `E_2del2_m`;
  `E_del1_del2_mp`, `E_del1_del2_mm` (11 generators).
- Odd: `E_eps1_del1_pp`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`, `E_eps1_del1_mp`,
  `E_eps1_del1_mm`; `E_eps1_del2_pp`, `E_eps1_del2_pm`,
  `E_eps1_del2_mp`, `E_eps1_del2_mm` (8 generators).

**C(4), \(n=3\)**

- Even: `H_1`, `H_2`, `H_3`, `H_4`; `E_2del1_p`, `E_2del2_p`,
  `E_2del3_p`; for each pair `(i,j)` in `(1,2)`, `(1,3)`, `(2,3)`,
  `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pm`;
  `E_2del1_m`, `E_2del2_m`, `E_2del3_m`; for each such pair,
  `E_del{i}_del{j}_mp`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_mm` (22 generators).
- Odd: for each `k` in `1`, `2`, `3`,
  `E_eps1_del{k}_pp`, `E_eps1_del{k}_pm`, `E_eps1_del{k}_mp`,
  `E_eps1_del{k}_mm` (12 generators).

Here `p` and `m` in a root-generator suffix denote the plus and minus signs,
respectively. For \(i<j\), the proposed even labels mean:

| Label | Root | Oscillator realization |
|---|---|---|
| `E_2del{k}_p` / `E_2del{k}_m` | \(\pm2\delta_k\) | \((b_k^+)^2\) / \((b_k^-)^2\) |
| `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp` | \(\delta_i+\delta_j\) | \(b_i^+b_j^+\) |
| `E_del{i}_del{j}_pm` | \(\delta_i-\delta_j\) | \(b_i^+b_j^-\) |
| `E_del{i}_del{j}_mp` | \(-\delta_i+\delta_j\) | \(b_i^-b_j^+\) |
| `E_del{i}_del{j}_mm` | \(-\delta_i-\delta_j\) | \(b_i^-b_j^-\) |

For the odd labels, the first suffix sign is the \(\varepsilon\) sign and the
second is the \(\delta_k\) sign:

| Label | Root | Oscillator realization |
|---|---|---|
| `E_eps1_del{k}_pp` | \(\varepsilon+\delta_k\) | `a_1_p b_k_p` |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_pm` | \(\varepsilon-\delta_k\) | `a_1_p b_k_m` |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_mp` | \(-\varepsilon+\delta_k\) | `a_1_m b_k_p` |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_mm` | \(-\varepsilon-\delta_k\) | `a_1_m b_k_m` |

The `H_i` labels are retained as in the supplied oscillator realization:
`H_1` through `H_n` followed by `H_{n+1}`. The formal central symbol `K`
remains excluded from the basis as the scalar identity; if \(\kappa\) is
included in the PBW convention for the central extension, it precedes the
algebra generators.

Two complete PBW ordering proposals are offered for selection. In both,
indices within a family are ascending (pairs are lexicographic); the order
within each bracketed block is explicitly given below.

**Option A — B(0,n)-compatible parity-block ordering**

`κ < [odd: k ascending, suffix pp, pm, mp, mm] < [even: H_1,...,H_{n+1}; E_2del{k}_p (k ascending); E_del{i}_del{j}_pp (lexicographic pairs); E_del{i}_del{j}_pm (lexicographic pairs); E_2del{k}_m (k ascending); E_del{i}_del{j}_mm (lexicographic pairs); E_del{i}_del{j}_mp (lexicographic pairs)]`

This preserves the existing B(0,n) convention of placing the odd basis
before the even basis, while using a deterministic root-family order.

**Option B — triangular root ordering**

Use the positive roots determined by the simple system
\(\varepsilon-\delta_1,\delta_1-\delta_2,\ldots,\delta_{n-1}-\delta_n,
2\delta_n\). Order the generators as

`κ < [negative roots: odd E_eps1_del{k}_mp then E_eps1_del{k}_mm (k ascending); even E_2del{k}_m (k ascending), E_del{i}_del{j}_mm then E_del{i}_del{j}_mp (lexicographic pairs)] < [H_1,...,H_{n+1}] < [positive roots: even E_2del{k}_p (k ascending), E_del{i}_del{j}_pp then E_del{i}_del{j}_pm (lexicographic pairs); odd E_eps1_del{k}_pp then E_eps1_del{k}_pm (k ascending)]`

This places all negative root vectors before the Cartan basis and all positive
root vectors after it, as a triangular PBW ordering.

**Decision:** The human researcher approved the basis lists and oscillator
labels and selected Option A on 2026-10-11. Option A is finalized in
`handover/notation.md`; Option B is retained here as the unselected proposal.
