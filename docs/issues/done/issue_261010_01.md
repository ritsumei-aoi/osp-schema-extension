## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

### Response

### Proposal: basis and generator labels

Use the Frappat root convention from `docs/math/Cn1_definition.md`. The index `p`
means a plus sign and `m` a minus sign. For `i < j`,
`E_del{i}_del{j}_pp`, `_pm`, `_mp`, and `_mm` denote respectively
$\delta_i+\delta_j$, $\delta_i-\delta_j$, $-\delta_i+\delta_j$, and
$-\delta_i-\delta_j$. For the odd roots, suffixes are ordered
`pp`, `pm`, `mp`, `mm`; the first sign is the $\varepsilon$ sign and the
second is the $\delta_k$ sign:

| Label | Root | Oscillator realization |
|---|---|---|
| `E_eps1_del{k}_pp` | $\varepsilon+\delta_k$ | $a_{1,p} b_{k,p}$ |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_pm` | $\varepsilon-\delta_k$ | $a_{1,p} b_{k,m}$ |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_mp` | $-\varepsilon+\delta_k$ | $a_{1,m} b_{k,p}$ |
| `E_eps1_del{k}_mm` | $-\varepsilon-\delta_k$ | $a_{1,m} b_{k,m}$ |

Here `a_1_p`/`a_1_m` and `b_k_p`/`b_k_m` are the labels used in oscillator
words (the mathematical table writes their subscripts with underscores).
This retains the existing `E_2del{k}_p/m` and `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp/pm/mp/mm`
labels for even roots.

For every rank $n$, the even list consists of `H_1` through `H_{n+1}`,
`E_2del{k}_p/m` for $1\le k\le n$, and all four
`E_del{i}_del{j}_pp/pm/mp/mm` for $1\le i<j\le n$. The odd list consists of
the four `E_eps1_del{k}_pp/pm/mp/mm` for each $1\le k\le n$.
Expanded by rank:

| Algebra | Even basis | Odd basis |
|---|---|---|
| C(2), $n=1$ | `H_1, H_2, E_2del1_p, E_2del1_m` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm` |
| C(3), $n=2$ | `H_1, H_2, H_3, E_2del1_p, E_2del1_m, E_2del2_p, E_2del2_m, E_del1_del2_pp, E_del1_del2_pm, E_del1_del2_mp, E_del1_del2_mm` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm, E_eps1_del2_pp, E_eps1_del2_pm, E_eps1_del2_mp, E_eps1_del2_mm` |
| C(4), $n=3$ | `H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, E_2del1_p, E_2del1_m, E_2del2_p, E_2del2_m, E_2del3_p, E_2del3_m, E_del1_del2_pp, E_del1_del2_pm, E_del1_del2_mp, E_del1_del2_mm, E_del1_del3_pp, E_del1_del3_pm, E_del1_del3_mp, E_del1_del3_mm, E_del2_del3_pp, E_del2_del3_pm, E_del2_del3_mp, E_del2_del3_mm` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm, E_eps1_del2_pp, E_eps1_del2_pm, E_eps1_del2_mp, E_eps1_del2_mm, E_eps1_del3_pp, E_eps1_del3_pm, E_eps1_del3_mp, E_eps1_del3_mm` |

These lists have even/odd dimensions $4|4$, $11|8$, and $22|12$ respectively,
including all Cartan generators.

### PBW ordering options for human selection

For both options, use the positive roots
$2\delta_k$, $\delta_i+\delta_j$, $\delta_i-\delta_j$,
$\varepsilon+\delta_k$, and $\varepsilon-\delta_k$; their negatives are the
negative roots. In each family, sort $k$ increasingly or pairs $(i,j)$
lexicographically. For sign variants within a family, use suffix order
`pp`, `pm`, `mp`, `mm`, omitting suffixes that do not apply. Place `κ` first
if represented, and exclude `K = 1` from the basis, consistent with the
existing B(0,n) schema.

**Option A — parity-block, closest to the B(0,n) schema:**
`κ < [odd: positive roots, then negative roots] < [H_1,...,H_{n+1}] < [positive even roots] < [negative even roots]`.
Within the odd block, list all positive labels (`pp`, `pm`) by increasing $k$
and suffix, followed by all negative labels (`mp`, `mm`) by increasing $k$
and suffix. Within the even root blocks, order root families as `2del`,
`del+del`, `del-del`; the negative labels are `m`, `mm`, and `mp`
respectively.

**Option B — triangular root-space PBW order:**
`κ < [negative even roots] < [negative odd roots] < [H_1,...,H_{n+1}] < [positive odd roots] < [positive even roots]`.
Use the same within-family orders stated above; in the odd blocks, negative
labels are `mp`, `mm` and positive labels are `pp`, `pm`.

Option A preserves the existing schema's parity-first organization; Option B
puts negative roots, Cartan, and positive roots in conventional triangular
order.

**Human decision:** Option A (parity-block) was selected. The basis lists,
generator labels, oscillator realizations, and selected PBW ordering are
documented in `handover/notation.md`.
