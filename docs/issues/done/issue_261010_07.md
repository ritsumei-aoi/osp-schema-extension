#### Analysis of the Mathematical Structure

From `C_coboundary_definition.md`, the coboundary of an odd linear map f is:

> δf(X, Y) = (−1)^{p(X)} [X, f(Y)] − (−1)^{(p(X)+1)p(Y)} [Y, f(X)] − f([X, Y])

Since f must **reverse parity**, the general odd linear map on g = C(n+1) decomposes into two blocks:
- **Block (odd→even)**: f maps each odd generator E_eps1_del{k}_σ to a linear combination of the (2n²+n+1) even generators.
- **Block (even→odd)**: f maps each of the (2n²+n+1) even generators to a linear combination of the 4n odd generators.

The γ deformation (Schema 2) shows that γ(X, Y) is **linear in the gb parameters** and outputs generators of both parities depending on whether (X,Y) is an (odd,odd), (odd,even), or (even,even) pair.

#### Parameterization Options

- f(Z^{even}_j) = Σ_{k,σ} φ^{eo}_{j,k,σ} · E_eps1_del{k}_σ

---

**Option B — Weight-Graded (Root-Respecting) Map**

Only allow f to map each generator Z_α to generators at the **same root** but of opposite parity. However, in C(n+1), no root has generators of **both** parities: the even roots (±2δ_k, ±(δ_i±δ_j), 0) are purely even, and the odd roots (±ε±δ_k) are purely odd. The root-zero (Cartan) subspace is purely even with no odd counterpart in g itself (κ is the extension element, not a basis element of g). Therefore this option yields f = 0 identically — a trivial coboundary.

*Conclusion*: Option B is not viable for a non-trivial coboundary analysis.

---

**Option C — Targeted Minimal Map (Ansatz)**

Restrict the support of f to generators that appear in the output of γ. From the n=1 gamma data:
- γ(odd, odd) → odd generators with gb coefficients
- γ(odd, even) → even generators with gb coefficients
- γ(even, even) → odd generators with gb coefficients

A targeted ansatz:
- f(E_eps1_del{k}_σ) = Σ_{j: Cartan + "short" even roots} φ^{oe}_{k,σ,j} · Z^{even}_j (restrict to Cartan H and root-2δ generators only)
- f(H_l) = Σ_{k,σ} φ^{eo}_{l,k,σ} · E_eps1_del{k}_σ
- f(E_2del{k}_±) = Σ_{m,σ} ψ^{eo}_{k,±,m,σ} · E_eps1_del{m}_σ
- f(E_del{i}_del{j}_...) = 0 (zero on long even roots)

This is motivated by the observation that the γ output lives in a subspace of g. Fewer parameters, more tractable system.

#### Recommendation

I recommend **Option A** (most general) for mathematical completeness: it constructs the full coboundary space and, combined with the γ data from Schema 2, allows a complete check of whether the deformation is trivial by solving the linear system γ = δf for the φ parameters.

The Schema 4 JSON would encode the **symbolic coboundary** δf(X,Y) for all basis pairs (X,Y), expressed as a sum of terms `coeff · φ_label · Z_label`, mirroring the structure of Schema 2 (gamma_structure).
