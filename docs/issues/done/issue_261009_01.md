## Context & Background
C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) uses a standard fermionic pair (a_1_p, a_1_m) instead of
B(0,n)'s supplementary fermion a_0. This changes the basis structure, PBW
ordering, and oscillator relations throughout the entire schema.

The existing B(0,n) implementation is available for reference at:
https://github.com/ritsumei-aoi/osp-triviality

For C(n+1) root structure, refer to:
Frappat et al., "Dictionary on Lie Algebras and Superalgebras", arXiv:hep-th/9607161

#### Proposal: basis and generator labels

Use `H_1, ..., H_{n+1}` for the Cartan generators. For even root generators,
use the labels already used by the B(0,n) schema:

- `E_2del{k}_p` and `E_2del{k}_m` denote roots `2δ_k` and `-2δ_k`.
- For `i < j`, `E_del{i}_del{j}_pp`, `_pm`, `_mp`, and `_mm` denote,
  respectively, `δ_i+δ_j`, `δ_i-δ_j`, `-δ_i+δ_j`, and `-δ_i-δ_j`.
- `E_eps1_del{k}_pp`, `_pm`, `_mp`, and `_mm` denote, respectively,
  `ε+δ_k`, `ε-δ_k`, `-ε+δ_k`, and `-ε-δ_k`. The first sign is the
  ε sign and the second is the δ sign.

The proposed even and odd basis lists are:

| Algebra | Even basis | Odd basis |
|---|---|---|
| C(2), `n=1` | `H_1, H_2, E_2del1_p, E_2del1_m` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm` |
| C(3), `n=2` | `H_1, H_2, H_3, E_2del1_p, E_2del2_p, E_del1_del2_pp, E_del1_del2_pm, E_2del1_m, E_2del2_m, E_del1_del2_mp, E_del1_del2_mm` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm, E_eps1_del2_pp, E_eps1_del2_pm, E_eps1_del2_mp, E_eps1_del2_mm` |
| C(4), `n=3` | `H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, E_2del1_p, E_2del2_p, E_2del3_p, E_del1_del2_pp, E_del1_del2_pm, E_del1_del3_pp, E_del1_del3_pm, E_del2_del3_pp, E_del2_del3_pm, E_2del1_m, E_2del2_m, E_2del3_m, E_del1_del2_mp, E_del1_del2_mm, E_del1_del3_mp, E_del1_del3_mm, E_del2_del3_mp, E_del2_del3_mm` | `E_eps1_del1_pp, E_eps1_del1_pm, E_eps1_del1_mp, E_eps1_del1_mm, E_eps1_del2_pp, E_eps1_del2_pm, E_eps1_del2_mp, E_eps1_del2_mm, E_eps1_del3_pp, E_eps1_del3_pm, E_eps1_del3_mp, E_eps1_del3_mm` |

These counts agree with `dim(g_even)=2n²+n+1` and `dim(g_odd)=4n`.
The Cartan generator labels follow the supplied oscillator realization;
in particular, `H_1` through `H_{n+1}` are independent Cartan elements.
`K` (the scalar identity) and the extension symbol `κ` are not included in
these finite-dimensional Lie-superalgebra basis lists or in the proposed
ordering.

#### PBW ordering options for human selection

Both options use the even-root sequence `H_1,...,H_{n+1}`, then all
`E_2del{k}_p` in increasing `k`, then all positive pair-root generators
`E_del{i}_del{j}_pp, E_del{i}_del{j}_pm` in lexicographic `(i,j)` order,
then all `E_2del{k}_m` in increasing `k`, then all negative pair-root
generators `E_del{i}_del{j}_mp, E_del{i}_del{j}_mm` in lexicographic
`(i,j)` order.

1. **Option A — odd block before even block (recommended for continuity with B(0,n)):**
   order all odd generators first, by increasing `k` and suffix order
   `pp, pm, mp, mm`; follow with the even-root sequence above.
   Thus the block order is `[odd] < [even]`.
2. **Option B — even block before odd block:**
   order by the even-root sequence above, then all `E_eps1_del{k}_pp` and
   `E_eps1_del{k}_pm` in increasing `k`, followed by all
   `E_eps1_del{k}_mp` and `E_eps1_del{k}_mm` in increasing `k`.
   Thus the block order is `[even] < [odd]`, with the odd block grouped
   into positive-ε and negative-ε roots.

Each option is a total ordering of the displayed basis.

#### Human decision and completion

The human researcher approved the proposed basis lists and generator labels
and selected **Option A**, with the odd block ordered before the even block.
The finalized conventions and complete basis lists have been documented in
`handover/notation.md`. All completion criteria are satisfied.
