### Response

I have analyzed the inhomogeneous deformation (Schema 2) requirements for C(n+1) = osp(2|2n).

#### 1. Gamma Computation Method
The gamma structure for C(n+1) is defined by the deformed commutator $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$. To compute $\gamma_{abc}$, I propose the following method:
1.  **Oscillator Expansion**: Express generators $X, Y \in \mathfrak{g}$ as polynomials in $a_1^\sigma$ and $b_j^s$.
2.  **Bracket Evaluation**: Compute the bracket $[X, Y]_\gamma$ by substituting $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$ into the algebraic expression, isolating terms proportional to $\kappa$.
3.  **Coefficient Extraction**: The gamma coefficients $\gamma(X, Y) = \sum_Z \gamma_{XYZ} Z$ are extracted as the structure constant-like coefficients of these $\kappa$-linear terms.

#### 2. Sign Convention Options

**Option 1: Minimalist Convention (Recommended)**
- Set $\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} = 1$ for all $\sigma, j, s$.
- Consistent with the B(0,n) triviality studies (Aoi 2026), minimizing the number of free parameters for the initial implementation.

**Option 2: Symmetric Convention**
- Introduce alternating signs based on parity: $\mathrm{gb}_{+, j, +} = 1, \mathrm{gb}_{-, j, -} = -1$.
- May simplify the expression of $\gamma_{abc}$ for specific subsets of the algebra but increases complexity in the initial structure constant table.

**Recommendation**: I propose Option 1 for the initial implementation to ensure compatibility with existing B(0,n) infrastructure.

Please approve the computation method and sign convention (Option 1) so I may proceed to implement the generator script.
