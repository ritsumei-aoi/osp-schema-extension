### Triviality search report

**Method.** For each rank, I treated a coordinate as an ordered triple
\((X,Y,Z)\), with \(Z\) in the Lie-superalgebra basis. Layer 4 gives a linear
map \(A:f\mapsto\delta f\) in the formal odd-map coefficients \(\phi,\psi\);
Layer 2 gives the parameter-linear map \(G:\mathrm{gb}\mapsto\gamma\), and
Layer 3 is its all-\(+1\) evaluation. The test is whether
\(G(\mathrm{gb})\in\operatorname{im}A\), not whether the displayed
coefficients match term-by-term with some arbitrary choice of \(\phi,\psi\).
The scalar \(K=1\) outputs were omitted from this test, as the schema and
coboundary definition specify triviality up to scalar.

| \(n\) | Algebra | Layer 3 entries (including \(K\)) | Lie-basis entries tested | Layer 4 coordinates | Independent gb directions with exact witnesses |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | C(2) | 112 | 92 | 212 | 4/4 |
| 2 | C(3) | 652 | 568 | 2,748 | 8/8 |
| 3 | C(4) | 1,840 | 1,660 | 13,976 | 12/12 |

For a concrete C(2) check, Layer 3 has coefficient \(2\) for
\((X,Y,Z)=(\texttt{E\_eps1\_del1\_pp},\texttt{E\_eps1\_del1\_pp},
\texttt{E\_eps1\_del1\_pp})\) at the all-\(+1\) profile. Layer 4 expresses
that coordinate as
\(4\psi_{\texttt{H\_1 from E\_eps1\_del1\_pp}}
-2\psi_{\texttt{H\_2 from E\_eps1\_del1\_pp}}\); the exact witness values
\(1/3\) and \(-1/3\) give \(2\). The full rational systems, not just this
coordinate, were checked by exact substitution: every Lie-basis row agrees
for the all-\(+1\) profile at all three ranks.

**Findings and parameter condition.** I also tested each independent gb unit
direction from the symbolic Layer 2 data against the corresponding Layer 4
map. Exact rational witnesses exist for all 4, 8, and 12 directions at
\(n=1,2,3\), respectively. Since both \(\gamma\) and \(\delta f\) are linear,
their linear combinations show that **every assignment of the gb parameters
is trivial for the tested ranks**. In particular, the nonzero all-\(+1\)
profiles are trivial, and the all-zero profile is trivially zero. There are
no non-trivial gb cases or unmatched Lie-basis coefficients in these data.
Although an individual Layer 3 coefficient need not equal a Layer 4
coefficient before choosing \(f\), such a pointwise difference is not an
obstruction: a single global odd map matches the full table.

**Conjecture.** For every \(n\geq1\), every gb assignment in this
inhomogeneous family yields a coboundary in the full odd-map space; hence
there are no additional gb vanishing or equality constraints for triviality.
The exact data check establishes this for \(n=1,2,3\); the extension to
\(n\geq4\) remains a conjecture rather than a result of these finite-rank
datasets. The issue asks for non-trivial examples, but none occur in the
provided parameter family, so reporting any would contradict the comparison.
