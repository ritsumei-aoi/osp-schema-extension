### Response

#### 1. Mathematical Grounding & Formal Definition of Schema 2 for $C(n+1)$

Following `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`:
- The superspace $V = V_{\bar{0}} \oplus V_{\bar{1}}$ has basis:
  - $V_{\bar{1}}$ (fermionic oscillators): $a_1^+, a_1^-$ ($2$ generators, parity 1)
  - $V_{\bar{0}}$ (bosonic oscillators): $b_j^+, b_j^-$ for $j=1, \ldots, n$ ($2n$ generators, parity 0)
- The extension $A = V \oplus \mathbb{C} K \oplus \mathbb{C} \kappa$ has deformed relations:
  $$ [b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa, \quad \sigma \in \{+,-\}, s \in \{+,-\} $$
  with $\kappa$ odd ($p(\kappa)=1$), $\kappa^2 = 0$, and $\mathrm{gb}$ parameters having odd parity ($p(\mathrm{gb}) = 1$).
- The deformed bracket on $C(n+1)$ is given by:
  $$ [X, Y]_\gamma = [X, Y]_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y) $$
  where $\gamma: \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ (or $\mathfrak{g} \oplus \mathbb{C} K$) is a 2-cocycle.

#### 2. Specification of the `gb_matrix` (Size $2 \times 2n$)

The deformation parameters form a $2 \times 2n$ matrix indexed by $(\sigma, (j, s))$:
- Rows: Fermionic oscillators $\sigma \in \{a_1^+, a_1^-\}$ (2 rows)
- Columns: Bosonic oscillators $b_j^s$ ($j=1, \ldots, n$, $s \in \{+,-\}$) ($2n$ columns)

##### Parameter Ordering Convention:
For each $n$, the $4n$ parameters are ordered as:
```json
[
  "gb_a1p_b1p", "gb_a1p_b1m", ..., "gb_a1p_bnp", "gb_a1p_bnm",
  "gb_a1m_b1p", "gb_a1m_b1m", ..., "gb_a1m_bnp", "gb_a1m_bnm"
]
```
- $n=1$ ($C(2)$): 4 parameters (`gb_a1p_b1p`, `gb_a1p_b1m`, `gb_a1m_b1p`, `gb_a1m_b1m`)
- $n=2$ ($C(3)$): 8 parameters
- $n=3$ ($C(4)$): 12 parameters

#### 3. Sign Convention Options for Human Selection

In evaluating the commutator $[X, Y]_\gamma$ in the oscillator enveloping algebra $\mathcal{U}(A)$, two sign convention options arise depending on whether the derivation acts from the left exchange or as a skew-supersymmetric form:

##### **Option A: Direct Inhomogeneous Commutator Convention (Definition 1.1 of `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`)**
The exchange relation is applied directly as stated in `C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`:
$$ [b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \implies [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa $$
Under the graded derivation property of $\mathcal{U}(A)$, for quadratic generators $u v$ and $x y$:
$$ [u v, x y\}_\gamma = [u v, x y\}_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(u v, x y) $$
where $\gamma(X, Y)$ is evaluated by normal-ordering the single $\kappa$-exchange terms.

##### **Option B: Symmetric Gram / Skew-supersymmetric Pre-oscillator Matrix Convention**
The pre-oscillator bilinear form on $V \times V$ satisfies $(a|b) = -(-1)^{p(a)p(b)} (b|a)$.
Here the exchange relation is parametrized via a Gram matrix $G_{\alpha \beta} = (\alpha | \beta)$ such that:
$$ (b_j^s | a_1^\sigma) = \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s}, \quad (a_1^\sigma | b_j^s) = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} $$
which aligns with the pre-oscillator form bracket $[a, b] = (a|b) \kappa$ for $p(a) \ne p(b)$.

**Recommendation**: **Option A** is directly aligned with Eq. (22) of `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`, preserving the explicit minus sign $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \kappa$.
