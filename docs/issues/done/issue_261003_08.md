#### Exact Layer 3 / Layer 4 comparison

Triviality was tested in the approved sense: for a given \(gb\) vector, does
there exist an odd map \(f\) such that the Schema 3 gamma coefficients equal
the Schema 4 \(\delta f\) coefficients in the Schema 1 basis? For each rank,
I formed the exact rational linear maps
\[
A:\phi\longmapsto\delta f,\qquad G:gb\longmapsto\gamma,
\]
with one row for every ordered basis triple \((X,Y,Z)\). The necessary and
sufficient condition is \(G\,gb\in\operatorname{im}A\).

| Algebra | Triple rows | \(\phi\) columns | \(gb\) columns | \(\operatorname{rank}A\) | \(\operatorname{rank}[A\mid G]\) | \(\operatorname{rank}G\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C(2) | 512 | 32 | 4 | 28 | 28 | 4 |
| C(3) | 6,859 | 176 | 8 | 168 | 168 | 8 |
| C(4) | 39,304 | 528 | 12 | 516 | 516 | 12 |

Thus every one of the \(4n\) independent Schema 2 parameter directions lies
in the image of the general coboundary map, and the equality of augmented
and coboundary ranks proves this for every linear combination. For n=1, 2,
and 3, respectively, **all** \(gb\) assignments are trivial; the necessary
and sufficient condition is simply that the parameters are arbitrary
scalars, with no additional equations. Since \(\operatorname{rank}G=4n\),
nonzero parameter vectors give nonzero gamma structures, but those
structures are still coboundaries. The zero vector is trivial with \(f=0\).

As a concrete nonzero example, the approved C(2) profile has
\[
\gamma(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1},E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1})
=2E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}.
\]
The corresponding Schema 4 coefficient is
\(4\phi_{\!H_1\leftarrow E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}}
-2\phi_{\!H_2\leftarrow E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}}\).
One exact global witness for this profile is
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1})&=-H_2-2E_{2\delta_1},\\
f(E_{\varepsilon-\delta_1})&= H_2+2E_{-2\delta_1},\\
f(E_{-\varepsilon+\delta_1})&= H_2-2E_{2\delta_1},\\

-E_{-\varepsilon+\delta_1}+E_{-\varepsilon-\delta_1},
\end{aligned}
\]
with \(f\) zero on the other even basis generators. It gives coefficient
\(2\) in the displayed bracket and matches all 512 ordered triple rows, not
only this example. Exact solutions for the approved C(3) and C(4) profiles

There are no non-trivial \(gb\) assignments in these three data sets under
