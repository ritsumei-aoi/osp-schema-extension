### Response

**Decisions**
- Implemented `src/C_generators.py`: a small exact (Fraction) PBW normal-ordering engine for the oscillator algebra (letters kappa < a_1_p < a_1_m < b_1_p < b_1_m < ...). Brackets are computed as supercommutators of the oscillator realizations and decomposed into the basis (roots by their unique monomial, Cartans by an exact (n+2)x(n+2) inverse including a K column).
- Options considered: (a) hard-code the Frappat commutation table, (b) compute brackets from oscillator realizations. Chose (b): it follows "using the defined oscillator relations" literally, is the only route that also yields the deformed brackets of I05, and it is an independent check against hand formulas.
- The engine already contains the deformed rule `b a = a b - gb κ` (symbols tracked per gb); with gb=0 it is the undeformed algebra used here.
- Closure check: every bracket of basis elements lands in span(basis) with **zero K (scalar) component** — this confirms the doc's Cartans (H_1 = a⁺a⁻+b₁⁺b₁⁻, H_{n+1} = -b_n⁺b_n⁻-1/2) are consistent (the I01 ambiguity is resolved by computation).
- `structure_constants` lists all ordered pairs (see spec §1).
