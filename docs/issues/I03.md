### Response

**Executed by**: Claude Sonnet 4.6, 2026-10-03

Implemented `src/C_generators.py` with:
- Symbolic oscillator algebra engine using `Fraction` coefficients and PBW normalization
- `_normalize_word`: bubble-sort with anti-/commutator residuals, nilpotency for fermions
- `_graded_bracket`: [X,Y} = X*Y - (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} Y*X
- `build_generators(n)`: all generators for C(n+1) as oscillator words
- `compute_structure_constants(n)`: all non-zero [X,Y} in basis decomposition
- `build_schema1(n)`: full Schema 1 JSON

Bug fixed during development: initial fermionic nilpotency check zeroed words prematurely before normalization (e.g., `a_1_p * a_1_m * a_1_p` reduces to `a_1_p`, not zero). Fix: check for adjacent identical fermionic labels in PBW form only.

Results:

| 1 | 4 | 4 | 8 | 42 |
| 2 | 11 | 8 | 19 | 204 |
| 3 | 22 | 12 | 34 | 534 |

Unit tests: 34/34 pass (`tests/test_C_generators.py`). All parity checks pass.

Data files written: `data/C_1_structure.json`, `data/C_2_structure.json`, `data/C_3_structure.json`.

Note: decomposition algorithm was further improved during I04 — old greedy approach replaced with proper Gaussian elimination to correctly handle multi-word generators.
