### Response

**Decisions:** Implemented exact rational polynomial multiplication for the
fermionic CAR and bosonic CCR, then computed graded commutators and expanded
them in the selected generator basis. The reference has conflicting
normalizations for \(E_{\pm2\delta_n}\): the all-root formula is unscaled,
while the displayed simple generators use a factor of \(1/2\). I considered
using the unscaled convention uniformly or honoring the explicit simple-root
definition. I used the latter for the terminal long-root pair, which makes
its opposite-root bracket equal to the documented terminal Cartan; other
long-root pairs use the all-roots formula. The schema specification now
states this choice. Structure constants store one nonzero unordered pair in
basis order; graded antisymmetry determines the reverse bracket.

**Results and verification:** Added `src/C_generators.py`,
`tests/test_C_generators.py`, and generated `data/C_1_structure.json`,
`data/C_2_structure.json`, and `data/C_3_structure.json`. `python3
-m unittest tests/test_C_generators.py` passed (4 tests, covering ranks 1–3).
For ranks 1, 2, and 3, the generated files contain 8, 19, and 34 generators
and 20, 99, and 264 nonzero structure-constant entries, respectively. An
exhaustive super-Jacobi check over every ordered generator triple passed for
all three ranks. The generator validates closure by exact expansion in the
basis and uses the v5.0 fields and conventions in
`docs/json_schema_specification.md`.
