### Response

**Status**: done.

**Method.** Structure constants are computed exactly, not by hand. The engine `src/c_algebra.py` is an associative normal-ordering algebra over $\mathbb Q$ with the fermion pair $a^\pm$ and bosons $b_k^\pm$ (rules in its docstring). Each basis element is a fixed oscillator word. The graded bracket $[X,Y]=XY-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}YX$ is expanded, and the result is solved in the basis by exact Gaussian elimination. If a bracket left the span of the basis, the engine would raise an error. None did. Output: `src/C_generators.py` writes `data/C_{1,2,3}_structure.json`.

**Results.**
- Dimensions: $n=1$: 8 (even 4, odd 4); $n=2$: 19 (11, 8); $n=3$: 34 (22, 12). These match $2n^2+n+1$ and $4n$.
- Non-zero ordered brackets ($\kappa$-free): 40 for $n=1$, 198 for $n=2$, 528 for $n=3$.
- Closure: every bracket lies in the span of the basis. The $\kappa$-free brackets have no $K$-component.

**Errors found and fixed during this issue.**
1. A $K$-component (identity) in $\kappa$-brackets, which the first design did not allow. Fixed by splitting $\kappa$-parts into a $\mathfrak g$-valued part and a $K$-part (`Span.decompose`).
2. A sign error in the coordinate extraction. It negated every structure constant. Jacobi and antisymmetry checks would not have caught it, because negating all brackets gives an isomorphic Lie superalgebra. It was caught by a hand computation: $[a^+b^+,a^-b^-]=-H_1-2H_2$. The fix is in `Span.express` and `Span.decompose`. The regenerated files were checked after the fix.

**Tests** (`tests/test_c_algebra.py`, 16 pass): dimensions and parity for $n=1,2,3$; closure for $n=1,2,3$; normal-ordering relations (including the constant $b^-b^+=b^+b^-+1$); the deformed relation $ba=ab-\mathrm{gb}\,\kappa$; $\kappa^2=0$; graded antisymmetry; Jacobi for $n=1,2$; the Cartan weight check for $n=1,2,3$ (an oracle independent of the normal-ordering code, which counts letters); and the generator writing a valid Schema 1 file.

**Choices.** `E_{\pm2\delta_k}` carry $\tfrac12$ (decision A2). Odd elements are fermion first (A3). Coefficients are stored as strings such as `"-1/2"`.
