### Response

**Implementation** (`src/C_generators.py`)
- Exact (Fraction) normal-ordering engine for the Weyl–Clifford algebra with letters a_1_p<a_1_m<b_1_p<b_1_m<…, relations CAR/CCR, optional κ-deformation `[b_j^s, a^σ] = -gb κ` (used in I05).
- Basis names/realizations per `handover/notation.md`. Brackets are computed as `XY - (-1)^{xy} YX` directly in the oscillator algebra for **every ordered pair**, then expanded in the basis by an exact rational left-inverse of the realization matrix. The expansion is checked by reconstruction; a bracket outside the span raises an error. This makes closure (with K=1) a computed fact, not an assumption.
- Option considered: hand-coded root-system formulas for the structure constants (Frappat). Rejected as error-prone; oscillator computation follows the issue ("using the defined oscillator relations").

**Results** (`python3 src/C_generators.py`)
```
data/C_1_structure.json {'total': 8, 'even': 4, 'odd': 4}   40 entries
data/C_2_structure.json {'total': 19, 'even': 11, 'odd': 8} 198 entries
data/C_3_structure.json {'total': 34, 'even': 22, 'odd': 12} 528 entries
```
No bracket left the span of the basis, i.e. the algebra closes with K=1 (confirms assumption A2 of I01).

**Tests** (`tests/test_C_generators.py`): counts, parities, fermion-number parity of realizations, schema keys / algebra / central_elements, parity-consistency and antisymmetry of stored brackets, regeneration equals stored file, hand-computed brackets ([E_2del1_p,E_2del1_m]=4H_2, {E_eps1_del1_pm,E_eps1_del1_mp}=H_1).
`python3 -m pytest -q` → `20 passed`.
