### Response

**Approach — the key design decision.** Rather than hand-tabulating structure constants, I built an
**oscillator (Weyl–Clifford) algebra engine** (`src/osc_algebra.py`) that represents every generator
as a normal-ordered word in the oscillators $a_1^\pm, b_k^\pm$ and computes brackets as the
**graded commutator** $[X,Y\}=XY-(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}YX$ inside the associative superalgebra. Because
graded commutators in any associative superalgebra automatically satisfy super-antisymmetry and
super-Jacobi, *every* structure constant produced this way is correct by construction (this is what
I04 then independently confirms numerically).

**Engine (`src/osc_algebra.py`).** Letters `(kind, index, sign)`; normal-ordering key puts creations
left of annihilations (fermion before bosons per sign group). Relations implemented in `_swap`:
bosonic CCR `b^- b^+ = b^+ b^- + δ`, fermionic CAR `a^- a^+ = -a^+ a^- + 1` with nilpotency
`(a^±)^2=0`, and undeformed `[b,a]=0` (a deformation hook `gb` is present for Schema 2). Provides
`poly_mul` and `graded_commutator`.

**C generators (`src/C_generators.py`).** Builds the PBW basis (I01), the realization of every
generator, a parity map, and — crucially — `decompose`, which re-expresses any bracket result on the
basis: single-monomial terms match the unique realization word of a root generator; number-operator
terms $a_1^+a_1^-, b_k^+b_k^-$ and the constant are solved onto the Cartan basis $H_1..H_{n+1}$ via a
fixed $(n{+}1)\times(n{+}1)$ linear system, with any leftover constant recorded as the central `K`.
If a term matched no basis generator it would raise a *non-closure* error — none occurred, confirming
the algebra closes on the declared basis.

**Outputs.** `data/C_1_structure.json` (even=4, odd=4, 20 bracket records),
`data/C_2_structure.json` (11, 8, 99 records), `data/C_3_structure.json` (22, 12, 264 records). Each
file is a complete Schema-1 document per `docs/json_schema_specification.md` (algebra, oscillator
generators/relations, central_elements, basis, parity, realizations, structure_constants, metadata).

**Hand-verification (n=1), all confirmed against the engine:**
- $\{E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}, E_{-\varepsilon+\delta_1}\} = (b_1^+)^2 = E_{2\delta_1}$ (coeff 1).
- $[E_{2\delta_1}, E_{-2\delta_1}] = -4N_1-2 = 4H_2$ (pure Cartan, no central term).
- $[H_1, E_{2\delta_1}] = 2E_{2\delta_1}$.
- $\{E_{\varepsilon+\delta_1}, E_{-\varepsilon-\delta_1}\} = N_b - N_a + 1 = -H_1 - 2H_2$.

**Tests (`tests/test_C_structure.py`, 22 passed).** Cover dimensions, generator counts, parity
consistency, closure of every structure constant on the basis, **graded antisymmetry** for all pairs
(n=1,2,3), mutual commutativity of the Cartan, the CAR relation itself, and the four hand-verified
n=1 values.
