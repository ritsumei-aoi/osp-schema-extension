### Response

**Method**
- `src/C_generators.py`: exact normal-ordering engine for the oscillators $a_1^\pm$ (CAR) and $b_k^\pm$ (CCR), rational arithmetic via `fractions.Fraction`. Brackets are the graded commutator, expressed in the basis by an exact linear solve (sympy). K is included as an extra column, so any constant term would show up as a K coefficient.

**Decisions** (where the spec left the choice open)
- Bilinears are Weyl-symmetrized so the constant parts are fixed: $H_\varepsilon = a_1^+a_1^- - \tfrac12$, $H_k = b_k^+b_k^- + \tfrac12$, $E_{2\delta_k}^{\pm} = \tfrac12 (b_k^\pm)^2$, mixed terms are plain products.
- This differs from the schema table in I02 in two places: (i) $H_\varepsilon$ carries the $-\tfrac12$ symmetrization constant, not the bare $a_1^+a_1^-$; (ii) $E_{\pm2\delta_k}$ carry $\tfrac12$. Both are the standard Weyl-symmetric choice. I did not use the difference form $H_k = b_k^+b_k^- - b_{k+1}^+b_{k+1}^-$ from the B(0,n) table, because it does not give the basis of I01 (one $H_k$ per index, each the number operator of mode $k$).
- Ordering of the even block follows `handover/notation.md` exactly: $E_{2\delta}^+$, mixed $pp/pm/mp$, $E_{2\delta}^-$, mixed $mm$.
- Structure constants are stored once per unordered pair with $X$ before $Y$ in PBW order; antisymmetry is implicit (`sign_rule: "graded"`).

**Results**
- Dimensions: n=1: 8 (4+4), n=2: 19 (11+8), n=3: 34 (22+12), all matching the formulas.
- Closure: the brackets close on the basis with **no K term** for n=1, 2, 3. Nonzero pairs $(X,Y)$: 19, 84, 219; bracket entries $(X,Y,Z)$ in the JSON: 21, 90, 231 (counted from the files).
- Files written: `data/C_1_structure.json`, `data/C_2_structure.json`, `data/C_3_structure.json`. Each has the 10 top-level keys of `docs/json_schema_specification.md`.

**Tests** (`tests/test_C_generators.py`): `python3 -m pytest -q` gives **16 passed**. Covered: generator counts for n=1,2,3; parity of labels; no central term; parity grading of brackets; known n=1 values ($[E_{2\delta}^+,E_{2\delta}^-] = -H_1$, $[H_1,E_{2\delta}^+] = 2E_{2\delta}^+$, $[\texttt{E\_eps1\_del1\_pm}, \texttt{E\_eps1\_del1\_mm}] = 2\,\texttt{E\_2del1\_m}$); JSON output equal to a fresh run of the generator.

**Note for I04**: Jacobi and antisymmetry are *not* checked here, only closure and parity. These are the I04 job.
