### Response

#### Implementation
- `src/oscillator_algebra.py`: exact (Fraction) normal-ordering engine for $\mathcal{U}(A)$ with generators `kappa, a_1_p, a_1_m, b_k_p, b_k_m`. Rewrite rules: $x\kappa = (-1)^{p(x)}\kappa x$, $\kappa^2 = (a_1^\pm)^2 = 0$, $a_1^-a_1^+ = -a_1^+a_1^- + 1$, $b_k^-b_k^+ = b_k^+b_k^- + 1$, distinct bosons commute, $b a = a b$ (undeformed) or $b_j^s a_1^\sigma = a_1^\sigma b_j^s - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\kappa$ (deformed, symbolic gb; used from I05). Super-bracket $[A,B\} = AB - (-1)^{p(A)p(B)}BA$.
- `src/C_generators.py`: basis in PBW order (I01), realizations, and exact decomposition of any quadratic oscillator polynomial into $\mathfrak{g} \oplus \mathbb{C}K$ by inverting the (square, invertible) matrix of basis realizations over the normal-ordered monomials. Structure constants are computed for **all ordered pairs**; the generator raises if a bracket leaves $\mathfrak{g}\oplus\mathbb{C}K$ or has a $K$ component.
- `tests/test_C_generators.py`: counts, parity (label vs realization), basis lists vs `notation.md`, oscillator relations (CAR/CCR/deformed rule/κ nilpotency), top-level key conformance with the spec, structure-constant format & grading, hand-computed brackets, and regeneration-equals-stored-data for n=1,2,3.
