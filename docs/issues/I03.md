### Response

#### Decisions and Results

**Implementation**: `src/C_generators.py` implements the full oscillator algebra computation:
- `OscillatorAlgebra` class: normal ordering via bubble sort with swap relations (CAR for fermions, CCR for bosons, commuting mixed pairs).
- `CnAlgebra` class: builds all generators from oscillator realizations, computes graded brackets, and decomposes results into the generator basis. Cartan sector decomposition handled via forward substitution on the H_1,...,H_{n+1} system.

**JSON output** (in `data/`):
