#### Design decisions
* **Computation method**: structure constants are *computed*, not typed in. $[X,Y\}$ is
  evaluated as $XY - (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}YX$ in the oscillator algebra by normal ordering and then
  decomposed in the basis. Alternative considered: hard-coding the root-string formulas
  $[E_\alpha,E_\beta] = N_{\alpha\beta}E_{\alpha+\beta}$. Rejected: the realization-based
  computation is independent of sign conventions and is exactly what Schemas 2–4 need (the
  same engine with κ switched on).
* **Both orders stored**: `(X,Y)` and `(Y,X)` are computed independently (record counts
  40 / 198 / 528 for n = 1,2,3; non-zero ordered pairs 38 / 184 / 486), so I04's antisymmetry
  check is a genuine test of the generator.
* **Closure assertion**: the generator raises if any bracket produces a constant (`K`) term.
  None occurred — confirming that the Frappat Cartan generators with their ±1/2 constants
  are the symmetrised quadratics and $\mathfrak g$ closes without $K$.
* **Degree of generality**: the engine handles arbitrary $n$; only n = 1, 2, 3 are emitted as
  required.
