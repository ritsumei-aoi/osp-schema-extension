### Response

#### Implementation
* `src/oscillator_algebra.py` — exact engine: sparse polynomials over $\mathbb{Q}$ (`Poly`, used
  later for the gb-symbolic layers) and a normal-ordering Weyl–Clifford superalgebra
  `OscillatorAlgebra(n, deformed)` with generators `kappa < a_1_p < a_1_m < b_1_p < b_1_m < …`.
  Rewriting rules: CAR, CCR, $(a_1^\pm)^2=\kappa^2=0$, κ odd super-central, and (deformed mode only)
  $[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = -\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,j,s}\kappa$. Products are normal-ordered recursively with
  memoization; the super-commutator is $XY - (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)}YX$.
* `src/C_generators.py` — basis labels in PBW order (`odd_labels`, `even_labels`), oscillator
  realization of each generator (`realization`), class `CBasis` which decomposes any normal-ordered
  quadratic element into basis coordinates plus a `K` (constant) coordinate by exactly inverting the
  $(n+2)\times(n+2)$ Cartan block, `structure_constants()` over **all ordered pairs**, and
  `build_schema1` / `write_schema1` producing `data/C_{n}_structure.json`.

Design choice: the structure constants are *computed* from the oscillator realization, not
transcribed from tables. The generator asserts (i) no κ terms, (ii) no constant (`K`) terms survive
in any bracket, (iii) $p(Z)=p(X)+p(Y)$, (iv) basis counts equal $2n^2+n+1 \mid 4n$.

Alternative considered: hard-coding the root-vector brackets from the Frappat tables. Rejected
because the Cartan normalisation of C(n+1) (simple-root $H_k$ including the $-\tfrac12$ shift) is
exactly where transcription errors occur; computing from the realization makes I04 an independent check.

#### Correction made during this issue
My hand-written inverse Cartan formulas in `notation.md` §2 were wrong (sign of $H_{n+1}$). The
exact linear algebra in `CBasis` gave $N_a = H_1-\sum_{k=2}^{n}H_k+H_{n+1}$,
$N_k=\sum_{l=k+1}^{n}H_l-H_{n+1}$; verified by direct substitution and fixed in `notation.md`
and in the test.
