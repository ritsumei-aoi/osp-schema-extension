Output: `data/C_1_structure.json` (20 structure-constant entries), `data/C_2_structure.json` (99), `data/C_3_structure.json` (264).

**Method**

- Oscillator words are normal-ordered exactly with rationals (`fractions.Fraction`): fermions before bosons, `+` before `-`, then by index. Rules: $(a^\pm)^2=0$, $\{a^-,a^+\}=1$, $[b_k^-,b_l^+]=\delta_{kl}$.
- The super-commutator is $[A,B]=AB-(-1)^{p(A)p(B)}BA$. The coordinates of a bracket in the basis are obtained from an exact left inverse of the basis matrix. A bracket that is not in the span is reported as a residual, not dropped.
- The κ-deformation is already present in the engine (the anomaly $[b_k^s,a_1^\sigma]=-\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma,k,s}\kappa$ with κ moved to the front, $\kappa^2=0$). This is not used in I03. The κ-free part is kept, and the κ-linear part is reused in I05.

**Decisions**

1. *Ordering and labels* follow I01 (odd block, then even block, with the ordering fixed there).
2. *Normalization* $E_{\pm2\delta_k}=(b_k^\pm)^2$, as decided in I01.
3. *Closure check*: the Cartan elements $H_{n+1}=-b_n^+b_n^- - \tfrac12$ and $H_1=a_1^+a_1^-+b_1^+b_1^-$ were tested with no adjustment. The residual outside the basis span is zero for all brackets when $n=1,2,3$ (no $K$ component appears). So no constant had to be added.
4. *Frappat strings* are written from the normal-ordered words, with constants shown as numbers (for example `-1/2 - b_1^+ b_1^-`).

**Verification (run output)**

- `python3 -m pytest -q` gives `15 passed in 1.66s`. The tests check: dimension counts (even $2n^2+n+1$, odd $4n$, total $2n^2+5n+1$) for $n=1,2,3$; unique labels and the parity rule; zero closure residual for $n=1,2,3$; super-skew symmetry for $n=1,2$; the hand-computed brackets $[E_{2\delta},E_{-2\delta}]=4H_2$ and $\{a^+b^+,a^-b^-\}=-H_1-2H_2$; Cartan elements acting diagonally.
- Dimensions reported by the engine: $n=1$: 8 (4 even, 4 odd); $n=2$: 19 (11, 8); $n=3$: 34 (22, 12).

**Note**: The B(0,n) spec (`B0n_schema_v5.md`) lists the structure constants as a list of graded brackets. The generated list covers pairs $i\le j$ only, and the super-skew rule is stated per entry.
