**Implementation**:
- `src/C_generators.py`: `OscAlgebra` gives the normal-ordering engine for the oscillators $a_1^\pm, b_k^\pm$. Coefficients are dual numbers $c_0 + c_\kappa\kappa$ so $\kappa^2=0$ is enforced. Contractions follow the rules in `handover/notation.md`.
- `src/C_structure.py`: builds the Weyl-symmetric basis, the supercommutators, and their expansion in the basis. The expansion is exact (sympy rationals, left-inverse solve with a residual check). It writes `data/C_{n}_structure.json` with the fields in `docs/json_schema_specification.md`.
- `tests/test_C_structure.py`: 12 tests.

**Decisions**:
- The basis elements are Weyl-symmetric, so the algebra closes without constant terms (checked for $n=1,2,3$).
- Options considered for the realization: Frappat-style generators with explicit constants, or the Weyl-symmetric quadratics (chosen). Reason: the constants are ordering-dependent, while the symmetric form closes exactly.
- The JSON field `generator_realization` lists the normal-ordered form, so the constants (for example $-\tfrac12$ on $W(a_1^+,a_1^-)$) are explicit.
