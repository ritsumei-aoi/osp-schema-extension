### Response

**Decisions & Analysis**:
1. **Implementation approach**: A symbolic oscillator algebra engine was implemented in `src/C_generators.py` to evaluate the graded commutators and deduce the structure constants of $C(n+1) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)$.
2. **Generators**: The engine calculates commutators using the CAR and CCR rules applied directly to quadratic polynomials. Gaussian elimination was used to express resulting commutators back into the Cartan-Weyl basis defined in Issue I01.
3. **Tests**: A unit test suite `tests/test_C_generators.py` verifies parity correctness, counting logic ($2n^2+n+1$ even generators, $4n$ odd generators), and consistency against the defined schema for $n=1, 2, 3$.
