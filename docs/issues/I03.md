Design decisions:
* Structure constants are computed **from the oscillator realization** (not typed in), by
  normal-ordering $XY \mp YX$ and decomposing into the basis with an exact rational matrix inverse.
  The decomposition target is $\mathfrak g \oplus \mathbb C K$; for Schema 1 the generator *asserts* that the
  $K$-component is zero (it is, for all pairs and all $n$), which proves closure of the chosen basis.
* All ordered pairs $(X,Y)$ with nonzero bracket are stored (so I04 can check antisymmetry from the file).
* Alternative considered: hard-coding root-string formulas $N_{\alpha,\beta}$ from Frappat's tables.
  Rejected: the oscillator computation is self-verifying (closure is checked) and gives the
  normalization-dependent constants (e.g. $4H_{n+1}$) without manual sign bookkeeping.
