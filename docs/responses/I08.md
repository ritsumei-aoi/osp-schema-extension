## Triviality Search Report (C(n+1) = osp(2|2n), n = 1, 2, 3)

Code: `src/triviality_search.py` (+ `src/gauge_f.py`, `src/coboundary.py`); tests: `tests/test_triviality.py`, whole suite `python -m pytest -q` → `52 passed`. Command: `python src/triviality_search.py`.

### 0. Conventions and assumptions (decisions taken without human input)
- Triviality means: ∃ odd linear f: g → g (g = span of the Schema 1 basis, no K) with γ = δf, δ as in `C_coboundary_definition.md`. "Up to scalar" is automatic because γ and δf are both linear (the scalar can be absorbed in f).
- γ is linear in gb (κ² = 0) and the image of δ is a linear space. Hence the set T of trivial gb is a **linear subspace** of C^{4n}; it is determined exactly by the 4n unit vectors (rank computation over ℚ, no sampling).
- Odd generators are the Weyl-symmetrised `(ab+ba)/2` (I05 decision). The alternative convention is analysed in §4 because it changes the answer.

### 2. Analysis of discrepancies
In the main convention there are no discrepancies (residual = 0 everywhere), so there is no generator pair (X,Y) with inconsistent γ_{XY}^Z. Mathematical reason (gauge argument, `src/gauge_f.py`): put `b'_j^s = b_j^s + κ u_{j,s}`, `u_{j,s} = gb_{+,j,s} a_1^- + gb_{−,j,s} a_1^+`. Then `[b'_j^s, a^τ] = −gb_{τ,j,s}κ + κ{u_{j,s},a^τ} = 0` (because {a^+,a^-}=1, {a^±,a^±}=0 — exactly the CAR of the *standard* pair), so the generators written in b', a satisfy the undeformed relations and the deformed algebra is the undeformed one in different coordinates. To first order in κ the coordinate change is X ↦ X + κ·D(X) with D the odd derivation D(b)=u, D(a)=0, and `δ(−D) = γ` (checked exactly for all unit gb, n=1,2,3). The needed ingredient is that **every** odd pair (a^σ, b_j^s) is "dual": {a^+,a^-}=1 lets u_{j,s} cancel both gb_{+,j,s} and gb_{−,j,s} independently. (No B(0,n) computation was done here, so no comparison with that case is claimed.)

Further exact structural facts (computed):
| n | #odd maps | rank δ | dim ker δ | dim T (all f) | T for f odd→even only | f even→odd only | f vanishing on Cartan | f vanishing on root vectors E_2del, E_del_del |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 32 | 28 | 4 | 4 of 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 4 of 4 |
| 2 | 176 | 168 | 8 | 8 of 8 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 8 of 8 |
| 3 | 528 | 516 | 12 | 12 of 12 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 12 of 12 |
- dim ker δ = 4n = number of odd generators, consistent with f being unique up to the inner odd derivations ad(E), E odd (these satisfy δ = 0 by the Jacobi identity; the identification of the whole kernel with them was not separately checked).
- A trivializing f must act on the Cartan generators **and** on the odd generators (restricting to either one kills all nonzero gb), but acting on Cartan + odd generators alone already suffices (f may vanish on the even root vectors E_2del, E_del_del).

### 3. Conjecture / theorem for the conditions on gb
> **Claim.** For C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) the inhomogeneous deformation `[b_j^s, a_1^σ] = −gb_{σ,j,s}κ` is trivial for **every** gb ∈ C^{4n} (no condition on gb); explicit f = −D_gb, f unique modulo ad(g_odd).
Evidence: exact for n=1,2,3 (T = whole 4n-dim space, via linearity — covers all sign profiles and all complex gb); structural proof sketch above, which is independent of n (it uses only {a_1^+,a_1^-}=1 and the shape of the bilinear form), so the statement is conjectured (and argued) for all n ≥ 1; a fully written induction/proof for general n is not done.
The expected analogue of a "condition on gb" is empty here; the only way to get non-trivial behaviour is to change the setting, as follows.

### 4. Where non-triviality does appear (sensitivity to conventions)
(a) *Normal-ordered odd generators* (`a b` instead of `(ab+ba)/2`), γ g-part is unchanged, but each bracket acquires a central κK term. Exactly (verified for n=1,2 and every gb parameter, `test_central_residual_formula`):
`κK-coefficient of [X,Y} = −½ Σ_{Z odd} c_{XY}^Z gb_Z`, where c are the Layer 1 structure constants and gb_Z = gb_{σ,k,τ} for Z = E_eps1_del{k}_{στ}.
It is nonzero exactly for the pairs (X,Y) with an odd component in [X,Y]_0 (mixed even/odd pairs, e.g. 6 pairs for gb_{a⁺,b⁺}, n=1: (E_pp,H_1)=1, (E_pp,H_2)=−½, (E_pm,E_2δ+)=−1 and their transposes). Since 1 ∉ span(g) (the Cartan span contains N+½ but not 1), no g-valued f can produce it: with f required to take values in g, such γ is **non-trivial for every gb ≠ 0** (trivial iff gb = 0). It is the coboundary of the odd map f_K: E_Z ↦ (gb_Z/2)K (odd → even central K), i.e. trivial again if K-valued f is allowed — an artefact of the odd central shift `ab = S(ab) + (gb/2)κ`, not a genuine obstruction.
(b) Restricted classes of f (table in §2): non-trivial (T=0) unless f acts on both Cartan and odd generators.
