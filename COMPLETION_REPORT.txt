================================================================================
FINAL THEOREM
================================================================================

THEOREM STATEMENT:
An inhomogeneous deformation γ_gb of C(n+1) = osp(2|2n) defined by
  [b_j^s, a_1^σ]_γ = -gb_{σ,j,s} · κ
is trivial (i.e., γ_gb = δf for some odd linear map f) if and only if
  gb_{σ,j,s} = 0 for all σ ∈ {+,-}, j ∈ {1,...,n}, s ∈ {+,-}

INTERPRETATION:
No non-trivial inhomogeneous deformations exist for C(n+1).

KEY INSIGHT:
The structural obstruction arises from the incompatibility between:
  1. The deformation cocycle γ: g × g → C·κ (central extension)
  2. The coboundary δf: g × g → g (within the algebra)

These spaces are orthogonal in the extended structure, making their equality
impossible unless both vanish.
