+### 1. Mathematical Analysis & `gb_matrix` Design
+
+In accordance with `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` (Section 1), the inhomogeneous deformation of $C(n+1) = \mathfrak{osp}(2|2n)$ is parametrized by the exchange relations between the standard fermionic pair $(a_1^+, a_1^-)$ and the bosonic oscillators $(b_j^+, b_j^-)$ for $j = 1, \ldots, n$.
+
+#### 1.1 Matrix Structure & Dimension
+The deformation parameters form a $2 \times 2n$ matrix:
+- **Rows (Fermionic sector, $m=1$)**: Index $\sigma \in \{+, -\}$ corresponding to ordered oscillators $[a_1^+, a_1^-]$.
+- **Columns (Bosonic sector, $n$ modes)**: Index $(j, s)$ with $j \in \{1, \ldots, n\}$ and $s \in \{+, -\}$ corresponding to ordered oscillators $[b_1^+, b_1^-, b_2^+, b_2^-, \ldots, b_n^+, b_n^-]$.
+- **Total parameter count**: $2 \times 2n = 4n$ parameters.
+  - $n=1$ ($C(2)$): 4 parameters.
+  - $n=2$ ($C(3)$): 8 parameters.
+  - $n=3$ ($C(4)$): 12 parameters.
+
+#### 1.2 Canonical Parameter Labels
+We adopt standard alphanumeric parameter labels:
+$$\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \longrightarrow \texttt{gb\_a1}\{\sigma\}\texttt{\_b}\{j\}\{s\}$$
+Specifically:
+- For row $a_1^+$: `gb_a1p_b{j}p`, `gb_a1p_b{j}m` ($j = 1, \ldots, n$).
+- For row $a_1^-$: `gb_a1m_b{j}p`, `gb_a1m_b{j}m` ($j = 1, \ldots, n$).
+
+#### 1.3 Parity Specification
+As established in `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` (Section 2, Parity Note):
+- The central carrier $\kappa$ is odd ($p(\kappa) = 1$).
+- Total bracket parity is preserved: each $\mathrm{gb}$ parameter carries parity 1 ($p(\mathrm{gb}) = 1$).

+### 2. Gram Matrix Sign Convention Options
+
+In the deformed universal enveloping algebra / oscillator superspace, we consider the exchange relations between bosons $b_j^s$ ($p=0$) and fermions $a_1^\sigma$ ($p=1$).
+
+#### Option A (Recommended — Literal Match to Definition)
+From `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` line 22:
+$$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \iff [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
+- **Pros**: Matches `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md` exactly as written.
+- **Normal Ordering Rule**: Moving $b_j^s$ past $a_1^\sigma$ to standard order ($a_1^\sigma < b_j^s$) gives:
+  $$b_j^s a_1^\sigma = a_1^\sigma b_j^s - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
+
+#### Option B (Fermion-First Negative Convention)
+$$[a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \iff [b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
+- **Normal Ordering Rule**:
+  $$b_j^s a_1^\sigma = a_1^\sigma b_j^s + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
+
+**Recommendation**: We recommend **Option A** to maintain literal mathematical alignment with `docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`.

+### 3. Proposed $\gamma$ Computation Methodology
+
+#### 3.1 Faithful Adjoint Representation Inversion on Superspace $V$
+Rather than ad-hoc associative normal ordering that can suffer from central scalar gauge ambiguities (e.g. shifts by $K=1$), we employ the faithful representation of $C(n+1)$ on the fundamental oscillator superspace:
+$$V = V_{\bar{1}} \oplus V_{\bar{0}} = \mathrm{span}\{a_1^+, a_1^-\} \oplus \mathrm{span}\{b_1^+, b_1^-, \ldots, b_n^+, b_n^-\}, \quad \dim V = 2 + 2n.$$
+
+1. **Adjoint Action**: Generators $X \in \mathfrak{g}$ act on $v \in V$ via the graded commutator $\mathrm{ad}(X)(v) = [X, v\}$.
+2. **Deformed Expansion**: Under Option A, the action expands to first order in $\kappa$:
+   $$\mathrm{ad}(X)(v) = \mathrm{ad}_0(X)(v) + \kappa \cdot \mathrm{ad}_1(X)(v),$$
+   where $\mathrm{ad}_0(X)$ is the standard undeformed action and $\mathrm{ad}_1(X)$ is linear in $\mathrm{gb}$.
+3. **Cocycle Bracket Action**: The deformed bracket $[X, Y\}_\gamma = [X, Y\}_0 + \kappa \cdot \gamma(X, Y)$ acting on $v \in V$ gives:
+   $$\mathrm{ad}_0(\gamma(X, Y))(v) = \mathrm{ad}_0(X)(\mathrm{ad}_1(Y)(v)) + \mathrm{ad}_1(X)(\mathrm{ad}_0(Y)(v)) - (-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} \left[ \mathrm{ad}_0(Y)(\mathrm{ad}_1(X)(v)) + \mathrm{ad}_1(Y)(\mathrm{ad}_0(X)(v)) \right].$$
+4. **Exact Linear Inversion**: Because the adjoint map $\mathrm{ad}_0: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{gl}(V)$ has full rank equal to $\dim \mathfrak{g} = 2n^2 + 5n + 1$, the operator $\mathrm{ad}_0(\gamma(X, Y))$ uniquely decomposes into basis elements $Z \in \mathfrak{g}$:
+   $$\gamma(X, Y) = \sum_{Z \in \mathfrak{g}} \gamma_{XY}^Z Z.$$
+5. **Guaranteed Properties**:
+   - **Graded Skew-Symmetry**: $\gamma(Y, X) = -(-1)^{p(X)p(Y)} \gamma(X, Y)$ holds algebraically by construction.
+   - **Zero Ambiguity**: Scalar central constants $K$ act trivially on $V$ ($[K, v] = 0$), so no scalar ambiguities occur.
