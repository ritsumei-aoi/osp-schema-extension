### 1. Notation Conventions for Generators & Oscillators

#### Oscillators
- **Fermionic standard pair** ($p=1$):
  - $a_1^+$ (`a_1_p`), $a_1^-$ (`a_1_m`) with CAR: $\{a_1^-, a_1^+\} = 1$, $(a_1^\pm)^2 = 0$.
- **Bosonic oscillators** ($p=0$):
  - $b_k^+$ (`b_k_p`), $b_k^-$ (`b_k_m`) for $k = 1, \dots, n$ with CCR: $[b_j^-, b_k^+] = \delta_{jk}$.
- **Central elements**:
  - $K$: Central scalar ($K = 1$).
  - $\kappa$: Odd central nilpotent parameter ($p(\kappa) = 1$, $\kappa^2 = 0$).

### 3. Concrete PBW Ordering Options for Selection

In all options, the global graded ordering follows the base convention:
$$\kappa < [\text{odd basis}] < [\text{even basis}]$$

We propose two concrete ordering schemes for the internal arrangement of the odd basis:

#### Option A: Bosonic Mode Block Ordering (Recommended)
- **Structure**: Group primarily by the bosonic oscillator index $k \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, and secondarily by the signs of $\varepsilon$ and $\delta_k$ (`pp`, `pm`, `mp`, `mm`).
- **Order for $n=2$**:
  `E_eps1_del1_pp`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`, `E_eps1_del1_mp`, `E_eps1_del1_mm`,
  `E_eps1_del2_pp`, `E_eps1_del2_pm`, `E_eps1_del2_mp`, `E_eps1_del2_mm`
- **Rationale**: Directly aligns with the $4 \times 1$ block structure of the deformation matrix $\mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s}$ for each bosonic mode $j$. Simplifies indexing, tensor blocks, and coboundary matrix representations.

#### Option B: Global Root Polarity Split (Direct $B(0,n)$ Analogue)
- **Structure**: Group all positive $\varepsilon$ roots first, then all negative $\varepsilon$ roots, matching the positive-then-negative convention of $B(0,n)$ (where all `E_del{k}_p` precede all `E_del{k}_m`).
- **Order for $n=2$**:
  - Positive $\varepsilon$:
    `E_eps1_del1_pp`, `E_eps1_del2_pp`, `E_eps1_del1_pm`, `E_eps1_del2_pm`
  - Negative $\varepsilon$:
    `E_eps1_del1_mp`, `E_eps1_del2_mp`, `E_eps1_del1_mm`, `E_eps1_del2_mm`
- **Rationale**: Strictly preserves the root polarity partitioning $\Delta_{\bar{1}}^+ < \Delta_{\bar{1}}^-$.
