### 2. Gram Matrix Sign Convention Options for Human Selection

Under the skew-supersymmetric pre-oscillator form on $V = V_{\bar{0}} \oplus V_{\bar{1}}$:
$$[u, v\} = \begin{cases} (u|v) K & p(u) = p(v) \\ (u|v) \kappa & p(u) \ne p(v) \end{cases}$$
Because $p(b_j^s) = 0$ and $p(a_1^\sigma) = 1$, graded skew-symmetry requires:
$$[a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = - [b_j^s, a_1^\sigma]$$

We propose two concrete sign conventions for human selection:

#### Option 1: Frappat / Reference Document Standard (Recommended)
Following Eq. (22) of [`docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md`](file://<home>/notebook/git/thm01-R/thm01-R-gemini38f-r2-F/docs/math/C_inhomogeneous_definition.md):
$$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \iff [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
- **Pre-oscillator form**: $(b_j^s \mid a_1^\sigma) = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s}$, $(a_1^\sigma \mid b_j^s) = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s}$.
- **Rationale**: Exactly reproduces the relation stated in the mathematical reference document.

#### Option 2: Direct Positive Commutator Convention
$$[b_j^s, a_1^\sigma] = + \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa \iff [a_1^\sigma, b_j^s] = - \mathrm{gb}_{\sigma, j, s} \cdot \kappa$$
